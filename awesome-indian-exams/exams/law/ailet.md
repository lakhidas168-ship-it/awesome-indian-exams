# AILET (NLU Delhi)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from the AILET 2027 notification (dated 23.07.2026)
> and the AILET 2027 BA LLB (Hons.) exam-details page on nationallawuniversitydelhi.in, both fetched on

AILET is National Law University Delhi's own entrance for its Five-Year BA LLB (Hons.) and LLM programmes
(academic year 2027-28).

## Exam pattern

BA LLB (Hons.): one paper, 150 multiple-choice questions for 150 marks, 120 minutes.

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| English Language | 50 | 50 |
| Current Affairs and General Knowledge | 30 | 30 |
| Logical Reasoning | 70 | 70 |

**+1** correct, **−0.25** wrong (four wrong answers deduct 1 mark). Ties are broken first by higher marks in
Logical Reasoning, then by seniority in age, then by computerised draw of lots.

## Syllabus

Per the official exam-details page: English language (50), current affairs and general knowledge (30), and
logical reasoning (70). Legal principles may appear inside the logical-reasoning section to test logical
aptitude, but the exam needs no legal knowledge or technical understanding.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Logical reasoning is almost half the paper (70/150) and decides ties. Practise it daily.
2. Read English comprehension every day; it is the next-biggest section (50/150).
3. Keep a daily current-affairs habit for the 30-mark GK section.
4. Solve the official previous-year BA LLB papers linked from the exam-details page under timed conditions.
