# AILET (NLU Delhi)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

AILET is National Law University Delhi's own entrance for its BA LLB (Hons.), LLM and PhD programmes.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Law University Delhi |
| BA LLB paper | 150 questions, 120 minutes |

## Official sources

- NLU Delhi (official): <https://nationallawuniversitydelhi.in>

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| English Language | 50 |
| Current Affairs and General Knowledge | 30 |
| Logical Reasoning | 70 |

**+1** correct, **−0.25** wrong.

## Syllabus

English comprehension and usage, current affairs, and logical (including legal-style) reasoning.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Logical reasoning is almost half the paper. Practise it daily.
