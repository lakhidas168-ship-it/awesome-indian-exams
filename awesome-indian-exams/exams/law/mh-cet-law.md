# MAH CET Law

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

MAH CET Law is Maharashtra's entrance for three-year and five-year LLB programmes in the state.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | State CET Cell, Maharashtra |
| Mode | Computer-based; separate tests for 3-year and 5-year LLB |

## Official sources

- State CET Cell, Maharashtra (official): <https://cetcell.mahacet.org>

## Exam pattern

Objective questions on legal aptitude and reasoning, general knowledge and current affairs, logical and
analytical reasoning, English and (5-year LLB) basic mathematics. The information brochure has the number of
questions, time and marking.

## Syllabus

As listed in the brochure for each programme.
