# RRB Technician (Grade 1 Signal and Grade 3)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

RRB Technician recruits technicians for railway workshops, signalling and maintenance. Grade 1 Signal needs a
science degree or engineering diploma. Grade 3 needs Class 10 with ITI, or an apprenticeship in the listed trades.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) |
| Stages | CBT → document verification → medical examination |
| Posts | Technician Grade 1 Signal; Technician Grade 3 (trade-wise) |

## Official sources

- Centralised RRB application portal: <https://www.rrbapply.gov.in>
- Example regional RRB website (RRB Chandigarh): <https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**CBT:** 100 questions, 90 minutes, **−1/3** per wrong answer, for both grades. Sections: mathematics,
general intelligence and reasoning, general science, and general awareness. Grade 1 Signal's paper is set at a
higher level (basics of computers and applications, basic science and engineering) than Grade 3's. The CEN has the
section-wise question counts for each grade.

## Syllabus

Shared railway modules (maths, reasoning, general science, GA). Grade 1 adds physics, electronics and
computer basics at diploma/B.Sc level.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with RRB ALP: the science and engineering basics overlap heavily.
2. Grade 1 Signal aspirants should revise diploma-level electronics and computer fundamentals.
