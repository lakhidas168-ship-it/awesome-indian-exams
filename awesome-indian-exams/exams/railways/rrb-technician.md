# RRB Technician (Grade 1 Signal and Grade 3)

RRB Technician recruits technicians for railway signalling, workshops and maintenance. Technician Grade 1
Signal (Pay Level 5) needs a B.Sc in physics, electronics, computer science, IT or instrumentation, or an
engineering diploma or degree in those streams. Technician Grade 3 posts (Pay Level 2, trade-wise) need
Class 10 with ITI in the listed trade, or a completed Act apprenticeship in that trade. The current
notification is CEN 02/2026; its application window closed on 29.07.2026 and CBT dates will be announced
on the RRB websites.

## Exam pattern

**CBT (one per pay level):** 100 questions, 90 minutes, **−1/3 mark** per wrong answer, for both grades.
Marks are normalised across shifts. Qualifying marks: UR and EWS 40%, OBC-NCL 30%, SC 30%, ST 25%
(PwBD relaxation of 2 marks where reserved PwBD vacancies fall short).

Grade 1 Signal paper (100 marks): general awareness 10, general intelligence and reasoning 15, basics of
computers and applications 20, mathematics 20, basic science and engineering 35.

Grade 3 paper (100 marks): mathematics 25, general intelligence and reasoning 25, general science 40,
general awareness 10. The CEN calls this break-up tentative and indicative.

**After CBT:** DV shortlist equals the number of vacancies, on CBT merit (ties broken by age, then name).
Appointment needs the railway medical fitness test for the opted post's standard (mostly B-1; some posts
A-3, B-2 or C-1 — see Annexure A of the CEN).

## Syllabus

Shared railway modules (maths, reasoning, general science, GA). Grade 1 adds diploma/B.Sc-level basics of
computers (architecture, devices, networking, operating systems, MS Office, internet and email) and basic
science and engineering (units and mechanics, electricity and magnetism, electronics, microprocessors,
measurements and transducers). Grade 3 general science is Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences at Class 10
level. The CEN topic lists are illustrative, not exhaustive.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with RRB ALP: the science and engineering basics overlap heavily.
2. Grade 1 Signal aspirants should revise diploma-level electronics and computer fundamentals.
