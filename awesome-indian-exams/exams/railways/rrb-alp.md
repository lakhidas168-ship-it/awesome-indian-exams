# RRB Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

RRB ALP recruits Assistant Loco Pilots, who drive trains alongside the Loco Pilot. Candidates need ITI, a diploma
or an engineering degree in specified trades. Selection has two CBTs and a computer-based aptitude test.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) |
| Stages | CBT 1 (screening) → CBT 2 (Part A merit, Part B trade qualifying) → CBAT → document verification and medical examination |
| Qualification | ITI / diploma / engineering degree in listed trades (see the CEN) |

## Official sources

- Centralised RRB application portal: <https://www.rrbapply.gov.in>
- Example regional RRB website (RRB Chandigarh): <https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

| Stage | Questions | Time | Content |
|---|---:|---|---|
| CBT 1 | 75 | 60 min | Mathematics, reasoning, general science, general awareness (screening) |
| CBT 2 Part A | 100 | 90 min | Mathematics, reasoning, basic science and engineering, general awareness (merit) |
| CBT 2 Part B | 75 | 60 min | The candidate's trade (qualifying) |
| CBAT | — | — | Psychological aptitude test battery, taken by about eight times the number of vacancies |

Negative marking applies to the CBTs, and CBAT marks count towards the final merit. The CEN has the exact rules.

## Syllabus

Class 10 maths and science, reasoning and general awareness; "basic science and engineering" (engineering
drawing, units, measurements, mass-weight-density, work-power-energy, heat and temperature, basic electricity, levers,
occupational safety, environment, IT literacy); plus the syllabus of the candidate's own ITI trade for Part B.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Clear CBT 1 with the shared railway modules (maths, reasoning, science, GA).
2. For Part A, revise basic science and engineering from ITI/diploma first-year material.
3. Part B is qualifying but eliminates candidates: revise your trade theory.
4. Practise CBAT-style speed and memory tests. They are unlike any other paper.
