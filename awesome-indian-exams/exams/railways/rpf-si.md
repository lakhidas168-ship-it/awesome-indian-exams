# RPF Sub-Inspector

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

RPF SI (Executive) recruits Sub-Inspectors for the Railway Protection Force. The CBT has the same three
subjects as RPF Constable, set at graduate level.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Railway Recruitment Boards for the Railway Protection Force |
| Stages | CBT → PET and PMT → document verification |
| Qualification | Graduate degree |

## Official sources

- Centralised RRB application portal: <https://www.rrbapply.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**CBT:** 120 questions, 120 marks, 90 minutes, negative marking per the CEN. Sections: General Awareness,
Arithmetic, and General Intelligence and Reasoning, at graduate level. Then the PET and PMT.

## Syllabus

Graduate-level arithmetic, reasoning and general awareness (current affairs, history, geography, polity,
economy, science).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with SSC CGL/CPO Tier 1: the subjects and level are close.
2. Keep physical training running alongside the written preparation.
