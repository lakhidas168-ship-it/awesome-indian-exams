# RPF Sub-Inspector

RPF SI (Executive) recruits Sub-Inspectors in the Railway Protection Force and Railway Protection Special Force.
Selection is through a graduate-level CBT followed by PET/PMT, document verification and a medical examination.
Every number below is from CEN RPF 01/2024, the latest Sub-Inspector notification on the RRB sites.

## Exam pattern

**CBT (graduation level):** 120 questions in 90 minutes. One mark per correct answer, minus one-third mark
per wrong answer; unattempted questions score nothing. Marks are normalized across shifts. Minimum pass:
35% for UR, EWS and OBC-NCL, 30% for SC and ST. Section weightage: General Awareness 50 marks,
Arithmetic 35 marks, General Intelligence and Reasoning 35 marks. Questions are set in English, Hindi and
13 regional languages. Only one application per candidate; multiple applications mean rejection and debarment.

**PET (qualifying only, no marks):** men run 1600 m in 6 min 30 s, long jump 12 ft, high jump 3 ft 9 in;
women run 800 m in 4 min, long jump 9 ft, high jump 3 ft. One chance for the run, up to two chances each
for the jumps. Ex-servicemen skip PET but must take the PMT.

**PMT:** UR/EWS/OBC — men 165 cm, women 157 cm; men's chest 80 cm unexpanded and 85 cm expanded. SC/ST —
men 160 cm, women 152 cm; chest 76.2/81.2 cm. Garhwalis, Gorkhas, Marathas, Dogras, Kumaonese and other
notified categories — men 163 cm, women 155 cm; chest 80/85 cm.

CBT merit shortlists candidates for PET/PMT and document verification up to 10 times the vacancies in each
male/female/ExSM category group, with at most two PET/PMT rounds to cover shortfalls.

## Syllabus

- Arithmetic (35 marks): number system, whole numbers, decimals and fractions and their relationships,
  fundamental operations, percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount,
  tables and graphs, mensuration, time and distance.
- General Intelligence and Reasoning (35 marks): analogies, similarities and differences, spatial
  visualization and orientation, problem solving analysis, judgement, decision making, visual memory,
  observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning, verbal and figure classification, number
  series, non-verbal series, coding and decoding, statement-conclusion, syllogistic reasoning.
- General Awareness (50 marks): the environment and its application to society, current events, everyday
  observation and experience, Indian history, art and culture, geography, economics, polity, the
  Constitution, sports and general science.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Cover the three CBT sections with the shared modules below; the level is graduation, close to SSC CGL Tier 1.
2. Practise 120-question, 90-minute mocks with a one-third negative marking rule.
3. Keep running and jumping practice going alongside written preparation, since PET is qualifying but mandatory.
