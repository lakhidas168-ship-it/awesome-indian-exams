# RPF Constable

RPF Constable (Executive) recruits constables for the Railway Protection Force and Railway Protection Special
Force. Selection is through a Class 10 level CBT followed by PET/PMT and document verification. Every number
below is from CEN RPF 02/2024, the latest Constable notification on the RRB sites.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Railway Recruitment Boards for the Railway Protection Force |
| Post and pay | Constable (Executive), Level-3, initial pay ₹21,700 |
| Vacancies in this CEN | 4,208 (3,577 male, 631 female, including 420 ExSM) |
| Stages | CBT → PET and PMT → document verification |
| Qualification | Class 10 pass or equivalent from a recognised Board |
| Age (as on 01.07.2024) | 18–28 years (includes a one-time 3-year Covid relaxation over the normal 18–25) |
| Age relaxation | SC/ST +5 years, OBC-NCL +3 years, plus ExSM and other categories per the CEN table |
| Medical standard | B-1 (glasses, flat foot, knock knee, squint, colour blindness and similar conditions are disqualifying) |
| Fee | ₹500 (₹400 refunded on appearing in CBT); ₹250 for SC, ST, ExSM, female, minority and EBC candidates (refunded on appearing) |
| Application window | 15.04.2024 to 14.05.2024 |

## Exam pattern

**CBT (Class 10/Matric level):** 120 questions in 90 minutes. One mark per correct answer, minus one-third mark
per wrong answer; unattempted questions score nothing. Marks are normalized across shifts. Minimum pass: 35%
for UR, EWS and OBC-NCL, 30% for SC and ST. Section weightage: General Awareness 50 marks, Arithmetic 35
marks, General Intelligence and Reasoning 35 marks.

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| General Awareness | 50 |
| Arithmetic | 35 |
| General Intelligence and Reasoning | 35 |

**PET (qualifying only, no marks):** men run 1600 m in 5 min 45 s, long jump 14 ft, high jump 4 ft; women run
800 m in 3 min 40 s, long jump 9 ft, high jump 3 ft. One chance for the run, up to two chances each for the
jumps. Ex-servicemen skip PET but must take the PMT.

**PMT:** UR/EWS/OBC — men 165 cm, women 157 cm; men's chest 80 cm unexpanded and 85 cm expanded. SC/ST —
men 160 cm, women 152 cm; chest 76.2/81.2 cm. Garhwalis, Gorkhas, Marathas, Dogras, Kumaonese and other
notified categories — men 163 cm, women 155 cm; chest 80/85 cm.

CBT merit shortlists candidates for PET/PMT and document verification up to 10 times the vacancies in each
male/female/ExSM category group, with at most two PET/PMT rounds to cover shortfalls.

## Syllabus

- Arithmetic (35 marks): number system, whole numbers, decimals and fractions and their relationships,
  fundamental operations, percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount,
  tables and graphs, mensuration, time and distance.
- General Intelligence and Reasoning (35 marks): analogies, similarities and differences, spatial
  visualization and orientation, problem solving analysis, judgement, decision making, visual memory,
  observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning, verbal and figure classification, number
  series, non-verbal series, coding and decoding, statement-conclusion, syllogistic reasoning.
- General Awareness (50 marks): the environment and its application to society, current events, everyday
  observation and experience, Indian history, art and culture, geography, economics, general polity, the
  Indian Constitution, sports and general science.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. General awareness is 50 of 120 questions. Make it a daily habit (NCERT Class 6–10 + PIB).
2. Train running, long jump and high jump from the start; the PET is qualifying but mandatory.
3. Practise full papers at 90 minutes. The −1/3 penalty makes blind guessing costly.
