# RPF Constable

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

RPF Constable (Executive) recruits constables for the Railway Protection Force. The CBT is set at Class 10
level. The RRBs conduct the recruitment for the RPF.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Railway Recruitment Boards for the Railway Protection Force |
| Stages | CBT → PET and PMT → document verification |
| Qualification | Class 10 pass |

## Official sources

- Centralised RRB application portal: <https://www.rrbapply.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**CBT:** 120 questions, 120 marks, 90 minutes, **−1/3** per wrong answer.

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| General Awareness | 50 |
| Arithmetic | 35 |
| General Intelligence and Reasoning | 35 |

The CBT is followed by the Physical Efficiency Test (running, long jump, high jump) and the Physical Measurement Test.

## Syllabus

Class 10 level arithmetic and reasoning; general awareness (current affairs, history, geography, polity,
economy, science, sports).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. General awareness is 50 of 120 questions. Make it a daily habit (NCERT Class 6–10 + PIB).
2. Train running, long jump and high jump from the start.
