# RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

RRB NTPC recruits for Indian Railways' non-technical posts at graduate and undergraduate (Class 12) level, such
as Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Commercial cum Ticket Clerk and Junior Clerk cum Typist. It draws applicants in
the crores.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) |
| Stages | CBT 1 (screening) → CBT 2 → typing test or CBAT, depending on the post → document verification and medical examination |
| Levels | Graduate posts and Class 12 posts, with separate notifications |

## Official sources

- Centralised RRB application portal: <https://www.rrbapply.gov.in>
- Example regional RRB website (RRB Chandigarh): <https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

Both CBTs use **−1/3** per wrong answer.

| Stage | Questions | Time | General Awareness | Mathematics | General Intelligence and Reasoning |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CBT 1 (screening) | 100 | 90 min | 40 | 30 | 30 |
| CBT 2 (merit) | 120 | 90 min | 50 | 35 | 35 |

Posts such as Station Master and Traffic Assistant add a computer-based aptitude test (CBAT). Clerk and typist posts
add a qualifying typing skill test. The CEN lists which stages apply to which post.

## Syllabus

- **General Awareness:** current affairs, Indian history, geography, polity, economy, general science, Indian
  Railways, sports, culture, computer basics.
- **Mathematics:** number system, percentages, ratio, averages, interest, profit and loss, time and work, time-speed-distance,
  mensuration, elementary algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics.
- **Reasoning:** analogies, series, coding-decoding, syllogism, puzzles, Venn diagrams, data sufficiency, statement–conclusion.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. General awareness carries the most questions. Build it daily from NCERT and PIB.
2. Arithmetic and reasoning are shared with SSC and Group D: prepare them once.
3. Practise typing early if your target post needs it.
