# RRB Group D (Level 1)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

RRB Group D (Level 1) recruits track maintainers, helpers, assistants and porters across Indian Railways. It is
Class 10/ITI level and is usually the single largest recruitment exam in the country.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) |
| Stages | CBT → Physical Efficiency Test → document verification → medical examination |
| Qualification | Class 10 or ITI (see the CEN) |
| Minimum CBT marks | 40% (UR/EWS), 30% (OBC/SC/ST), per recent notices |

## Official sources

- Centralised RRB application portal: <https://www.rrbapply.gov.in>
- Example regional RRB website (RRB Chandigarh): <https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**CBT:** 100 questions, 100 marks, 90 minutes, **−1/3** per wrong answer.

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| General Intelligence and Reasoning | 30 |
| Mathematics | 25 |
| General Science | 25 |
| General Awareness and Current Affairs | 20 |

The CBT is followed by a qualifying Physical Efficiency Test. The CEN has the lifting and running standards.

## Syllabus

Class 10 level: NCERT physics, chemistry and life science; arithmetic and elementary maths; verbal and
non-verbal reasoning; current affairs and static GK.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. General Science is a quarter of the paper and comes almost entirely from NCERT Class 6–10. Read those books first.
2. Start physical training at the same time as the written preparation.
3. Practise full papers at 90 minutes. The −1/3 penalty makes blind guessing costly.
