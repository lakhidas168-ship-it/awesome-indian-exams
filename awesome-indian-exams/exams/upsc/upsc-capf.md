## Exam pattern

The written stage (450 marks) is held in one day:

| Paper | Content | Type | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I | General Ability and Intelligence | Objective, 125 questions, −1/3 per wrong answer | 250 | 2 h |
| II | General Studies, Essay and Comprehension | Descriptive (Part A: Essay; Part B: Comprehension, précis, counter-arguments, language) | 200 | 3 h |

Paper II details:
- **Part A (80 marks):** Essay questions which candidates may answer in either Hindi or English.
- **Part B (120 marks):** Comprehension, précis writing, arguments for/against, report writing, and English language skills — to be attempted in English only.

Only candidates who clear the Paper I qualifying minimum have Paper II evaluated. Candidates who
qualify in the written examination are called for the Physical Efficiency Tests (PET) and Medical
Standards Tests (both qualifying in nature):

| Event | Males | Females |
|---|---|---|
| 100 Metres race | 16 seconds | 18 seconds |
| 800 Metres race | 3 minutes 45 seconds | 4 minutes 45 seconds |
| Long Jump | 3.5 Metres (3 chances) | 3.0 Metres (3 chances) |
| Shot Put (7.26 kg) | 4.5 Metres | Not applicable |

*Note:* Pregnancy at the time of PET is a disqualification under UPSC rules.

Candidates qualifying in the medical standards tests are called for the Interview / Personality Test
(150 marks). The final merit list is determined by written marks (450) + interview marks (150), for a
total of 600 marks.

## Syllabus

The official syllabus is detailed in Appendix I of the examination notice.

**Paper I: General Ability and Intelligence**
*   **General Mental Ability:** Logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, data interpretation.
*   **General Science:** General awareness, scientific temper, scope and significance of science in everyday life, including new areas like information technology, biotechnology, and environmental science.
*   **Current Events of National and International Importance:** Culture, music, arts, literature, sports, governance, societal and developmental issues, industry, business, and globalization.
*   **Indian Polity and Economy:** Constitution of India, social systems, public administration, economic development in India, regional and international security issues, and human rights.
*   **History of India:** Ancient, medieval, and modern history, with a focus on the freedom movement.
*   **Indian and World Geography:** Physical, social, and economic geography of India and the world.

**Paper II: General Studies, Essay and Comprehension**
*   **Part A (Essay):** Topics from modern Indian history (freedom struggle), geography, polity, economy, knowledge of security and human rights, and analytical ability.
*   **Part B (Comprehension, Précis, and Language Skills):** Comprehension passages, précis writing, developing counter-arguments, simple grammar, and other aspects of language testing.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Foundations via NCERTs:** General science, history (modern/freedom movement), geography, and Indian polity form the bulk of Paper I questions. Build core conceptual clarity from NCERT Class 9–12 textbooks.
2. **Master Paper I PYQs:** Solve the last 5–10 years of official UPSC CAPF AC Paper I question papers under a strict 2-hour timer. With the −1/3 penalty, practice eliminating options and calibrating attempt strategy.
3. **Weekly descriptive writing for Paper II:** Practice writing one essay and one précis/argument every week. Unlike CSE, CAPF Paper II specifically requires arguments for-and-against and formal reports. Budget approximately 70–80 minutes for Part A (essay) and 100–110 minutes for Part B.
4. **Current affairs and security issues:** Read daily releases from PIB and national security developments, paying specific attention to border management, internal security, disaster response, and paramilitary forces.
5. **Early physical training:** Begin running (100 m sprint and 800 m stamina) and long jump practice early alongside written preparation. The PET is qualifying, but eliminates unprepared candidates with no second attempts (except long jump).
