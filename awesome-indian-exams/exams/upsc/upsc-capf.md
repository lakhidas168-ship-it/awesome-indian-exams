# UPSC CAPF (Assistant Commandant)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

CAPF (AC) selects Assistant Commandants (Group A officers) for BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP and SSB.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC |
| Stages | Written (2 papers) → physical and medical standards tests → interview |
| Qualification | Graduate degree |

## Official sources

- UPSC website (notifications under Examinations → Active Examinations): <https://upsc.gov.in>
- Official previous question papers: <https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers>

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Content | Type | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I | General Ability and Intelligence | Objective, −1/3 per wrong answer | 250 | 2 h |
| II | General Studies, Essay and Comprehension | Descriptive, no negative marking | 200 | 3 h |

Candidates who clear the written stage then take the physical tests and an interview.

## Syllabus

Paper I: general mental ability, general science, current events, polity, economy, history and geography.
Paper II: essays, comprehension, précis, arguments for and against, and English language skills.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Paper II is descriptive: practise essays and précis weekly.
2. Paper I overlaps heavily with UPSC CSE prelims GS.
