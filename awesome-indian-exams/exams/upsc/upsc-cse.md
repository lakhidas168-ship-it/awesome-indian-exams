# UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) for engineers

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Pattern, marks and the Electrical Engineering optional
> topic list below were checked against the official *Rules for Civil Services Examination
> to be held by UPSC in 2026* (Gazette of India Extraordinary, via DoPT) on 2026-09-28.

Many engineers take CSE. This page covers the structure and the **Electrical Engineering optional**, which
overlaps heavily with what a GATE/ESE EE aspirant already studies.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) |
| Stages | Preliminary (objective, screening) → Main (descriptive) → Personality test |
| Final merit | Mains written 1750 + Personality test 275 = **2025**. Prelims only decides who reaches Mains |
| Age and attempts | Set by the notification (general category: 21 to 32 years, 6 attempts, with category relaxations) |
| Qualification | Any graduate degree |

## Exam pattern

**Prelims** (negative marking: one-third of the question's marks per wrong answer)

| Paper | Marks | Time | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| General Studies I | 200 | 2 h | Decides who qualifies for Mains |
| General Studies II (CSAT) | 200 | 2 h | Qualifying, 33% needed |

**Mains** (descriptive)

| Paper | Marks | Counted in merit |
|---|---:|---|
| Paper A: Indian language | 300 | No (qualifying) |
| Paper B: English | 300 | No (qualifying) |
| Essay | 250 | Yes |
| General Studies I–IV | 4 × 250 | Yes |
| Optional subject Paper I and II | 2 × 250 | Yes |

Essay, General Studies and optional papers are evaluated only for candidates who score at
least 25% in each of Papers A and B.

**Electrical Engineering optional** (topic summary; the notification is the authority)

| Paper I | Paper II |
|---|---|
| Circuit theory | Control systems |
| Signals and systems | Microprocessors and microcomputers |
| Electromagnetic theory | Measurement and instrumentation |
| Analog electronics | Power systems: analysis and control |
| Digital electronics | Power system protection |
| Energy conversion (machines) | Digital communication |
| Power electronics and electric drives | |
| Analog communication | |

Compared with GATE EE, the new areas are **analog and digital communication** and **microprocessors**.
Everything else is the GATE/ESE core.
