# UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) for engineers

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Scheme, marks and the Electrical Engineering optional topic list
> below were checked on 2026-09-29 against the UPSC *Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination, 2026*
> notification and the *Civil Services Examination Rules – 2026* (Gazette of India Extraordinary, via
> the DoPT CSE portal), both opened this run.

Many engineers take CSE. This page covers the structure and the **Electrical Engineering optional**, which
overlaps heavily with what a GATE/ESE EE aspirant already studies.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) |
| Stages | Preliminary (objective, screening) → Main (descriptive) → Personality test |
| Final merit | Mains written 1750 + Personality test 275 = **2025**. Prelims only decides who reaches Mains |
| Age and attempts | Set by the notification (general category: 21 to 32 years on 1 August 2026, 6 attempts, with category relaxations) |
| Qualification | Any graduate degree |

## Exam pattern

**Prelims** (negative marking: one-third of the question's marks per wrong answer; blank answers carry no penalty)

| Paper | Marks | Time | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| General Studies I | 200 | 2 h | Decides who qualifies for Mains |
| General Studies II (CSAT) | 200 | 2 h | Qualifying, 33% needed |

**Mains** (descriptive; each paper is of three hours)

| Paper | Marks | Counted in merit |
|---|---:|---|
| Paper A: Indian language | 300 | No (qualifying) |
| Paper B: English | 300 | No (qualifying) |
| Essay | 250 | Yes |
| General Studies I–IV | 4 × 250 | Yes |
| Optional subject Paper I and II | 2 × 250 | Yes |

Essay, General Studies and optional papers are evaluated only for candidates who score at
least 25% in each of Papers A and B.

Compared with GATE EE, the new areas are **analog and digital communication** and **microprocessors**.
Everything else is the GATE/ESE core.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Prelims is only a screening step.** GS Paper I (200 marks) decides who reaches Mains while GS
   Paper II (CSAT) only has to be cleared at 33%. Build the base from NCERT Class 6–12 for history,
   geography, polity, economics and science, and add current affairs from PIB each day.
2. **Mains carries the merit.** Essay plus the four General Studies papers make up 1250 of the 1750
   written marks, so begin weekly answer writing early against the syllabus in the notification.
3. **Choose the optional subject early.** For an engineer the Electrical Engineering optional reuses the
   GATE/ESE core; only analog and digital communication and microprocessors are new.
4. **Practise on the official papers.** UPSC publishes every past paper free, including the Electrical
   Engineering optional papers; solve them under exam time.
5. **Clear the qualifying papers.** Papers A and B need 25% each and are not counted in the merit.
