The Combined Geo-Scientist Examination recruits geologists, geophysicists, chemists and hydrogeologists for the
Geological Survey of India and the Central Ground Water Board.

## Exam pattern

**Stage I (prelims):** two objective papers per stream, set in English only and answered on an OMR sheet.
Paper I is General Studies, common to all streams (2 hours, 100 marks, of science-graduate standard).
Paper II is the stream paper — Geology/Hydrogeology, Geophysics or Chemistry (2 hours, 300 marks).
Total 400 marks. One-third of the marks for a question is deducted for each wrong answer; blank answers
carry no penalty. Prelims marks count toward the final merit, and about six to seven times the number of
vacancies are admitted to the mains.

**Stage II (mains):** three descriptive (conventional) stream papers of 200 marks, 3 hours each (total 600).
Geologist, Geophysicist and Chemist streams take all three papers in their subject; the Hydrogeology stream
takes Geology Paper I and II plus Hydrogeology Paper III.

**Stage III:** Personality Test/Interview, 200 marks.

## Syllabus

General Studies (Paper I) plus stream subjects — geology, hydrogeology, geophysics, chemistry — at master's
level, as detailed in the appendix to the official notice.
