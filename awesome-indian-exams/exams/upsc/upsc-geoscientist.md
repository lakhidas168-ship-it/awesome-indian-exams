# UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

The Combined Geo-Scientist Examination recruits geologists, geophysicists, chemists and hydrogeologists for the
Geological Survey of India, the Central Ground Water Board and related departments.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC |

| Qualification | Master's degree in the relevant science (see notice) |

## Exam pattern

**Stage I:** two objective papers, 2 hours each: General Studies (100 marks) and the stream paper (Geology/
Hydrogeology, Geophysics or Chemistry; 300 marks), one-third negative marking.

**Stage II:** three descriptive stream papers of 200 marks, 3 hours each. **Stage III:** interview, 200 marks.

## Syllabus

Stream syllabi (geology, hydrogeology, geophysics, chemistry at master's level) as notified, plus general
studies.
