# UPSC Indian Forest Service (IFoS)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

The Indian Forest Service is selected through the UPSC CSE prelims (a shared screening test) followed by a
separate IFoS Mains and interview.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC |
| Stages | CSE Prelims (screening) → IFoS Mains (6 papers) → interview |
| Final merit | Mains 1400 + interview 300 = 1700 |
| Qualification | Degree in a science, agriculture, forestry or engineering subject (see notice) |

## Official sources

- UPSC website (notifications under Examinations → Active Examinations): <https://upsc.gov.in>
- Official previous question papers: <https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers>

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** the UPSC CSE Prelims (GS Paper I and CSAT). A separate IFoS cut-off applies.

**Mains** (descriptive):

| Paper | Marks |
|---|---:|
| I: General English | 300 |
| II: General Knowledge | 300 |
| III–VI: two optional subjects, two papers each | 4 × 200 |

Then a 300-mark interview.

## Syllabus

CSE Prelims syllabus for the screening stage; for Mains, English, general knowledge and two optional
subjects from the IFoS list (for example Botany, Zoology, Agriculture, Forestry, Geology, Engineering subjects).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with UPSC CSE for prelims; choose IFoS optionals from your degree subjects.
