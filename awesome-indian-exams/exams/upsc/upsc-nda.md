# UPSC NDA and NA

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

The NDA and NA examination selects Class 12 students (and those appearing) for the Army, Navy and Air Force
wings of the National Defence Academy and for the Naval Academy's 10+2 cadet entry. It is held twice a year.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC, followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) |
| Stages | Written (2 papers) → SSB interview → medical |
| Final merit | Written 900 + SSB 900 = 1800 |

## Official sources

- UPSC website (notifications under Examinations → Active Examinations): <https://upsc.gov.in>
- Official previous question papers: <https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers>

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| I | Mathematics | 120 | 300 | 2 h 30 min |
| II | General Ability Test (English + general knowledge) | 150 | 600 | 2 h 30 min |

Negative marking of one-third of a question's marks applies to wrong answers. The SSB interview carries 900 marks.

## Syllabus

Mathematics at Class 11–12 level (algebra, matrices, trigonometry, analytical geometry, calculus, vectors,
statistics and probability). GAT: English (grammar, vocabulary, comprehension) and general knowledge (physics,
chemistry, general science, history, freedom movement, geography, current events).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Maths is 300 marks at Class 12 level: NCERT plus timed practice.
2. GAT's general knowledge section rewards NCERT science and social science from Class 6–12.
3. Prepare for the SSB alongside the written exam: communication, fitness and officer-like qualities.
