# UPSC NDA and NA

> **Evidence status: ⚠️ unverified.** The UPSC website currently restricts automated access (HTTP 403), preventing the verification of the latest notification. The details below are based on previous cycles and require confirmation against the current official notification when accessible.

The NDA and NA examination selects Class 12 students (and those still appearing) for the Army, Navy and
Air Force wings of the National Defence Academy and for the Naval Academy's 10+2 cadet entry. It is held
twice a year, as NDA & NA (I) and (II).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC, followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) |
| Stages | Written (2 papers) → SSB interview and medical |
| Who can apply | Unmarried male and female candidates; 12th class pass of the 10+2 pattern, or appearing in it. Air Force and Navy wings and the 10+2 Naval Academy entry also require Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in Class 12 |
| Marital status | Must be unmarried, and must not marry until full training is complete |
| Final merit | Written 900 + SSB 900 = 1800 |

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| I | Mathematics | 300 | 2 h 30 min |
| II | General Ability Test — Part A English (200) + Part B general knowledge (400) | 600 | 2 h 30 min |

Both papers are objective type only; Mathematics and Part B of the GAT are set bilingually in Hindi and
English. One-third (0.33) of a question's marks is deducted for each wrong answer; a question with more than one answer marked is treated as wrong and draws the same penalty; a blank answer carries no penalty. The SSB interview and personality test carries 900 marks.

## Syllabus

Mathematics at Class 11–12 level: algebra; matrices and determinants; trigonometry; analytical geometry of two and three dimensions; differential calculus; integral calculus and differential equations; vector algebra; statistics and probability. GAT Part A — English, 200 marks: grammar and usage, vocabulary, comprehension and cohesion in extended text. GAT Part B — general knowledge, 400 marks, is split into six sections: physics, chemistry, general science, history and the freedom movement, geography, and current events.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Mathematics is 300 marks at Class 12 level: finish NCERT Class 11–12 once, then shift to timed papers.
2. In the 400-mark general knowledge paper, history and the freedom movement is a significant section. Take the periods in order — ancient India, medieval India, then modern India and the freedom movement — and pin every dynasty, art form and monument to its period on a timeline; NCERT history for Classes 6–12 are the standard foundation.
3. Use official previous question papers from the UPSC website to understand the question style and difficulty.
