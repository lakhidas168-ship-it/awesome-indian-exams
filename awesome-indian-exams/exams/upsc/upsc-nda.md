# UPSC NDA and NA

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The numbers below were read this run in the official NDA & NA
> Examination-II, 2026 notice (No. 10/2026-NDA-II). They match the page, but upsc.gov.in returns
> HTTP 403 to the hive's automated evidence fetcher, so the status cannot be upgraded to `official`

The NDA and NA examination selects Class 12 students (and those appearing) for the Army, Navy and Air Force
wings of the National Defence Academy and for the Naval Academy's 10+2 cadet entry. It is held twice a year.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC, followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) |
| Current cycle | NDA & NA (II) 2026 — written exam held 13 September 2026 |
| Stages | Written (2 papers) → SSB interview → medical |
| Vacancies (II/2026) | 394 (370 male + 24 female across NDA wings and the 10+2 Naval Academy entry) |
| Who can apply | Unmarried male/female candidates born 01-01-2008 to 01-01-2011; 12th pass (appearing candidates may apply). Air Force and Navy wings and the Naval Academy entry additionally require Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in Class 12 |
| Final merit | Written 900 + SSB 900 = 1800 |

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| I | Mathematics | 120 (as in recent papers; the notice does not state counts) | 300 | 2 h 30 min |
| II | General Ability Test — Part A English (200) + Part B general knowledge (400) | 150 (as in recent papers; the notice does not state counts) | 600 | 2 h 30 min |

Both papers are objective type only; Mathematics and Part B of the GAT are set bilingually in Hindi and
English. One-third (0.33) of a question's marks is deducted for each wrong answer; blank answers carry no
penalty. The SSB interview carries 900 marks.

## Syllabus

Mathematics at Class 11–12 level, per the notice's Appendix-I: algebra; matrices and determinants;
trigonometry; analytical geometry of two and three dimensions; differential calculus; integral calculus and
differential equations; vector algebra; statistics and probability. GAT Part A: English grammar and usage,
vocabulary, comprehension and cohesion. GAT Part B: physics (about 25%), chemistry (about 15%), general
science (about 10%), history and freedom movement (about 20%), geography (about 20%) and current events
(about 10%).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Maths is 300 marks at Class 12 level: NCERT plus timed practice.
2. GAT's general knowledge section rewards NCERT science and social science from Class 6–12.
3. Prepare for the SSB alongside the written exam: communication, fitness and officer-like qualities.
