# UPSC Indian Economic Service / Indian Statistical Service (IES/ISS)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

IES/ISS recruits economists and statisticians into the Indian Economic Service and Indian Statistical Service.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC |
| Stages | Written (6 papers, 1000 marks) → personality test (200) |
| Qualification | Postgraduate degree in economics (IES) or statistics (ISS), or as notified |

## Official sources

- UPSC website (notifications under Examinations → Active Examinations): <https://upsc.gov.in>
- Official previous question papers: <https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers>

## Exam pattern

Six papers: General English and General Studies (common), plus four papers in economics (IES) or
statistics (ISS). Descriptive papers take 3 hours; objective papers (some ISS papers) take 2 hours with one-third
negative marking. Then a 200-mark personality test.

## Syllabus

IES: microeconomics, macroeconomics, public finance, international economics, growth and development,
Indian economy. ISS: probability, statistical inference, linear models, sampling, econometrics, operations research,
and more, as notified.
