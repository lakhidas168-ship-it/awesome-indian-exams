The Indian Economic Service (IES) and Indian Statistical Service (ISS) Examination is conducted annually by UPSC to recruit specialist economists and statisticians as Group A Gazetted Officers into ministries and departments of the Government of India.

## Exam pattern

The written examination comprises 6 descriptive/objective papers totaling 1000 marks, followed by a 200-mark viva-voce personality test (total merit out of 1200 marks):

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration | Type |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| I | General English | 100 | 3 h | Descriptive |
| II | General Studies | 100 | 3 h | Descriptive |
| III | Economics-I / Statistics-I | 200 | 3 h | Descriptive / Objective |
| IV | Economics-II / Statistics-II | 200 | 3 h | Descriptive / Objective |
| V | Economics-III / Statistics-III | 200 | 3 h | Descriptive / Objective |
| VI | Economics-IV / Statistics-IV | 200 | 3 h | Descriptive / Objective |

Candidates must choose either Economics (for IES) or Statistics (for ISS) for Papers III to VI. Candidates who qualify the written examination are called for the Personality Test (200 marks).

## Syllabus

- **General English & General Studies:** Comprehension, essay writing, precise writing, current affairs, Indian history, geography, polity, and economy.
- **IES (Economics):** Microeconomics, macroeconomics, public finance, international economics, growth and development, and Indian economy.
- **ISS (Statistics):** Probability, statistical inference, linear models, sampling techniques, econometrics, operations research, and applied statistics.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Master core graduate-level textbooks for your chosen service (Advanced economic theory or rigorous statistical inference).
2. For the Indian Economy paper (IES) and General Studies, rely heavily on government publications: the Economic Survey, Union Budget documents (<https://www.indiabudget.gov.in>), and RBI bulletins.
3. For ISS, practice computational and analytical statistical problems using official previous question papers from UPSC.
4. Practise descriptive answer writing regularly for English and GS papers.
