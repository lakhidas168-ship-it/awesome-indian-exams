The Indian Economic Service (IES) and Indian Statistical Service (ISS) Examination is conducted annually by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) to recruit specialist economists and statisticians as Group A Gazetted Officers into ministries and departments of the Government of India.

## Exam pattern

The written examination comprises 6 papers totaling 1000 marks, followed by a 200-mark viva-voce personality test (total merit out of 1200 marks). All question papers must be answered in English only. Non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted in descriptive papers but not in objective papers. Negative marking (one-third of marks assigned) applies to objective papers.

## Syllabus

- **General English:** Essay writing, comprehension, grammar, and precise writing (graduate standard).
- **General Studies:** Current events, Indian polity and Constitution, history of India, geography, and general awareness.
- **General Economics (IES):** Advanced microeconomic and macroeconomic theory, economic growth and development, public finance, international economics, money and banking, growth models, and quantitative methods in economics (Master's degree standard).
- **Indian Economics (IES):** Planning, economic reforms, agriculture, industry, infrastructure, labour, foreign trade, public sector, and contemporary Indian economic problems.
- **Statistics (ISS):** Probability, statistical inference, linear models, design of experiments, multivariate analysis, sample surveys, industrial statistics, econometrics, and applied statistics (Bachelor's and Master's degree standard).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Master Core Discipline:** For IES, master advanced economic theory (micro, macro, growth, public finance) using standard post-graduate textbooks; for ISS, build rigorous command over probability, statistical inference, and sampling methods.
2. **Government Publications & Reports:** Rely heavily on government publications for Indian Economics and General Studies: the Economic Survey, Union Budget documents, and RBI bulletins.
3. **Practice Descriptive Writing:** Since most papers are subjective/essay type, regular answer-writing practice under timed conditions is essential.
4. **Solve Previous Papers:** Practice official previous years' question papers from UPSC to understand question depth and time management across objective and descriptive sections.
