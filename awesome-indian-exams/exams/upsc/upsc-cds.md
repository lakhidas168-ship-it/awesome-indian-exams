# UPSC Combined Defence Services (CDS)

> **Evidence status: ⚠️ secondary.** The official UPSC website is currently returning 403 Forbidden errors, preventing
> verification of the current notification. The information below is based on the previous verification. Always
> check the official UPSC portal before applying.

CDS selects graduates for the Indian Military Academy (IMA), the Indian Naval Academy (INA), the Air Force
Academy (AFA) and the Officers' Training Academy (OTA). It is held twice a year, as CDS (I) and CDS (II).

OTA: unmarried men and women aged 19–25, born between 01-07-2002 and 01-07-2008 |
| Qualification | IMA and OTA: a degree of a recognised university or equivalent. INA: a degree in engineering, or B.Sc. with Physics as a core or elective subject (with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2). | Final merit | Written marks equal the SSB marks for every course: IMA 300 + 300, INA 300 + 300, AFA 300 + 300, OTA 200 + 200 |

## Exam pattern

All papers are objective type, 2 hours each. The General Knowledge and Elementary Mathematics papers are set
bilingually in Hindi and English; calculators are not permitted.

| Entry | Papers | Written marks | SSB marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| IMA, INA, AFA | English (100), General Knowledge (100), Elementary Mathematics (100) | 300 | 300 |
| OTA | English (100), General Knowledge (100) | 200 | 200 |

One third (0.33) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer; a question left blank
carries no penalty. The standard of Elementary Mathematics is Matriculation (Class 10) level; the other papers
are of about graduate standard.

## Syllabus

- English (Code 11, 100 marks): understanding and workmanlike use of words.
- General Knowledge (Code 12, 100 marks): current events; matters of everyday observation and experience in
  their scientific aspects; history of India; and geography of a kind that needs no special study.
- Elementary Mathematics (Code 13, 100 marks, Matriculation level): arithmetic and number theory (number
  system, unitary method, percentage, simple and compound interest, profit and loss, ratio and proportion,
  logarithms); algebra (polynomials, quadratic equations, simultaneous linear equations and inequations, set
  language, laws of indices); trigonometry (ratios and their values at standard angles, simple identities,
  heights and distances); geometry (lines and angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, loci); mensuration
  (areas, surface areas and volumes of standard figures); and statistics (tabulation of data, graphical
  representation, measures of central tendency).
- SSB (Intelligence and Personality Test): Stage I has the Officer Intelligence Rating tests and the Picture
  Perception and Description Test; Stage II has the interview, Group Testing Officer tasks, psychology tests
  and the conference. The interviewing officer, the GTO and the psychologist assess the candidate holistically,
  with no separate weightage for each test; candidates are recommended or not recommended.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. OTA aspirants skip Elementary Mathematics — it applies only to IMA, INA and AFA — and put that time into
   English and General Knowledge.
2. Elementary Mathematics is only Matriculation (Class 10) level. Speed and accuracy win it: finish NCERT
   Class 8–10 maths, then shift to timed papers.
3. General Knowledge rewards NCERT science and social science from Class 6–10 plus a daily newspaper habit;
   English improves with daily reading.
4. The SSB is worth as much as the whole written exam (300 of 600 for IMA/INA/AFA, 200 of 400 for OTA). Work
   on communication, fitness and officer-like qualities alongside the written preparation, not after it.
