# UPSC Combined Medical Services (CMS)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

CMS recruits medical officers for the Railways, Ordnance Factories, CGHS, municipal bodies and other central
services. It is open to MBBS graduates.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | UPSC |
| Stages | Two computer-based papers → personality test (100 marks) |
| Qualification | MBBS |

## Official sources

- UPSC website (notifications under Examinations → Active Examinations): <https://upsc.gov.in>
- Official previous question papers: <https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers>

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Content | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---|
| I | General medicine and paediatrics | 250 | 2 h |
| II | Surgery, gynaecology and obstetrics, preventive and social medicine | 250 | 2 h |

Objective questions with a one-third penalty per wrong answer. The personality test carries 100 marks.

## Syllabus

The MBBS curriculum in the subjects named for each paper.
