UPSC recruits Enforcement Officers/Accounts Officers (EO/AO) and Assistant Provident Fund Commissioners (APFC)
for the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO, Ministry of Labour & Employment) through a recruitment
test and an interview.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) |
| Posts | Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer (EO/AO) and Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (APFC), EPFO, Ministry of Labour & Employment |
| Stages | Recruitment Test (RT) (objective, pen & paper) → Interview (personality test) |
| Qualification | A bachelor's degree in any subject from a recognised university or institute |
| Age limit | EO/AO: up to 30 years (UR/EWS), 33 (OBC) and 35 (SC/ST); APFC: up to 35 years (UR/EWS), 38 (OBC) and 40 (SC/ST), with PwBD relaxations as per the notice |
| Selection weightage | Recruitment Test and Interview in the ratio 75:25 (RT 300 marks + Interview 100 marks, Special Advt 52/2026) |
| Recent cycles | EO/AO and APFC advertised under Special Advt 52/2025; APFC (80 posts) advertised under Special Advt 52/2026 |

## Exam pattern

The selection has two stages.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Recruitment Test (RT), pen & paper, objective (multiple-choice) |
| Duration | 2 hours |
| Marking | All questions carry equal marks; wrong answer deducts one-third of the marks assigned to that question; a blank answer carries no penalty |
| Medium | English and Hindi |
| Stage 2 | Interview (personality test) |
| Weightage | RT 300 marks + Interview 100 marks, in the ratio 75:25 |

Special Advt 52/2025 runs this as a **Combined Recruitment Test (CRT)** to shortlist candidates for both the
EO/AO and the APFC posts; Special Advt 52/2026 uses the same scheme for APFC alone.

For the interview, the minimum level of suitability out of 100 marks is General/EWS 50, OBC 45 and SC/ST/PwBD 40.

Under Special Advt 52/2026 (APFC), online applications were open from 22.08.2026 to 11.09.2026 (1800 hrs) and
the Recruitment Test is scheduled for **20.12.2026**.

## Syllabus

The official syllabus is identical in both notices, except that 52/2025 says "Labour Laws" where 52/2026 says
"Labour Codes". It covers:

- General English — understanding of the English language and workmanlike use of words
- Indian Culture, Heritage and Freedom Movements
- Developmental Issues and Present Trends in Indian Economy
- Governance and Constitution of India
- General Science and Basic Knowledge of Computer Applications
- Elementary Mathematics, Statistics and General Mental Ability
- Industrial Relations, Labour Laws/Codes and Social Security in India
- Principles of Accountancy, Auditing and Insurance
- Current Events of National and International Importance

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Specialist subjects first.** Principles of Accountancy, Auditing and Insurance, and Industrial Relations,
   Labour Laws and Social Security in India are the least general and reward committed study; build them from
   the bare Acts and Ministry of Labour & Employment material.
2. **General studies next.** Governance and the Constitution, Indian economy, culture, heritage, the freedom
   movement and current events overlap with the UPSC CSE Prelims GS modules.
3. **Aptitude and English.** Practise Elementary Mathematics, Statistics, mental ability and English
   comprehension under timed conditions with negative marking, so accuracy holds up.
