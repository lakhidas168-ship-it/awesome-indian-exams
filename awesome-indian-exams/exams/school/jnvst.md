# JNV Selection Test (Navodaya, Class 6)

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

JNVST admits students (mostly from rural areas) to Class 6 in the residential Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas,
where education, boarding and lodging are free. Lateral entry to Classes 9 and 11 has its own test.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti |
| Class VI test | 80 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours, no negative marking |
| Who | Students in Class 5 in the same district (see the prospectus) |

## Official sources

- Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (official): <https://navodaya.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

80 objective questions, 100 marks, 2 hours, offline (OMR), no negative marking. Sections test mental ability,
arithmetic and language. Sources differ on whether a newer pattern splits the marks equally across four sections
including environmental studies, so check the current prospectus for the split.

## Syllabus

Class 5 level: non-verbal mental ability (figures, patterns), arithmetic, and reading comprehension in the
medium of the test.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Mental ability (figure puzzles) is the unusual part: practise it daily.
2. Class 5 NCERT maths covers arithmetic.
