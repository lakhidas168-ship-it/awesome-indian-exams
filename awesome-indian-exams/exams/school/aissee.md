# AISSEE (Sainik School entrance)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from the AISSEE 2026 Information Bulletin and the
> NTA public notice dated 10.10.2025, both fetched from official domains on 2026-09-28.

AISSEE admits students to Class 6 and Class 9 in Sainik Schools (English-medium CBSE residential schools
run by the Sainik Schools Society under the Ministry of Defence), which prepare cadets for the National
Defence Academy and other officer-training academies. The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts the
exam. For 2026-27 it also covers Class 6 in all 69 approved New Sainik Schools and Class 9 in the 19
approved New Sainik Schools that have progressed to Class 9.

## Exam pattern

**Class 6 entry:** 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes (2:00 PM–4:30 PM). Question booklet in 13
mediums: English, Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil,
Telugu, Urdu. English/Hindi opters get a bilingual (English + Hindi) booklet; regional-language opters
get their language plus English. In any translation ambiguity the English version is final (except the
Language section).

| Section | Topic | Questions | Marks each | Total marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | Language | 25 | 2 | 50 |
| B | Mathematics | 50 | 3 | 150 |
| C | Intelligence | 25 | 2 | 50 |
| D | General Knowledge | 25 | 2 | 50 |
| | Total | 125 | | 300 |

**Class 9 entry:** 150 questions, 400 marks, 180 minutes (2:00 PM–5:00 PM), English medium only.

| Section | Topic | Questions | Marks each | Total marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | Mathematics | 50 | 4 | 200 |
| B | Intelligence | 25 | 2 | 50 |
| C | English | 25 | 2 | 50 |
| D | General Science | 25 | 2 | 50 |
| E | Social Science | 25 | 2 | 50 |
| | Total | 150 | | 400 |

**No negative marking** for a wrong response in either paper.

**Qualifying criteria:** for admission to a Sainik School, General / OBC-NCL / Defence / ex-servicemen
candidates need at least 25% marks in each section and 40% in aggregate (no minimum applies to SC/ST
candidates). For approved New Sainik Schools, every candidate needs 25% in each section and 40% in
aggregate, irrespective of category. Final admission follows the AISSEE merit list, subject to vacancies,
medical fitness and document verification.

## Syllabus

The bulletin gives the full topic lists in Appendix II (Class 6 entry, Class 5 level) and Appendix III
(Class 9 entry, Class 8 level), with a question/marks break-up per topic. Broadly:

- **Class 6 entry:** Language, Mathematics, Intelligence, General Knowledge at Class 5 level.
- **Class 9 entry:** Mathematics, Intelligence, English, General Science, Social Science at Class 8 level.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Mathematics carries half the marks in both papers (150/300 for Class 6, 200/400 for Class 9): practise
   it daily from NCERT Class 5 (Class 6 entry) or Class 8 (Class 9 entry) books.
2. Work through the remaining sections topic by topic from the bulletin appendices, using the same NCERT
   textbooks.
3. Solve the previous years' AISSEE question papers hosted on the NTA website under timed, OMR-style
   conditions (no negative marking, so attempt every question).
