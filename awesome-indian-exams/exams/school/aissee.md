# AISSEE (Sainik School entrance)

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

AISSEE admits students to Class 6 and Class 9 in Sainik Schools, which prepare cadets for the defence
academies.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency |
| Stages | Pen-and-paper test → medical → admission |

## Official sources

- NTA exams portal: <https://exams.nta.nic.in>

## Exam pattern

Separate papers for Class 6 and Class 9 entry, on mathematics, intelligence, language and general knowledge (Class 6)
or mathematics, intelligence, English, general science and social science (Class 9). The bulletin has the marks and
time.

## Syllabus

Class 5 (for Class 6 entry) or Class 8 (for Class 9 entry) NCERT syllabus.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Mathematics carries the most marks in both papers: practise it daily.
