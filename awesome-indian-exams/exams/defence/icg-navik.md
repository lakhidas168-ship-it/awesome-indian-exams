# Indian Coast Guard Navik (GD) / Yantrik

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

The Indian Coast Guard recruits Navik (General Duty), Navik (Domestic Branch) and Yantrik through the Coast
Guard Enrolled Personnel Test (CGEPT).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Indian Coast Guard |
| Stages | Stage I written (CBT) → Stage II assessment and physical test → Stage III/IV medical and documents |

## Official sources

- Join Indian Coast Guard (official portal): <https://joinindiancoastguard.cdac.in>

## Exam pattern

**Stage I:** Section I, 60 questions in 45 minutes (maths, science, English, reasoning, general knowledge, at
Class 10 level). Section II, 50 questions in 30 minutes (maths and physics, at Class 12 level). Navik GD takes both
sections. Yantrik takes Section I plus a technical section for its trade.

## Syllabus

Class 10 and Class 12 NCERT maths and physics, English, reasoning, GK; engineering diploma syllabus for Yantrik.

1. Start physical training on day one: the physical tests decide many selections.
