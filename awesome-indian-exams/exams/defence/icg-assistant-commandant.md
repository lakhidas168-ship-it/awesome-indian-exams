# Indian Coast Guard Assistant Commandant (CGCAT)

*Evidence status: official. Verified against the 2027 Batch advertisement.*

## At a glance

The Indian Coast Guard (ICG) conducts the Coast Guard Assistant Commandant Admission Test (CGCAT) to recruit Assistant Commandants (Group 'A' Gazetted Officers).

*   **Stages:** Online Exam (Stage I), Preliminary Selection Board (Stage II), Final Selection Board (Stage III), Medical (Stage IV), Induction (Stage V).
*   **Age:** 21–25 years (as per the specific batch notification).
*   **Qualification:** Bachelor's degree for General Duty; Engineering degree for Technical branches.
*   **Pay Level:** Starts at Pay Level 10 (₹56,100).

## Exam pattern

The selection process consists of multiple stages:

1.  **Stage I (CGCAT):** Online computer-based examination.
2.  **Stage II (PSB):** Preliminary Selection Board (screening).
3.  **Stage III (FSB):** Final Selection Board (interview and testing).
4.  **Stage IV:** Medical examination.
5.  **Stage V:** Induction.

## Syllabus

The syllabus for the online examination (CGCAT) typically covers:

*   **General Duty:** English, Reasoning and Numerical Ability, General Science, Mathematical Aptitude, General Knowledge.
*   **Technical:** Professional subject knowledge (Engineering/Electrical/Electronics) in addition to the General Duty components.

*Refer to the official website for the detailed subject-wise syllabus.*
