## Official sources

- Agniveer Vayu portal (official): <https://agnipathvayu.cdac.in>

## Exam pattern

**Science subjects:** 60 minutes, English, Physics and Mathematics from the Class 12 CBSE syllabus.
**Other than science:** English plus reasoning and general awareness. **Both:** a combined paper. Marking: **+1**
correct, **−0.25** wrong.

## Syllabus

CBSE Class 11–12 physics and mathematics, English, reasoning and general awareness.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Start physical training on day one: the physical tests decide many selections.
2. NCERT Class 11–12 physics and maths for the science paper.
