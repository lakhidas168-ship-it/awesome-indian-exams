# Agniveer Vayu (Air Force)

The Air Force enrols Agniveer Vayu for four years under the Air Force Act 1950: an online test, then a
physical fitness test with adaptability tests, then a medical examination.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Indian Air Force |
| Stages | Phase I online test → Phase II (physical fitness test, Adaptability Test I and II) → Phase III medical |
| Engagement | Four years under the Air Force Act 1950; up to 25% of each batch may be enrolled in the regular cadre on centralised merit |
| Who can apply | Unmarried candidates meeting the education criteria below; the date-of-birth block is published in each intake notice |
| Intake dates and fee | See the current notification on the portal |

## Exam pattern

Phase I is an objective online test; questions are bilingual (English and Hindi) except the English paper.
Candidates eligible on a science qualification may opt for both papers in one sitting on the same system.

- **Science subjects:** 60 minutes — English, Physics and Mathematics.
- **Other than science subjects:** 45 minutes — English, plus Reasoning and General Awareness (RAGA).
- **Science + other than science:** 85 minutes — English, Physics, Mathematics and RAGA.
- **Marking:** +1 for a correct answer, 0 for an unattempted question, −0.25 for a wrong answer.
- Candidates must qualify in each paper separately; Phase I results and the Phase II shortlist are published
  on the portal.

Phase II: 1.6 km run within 6 minutes 30 seconds, plus 10 push-ups, 10 sit-ups and 20 squats in the
stipulated time; then Adaptability Test I (objective written test) and Adaptability Test II. Phase III is
medical examination at a designated medical boarding centre.

Service terms in the notification: Agniveer package of Rs. 30,000 per month with yearly increment plus
allowances; Seva Nidhi exit package of about Rs. 10.04 lakh after four years; non-contributory life cover of
Rs. 48 lakh; 30 days annual leave per year.

## Syllabus

- **Science paper:** English, Physics and Mathematics as per the Class 10+2 CBSE syllabus.
- **Other than science paper:** English as per the Class 10+2 CBSE syllabus, plus Reasoning and General
  Awareness (RAGA).
- **Eligibility base:** science needs Intermediate/10+2 with Mathematics, Physics and English (minimum 50%
  aggregate and 50% in English), or a three-year engineering diploma in a listed stream, or a two-year
  vocational course with Physics and Maths, each with 50% aggregate and 50% in English; other than science
  needs Intermediate/10+2 in any approved subject, or a two-year vocational course, with 50% aggregate and
  50% in English. Only COBSE-member boards count.
- Detailed syllabus and model question papers are published on the portal with each intake.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Start physical training on day one: the 1.6 km run timing plus push-ups, sit-ups and squats eliminate many candidates.
2. NCERT Class 11–12 Physics and Mathematics for the science paper; NCERT English and daily reasoning practice otherwise.
3. Attempt the mock test and tutorial on the portal before the exam day.
