SBI Clerk recruits Junior Associates (Customer Support and Sales) for the State Bank of India. Vacancies are
state-wise; you apply for one State/UT only, and the local language test matters.

## Exam pattern

**Prelims (Phase I):** online objective test, 100 marks in 1 hour, each section separately timed. No minimum
qualifying marks are prescribed for an individual test or the aggregate; about 10 times the vacancies per
category are shortlisted for mains on aggregate prelims marks.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **1 hour** |

**Mains (Phase II):** online objective test, 190 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours 40 minutes, each test separately
timed. Only mains marks count for the merit list (prelims marks are not added). You must score the minimum
aggregate qualifying marks decided by the bank (5% relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/XS/DXS); no sectional
cut-offs, and section-wise marks are not maintained.

| Test | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| General/Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 minutes |
| General English | 40 | 40 | 35 minutes |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 45 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 minutes |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **2 hours 40 minutes** |

**Negative marking (both phases):** 1/4th of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer.

**Phase III (local language):** provisionally selected candidates who did not study the specified local
language of their State/UT in class 10 or 12 appear for the Local Language Proficiency Test after the mains
and before joining; failing it means no appointment. Trained SBI apprentices get 5 bonus marks (2.5% of 200)
added to their mains aggregate, if the notification's conditions are met.

## Syllabus

The notification specifies test areas, not a detailed topic-wise syllabus. The shared banking modules cover
them: reasoning, quantitative aptitude, English, general and financial awareness, computer aptitude.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Target the mains aggregate cut-off: SBI Clerk prescribes no sectional minimum marks, and only mains decides
   the merit list. Prelims is a filter.
2. Practise puzzles, seating arrangement and data interpretation daily; every section is separately timed
   (20 minutes each in prelims, 35–45 minutes in mains), so practise each section to its own clock.
3. Build banking and financial awareness for mains from RBI and PIB releases.
4. If you did not study your State/UT's local language in class 10 or 12, prepare for the local language test:
   failing it cancels a provisional selection.
