# SBI Clerk (Junior Associate)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice

SBI Clerk recruits Junior Associates (Customer Support and Sales) for the State Bank of India. Vacancies are
state-wise, and the local language test matters.

## Official sources

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes (20 minutes per section), sections English Language,
Numerical Ability and Reasoning Ability, with negative marking of **1/4** per wrong answer.

**Mains:** 190 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours 40 minutes: General English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning and
Computer Aptitude, General/Financial Awareness, with sectional timing and negative marking. Then the local
language test.

## Syllabus

The shared banking modules: reasoning, quantitative aptitude, English, general and financial awareness,
computer aptitude.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Clear each section's cut-off, not just the total: bank exams time and score sections separately.
2. Practise puzzles, seating arrangement and data interpretation daily. They decide most reasoning and quant marks.
3. Build banking and financial awareness for mains from RBI and PIB releases.
