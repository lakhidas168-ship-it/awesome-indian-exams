SBI Clerk recruits Junior Associates (Customer Support and Sales) in the clerical cadre for the State Bank of
India. Vacancies are allocated state-wise; candidates apply for vacancies in one State or Union Territory only,
and qualifying the local language test of the opted state is mandatory.

## Exam pattern

**Phase I: Preliminary Examination:** Online objective test consisting of 100 questions for 100 marks with a total
duration of 1 hour. Each section has a separate individual timing of 20 minutes.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **1 hour** |

*Preliminary qualifying criteria:* There are no minimum qualifying marks prescribed for any individual section or
for the aggregate score. Section-wise marks are not maintained. Candidates numbering approximately 10 times the
number of vacancies in each category are shortlisted for the Main Examination from the merit list arranged in
descending order of aggregate marks scored.

**Phase II: Main Examination:** Online objective test consisting of 190 questions for 200 marks with a duration
of 2 hours and 40 minutes. Each section is separately timed.

| Section / Test | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| General/Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 minutes |
| General English | 40 | 40 | 35 minutes |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 45 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 minutes |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **2 hours 40 minutes** |

*Main qualifying criteria:* Only marks scored in the Main Examination are considered for the final selection and
merit list (preliminary exam marks are not added). Candidates must secure minimum aggregate qualifying marks
decided by the bank (with a 5% relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/XS/DXS). No minimum qualifying marks are prescribed
for individual subjects, and section-wise marks are not maintained.

**Negative marking:** In both Phase I and Phase II objective tests, 1/4th of the marks assigned to a question is
deducted for each incorrect answer.

**Phase III: Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT):** Provisionally selected candidates who did not study the
specified opted local language of their applied State/UT in Class 10 or Class 12 must appear for and qualify the
LLPT after the Main Examination and prior to joining. Failing the LLPT disqualifies the candidate from appointment.
The test format comprises:
1. *Non-Verbal Test (30 marks):* Objective section (3 passages, 15 questions, 15 marks) and Subjective section (3 passages, 15 questions, 15 marks).
2. *Verbal Test (20 marks).*
Candidates must score the minimum qualifying marks specified by the bank (5% relaxation for reserved categories).

**Apprentice weightage:** Trained apprentices of SBI who commenced engagement on or after 01.01.2022, completed
one year of branch training satisfactorily on or before 31.08.2026, and hold the joint National Apprenticeship
Certificate (issued by SBI-NSDC/BFSI-SSC) receive 5 bonus marks (2.5% of 200) added to their Main Examination
aggregate score.

## Syllabus

The official notification defines test areas rather than an exhaustive topic-wise syllabus. The topics align
with standard banking exam domains covered in the shared modules:
- **English Language:** Reading comprehension, cloze test, error detection, sentence rearrangement, vocabulary.
- **Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude:** Number series, simplification, quadratic equations, arithmetic (percentages, ratio, profit & loss, simple & compound interest, time & work), data interpretation (bar graphs, line charts, pie charts, tables).
- **Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude:** Puzzles and seating arrangement, coding-decoding, blood relations, syllogisms, inequalities, direction sense, data sufficiency, fundamental computer terminology and hardware/software basics.
- **General & Financial Awareness:** Banking awareness, Reserve Bank of India policy rates, financial schemes, current economic events, national news, awards and static banking general knowledge.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Target aggregate marks, not sectional cut-offs:** SBI Clerk does not enforce sectional minimum qualifying
   marks. Your objective is to maximize aggregate marks across the paper while respecting the sectional timer.
2. **Train with strict 20-minute section clocks:** In Prelims, you cannot switch sections or carry over unused
   minutes. Solve sectional test sets strictly within 20 minutes for English, Numerical Ability, and Reasoning.
3. **Build speed in arithmetic and reasoning puzzles:** Core arithmetic concepts (percentages, ratios) drive
   data interpretation in mains. Practice daily sets of high-density seating arrangements and circular/linear puzzles.
4. **Leverage General & Financial Awareness for Mains:** With 50 questions in 35 minutes, this section offers
   the highest score-to-time ratio in Phase II. Focus on the latest 5–6 months of banking news, monetary policy
   announcements, and economic schemes using official bulletins from RBI and PIB.
5. **Confirm local language eligibility early:** If your matriculation or higher secondary mark sheets do not
   explicitly list the local language of the state you applied for, allocate dedicated practice to reading,
   writing, and comprehension of that language to pass the 50-mark LLPT without surprises.
