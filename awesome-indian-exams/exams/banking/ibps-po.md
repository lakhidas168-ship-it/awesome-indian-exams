# IBPS PO (Probationary Officer / Management Trainee)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The prelims pattern below follows the revised 2025 pattern reported by
> several non-official sources on 2026-09-27. IBPS revises the pattern from time to time, so confirming it
> against the current notification is a hive task.

IBPS PO recruits probationary officers for the participating public sector banks. With SBI PO it is the main
officer-entry exam in Indian banking. Its prelims modules are the same as those of IBPS Clerk, IBPS RRB and SBI.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) |
| Stages | Prelims (CBT, qualifying) → Mains (CBT + descriptive) → interview |
| Qualification | Graduate degree |
| Final merit | Mains + interview (prelims only qualifies) |

## Official sources

- IBPS official website (notification, results, score cards): <https://www.ibps.in>

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** 60 minutes with **20 minutes per section** (sectional timing), 100 questions, **−0.25** of a question's
marks per wrong answer.

| Section | Questions | Marks (revised 2025 pattern) | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 30 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 40 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

**Mains:** objective sections on reasoning and computer aptitude, general/economy/banking awareness, English,
and data analysis and interpretation, plus a short descriptive English test (letter and essay). Section-wise
marks and time are in the notification.

## Syllabus

- **Quantitative Aptitude and DI:** simplification, number series, quadratic equations, arithmetic word problems,
  data interpretation (tables, bar, line, pie, caselets).
- **Reasoning:** puzzles and seating arrangement, syllogism, inequality, blood relations, direction, coding,
  input-output, data sufficiency.
- **English:** reading comprehension, cloze test, error detection, para jumbles, sentence improvement.
- **Awareness (mains):** banking and financial awareness, economy, static GK and current affairs.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Speed practice for **Quant** and **Reasoning**. With sectional timing, clearing each section's cut-off matters
   as much as the total.
2. **Puzzles and seating arrangement** daily. They carry the biggest share of reasoning marks.
3. **Banking awareness** from RBI's official publications and PIB for mains.
4. Write one descriptive letter or essay a week for the mains descriptive test.
