# IBPS PO (Probationary Officer / Management Trainee)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** Every number below is from the CRP PO/MT-XVI detailed
> notification (dated 30.06.2026, vacancies of 2027-28), read from the official PDF on 2026-09-29,
> and the cycle, dates and fee are also confirmed on the official application portal. The hive's
> recording fetcher is blocked by www.ibps.in (0 bytes), so per the evidence rules the page stays
> `secondary`, not `official`. Read the current notification before you apply or pay a fee.

IBPS PO recruits probationary officers and management trainees for eleven participating public sector
banks. With SBI PO it is the main officer-entry exam in Indian banking. Its prelims modules are the
same as those of IBPS Clerk, IBPS RRB and SBI.

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes, with each test separately timed at 20 minutes. You must
clear a cut-off in **each of the three tests** to be shortlisted for the Mains.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 30 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 40 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

**Mains:** 170 objective questions, 200 marks, 160 minutes, each test separately timed. The medium is
English and Hindi except English Language, which is English only.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Reasoning | 40 | 60 | 45 min |
| General / Economy / Banking Awareness / Digital & Financial Awareness (including RBI circulars) | 50 | 60 | 35 min |
| English Language | 40 | 20 | 35 min |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 40 | 60 | 45 min |
| **Total** | **170** | **200** | **160 min** |

A separate **descriptive paper** follows: 2 questions, 25 marks, 30 minutes, in English. It is an
**essay and a comprehension** passage — the notification says it may be broadly based on economic and
social issues, emerging trends in banking and technology, current events and ethics. IBPS may evaluate
it with an automated scoring mechanism, so write clearly rather than trying to be clever.

**Negative marking:** one fourth (0.25) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong
answer, in both Prelims and Mains. A blank answer carries no penalty.

**Scoring and shortlisting:** scores from different sessions are normalised by the equi-percentile
method, to two decimal points. You need a minimum score in **each** Mains test *and* a minimum total to
reach the Personality Test and interview.

**Personality Test:** a self-report, non-qualifying, but mandatory — skipping it makes you ineligible for
the interview.

**Interview:** 100 marks, minimum 40% (35% for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD). Final merit combines the Mains and
interview scores in the ratio 80:20. Only Mains marks count toward the merit list.

## Syllabus

**IBPS does not publish a topic-level syllabus for CRP PO/MT.** The notification names the tests
(English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability, Data Analysis & Interpretation, and
General/Economy/Banking/Digital & Financial Awareness) and stops there, so anything longer than that
comes from coaching material, not from IBPS. The linked shared modules below are the closest free
syllabus this repo can honestly offer.

What the notification *does* fix is the scope of the **descriptive paper**: economic and social issues,
emerging trends in banking and technology, current events, and ethics. Read one essay and one
comprehension on those, and one news item a day from a free source.

A practical study map, for orientation only (not an official IBPS list):

- **Quantitative Aptitude:** simplification, number series, quadratic equations, arithmetic word problems.
- **Data Analysis & Interpretation:** tables, bar, line, pie and caselet sets.
- **Reasoning:** puzzles and seating arrangement, syllogism, inequality, blood relations, direction,
  coding, input-output, data sufficiency.
- **English:** reading comprehension, cloze test, error detection, para jumbles, sentence improvement.
- **Awareness (Mains):** banking and financial awareness, economy, RBI circulars, static GK and
  current affairs.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Speed practice for **Quant** and **Reasoning**. With sectional timing, clearing each section's cut-off matters
   as much as the total.
2. **Puzzles and seating arrangement** daily. They carry the biggest share of reasoning marks.
3. **Banking awareness** from RBI's official publications, RBI circulars and PIB for mains. The Mains
   awareness paper names RBI circulars explicitly, so this section is not only current affairs.
4. Write **one essay and one comprehension** a week against the clock — two questions in 30 minutes, English
   only. Mains is 200 marks of which only 20 are English Language, but the descriptive paper is the part
   candidates most often lose by not practising timed writing.
5. Start **Data Analysis & Interpretation** early. It is 40 questions and 60 marks in 45 minutes, and it is
   the one Mains section that punishes untimed practice.
