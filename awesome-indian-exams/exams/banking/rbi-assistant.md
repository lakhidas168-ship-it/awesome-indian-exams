# RBI Assistant

RBI Assistant recruits assistants in various offices of the Reserve Bank of India. Selection is conducted through a country-wide competitive examination in two phases (Preliminary and Main examination) followed by a Language Proficiency Test (LPT) in the official/local language of the state concerned. Latest checked cycle: Notice dated October 06, 2023 (450 vacancies).

## Exam pattern

**Phase I: Preliminary Examination (Objective):** Online objective test consisting of 100 questions for 100 marks with a total duration of 60 minutes (1 hour). Each section has an individual sectional timing of 20 minutes.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 minutes |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 minutes** |

*Preliminary qualifying criteria:* Candidates have to qualify in the Preliminary Examination for appearing in the Main Examination. Candidates must pass in each of the objective tests as per the criteria decided by the Bank.

**Phase II: Main Examination (Objective):** Online objective test consisting of 200 questions for 200 marks with a total duration of 135 minutes (2 hours 15 minutes). Each section is separately timed.

| Section / Test | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Test of Reasoning | 40 | 40 | 30 minutes |
| Test of English Language | 40 | 40 | 30 minutes |
| Test of Numerical Ability | 40 | 40 | 30 minutes |
| Test of General Awareness | 40 | 40 | 25 minutes |
| Test of Computer Knowledge | 40 | 40 | 20 minutes |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **135 minutes** |

*Main qualifying criteria:* Final selection is based on candidate's performance in the Main Examination, biometric verification, and qualifying in the Language Proficiency Test (LPT) taken together in order of merit. Candidates must pass in each of the objective tests.

**Negative marking:** In both Preliminary and Main online examinations, there is negative marking for wrong answers. 1/4th (25%) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each incorrect answer.

**Phase III: Language Proficiency Test (LPT):** Candidates provisionally shortlisted from the Main online examination undergo an LPT in the official/local language of the State concerned. The test is qualifying in nature; candidates not proficient in the official/local language are disqualified.

## Syllabus

The official notification defines test areas corresponding to standard banking modules:
- **English Language:** Reading comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, error spotting, sentence completion and rearrangement.
- **Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude:** Number series, simplification, quadratic equations, arithmetic data, and data interpretation (tables, bar graphs, line charts).
- **Reasoning Ability:** Puzzles, seating arrangement, syllogism, inequality, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense.
- **General Awareness:** Current affairs, banking and financial awareness, RBI notifications, economic news, static GK.
- **Computer Knowledge:** Basic computer hardware and software, operating systems, MS Office (word processing, spreadsheets), internet, and networking basics.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Master speed and accuracy:** Prelims requires solving 100 questions in 60 minutes with sectional timing (20 minutes each). Regular timed sectional practice is essential.
2. **Clear sectional and aggregate cut-offs:** Practice each of the five sections for Mains thoroughly, giving special attention to General Awareness and Computer Knowledge alongside Quant, Reasoning, and English.
3. **Language proficiency:** Ensure fluency in reading, writing, speaking, and understanding the official/local language of the State you apply for, as LPT is mandatory and qualifying.
4. **Follow official updates:** Monitor Opportunities@RBI for upcoming recruitment notifications and official notices.
