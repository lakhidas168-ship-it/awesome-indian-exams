# SEBI Grade A (Assistant Manager)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

SEBI Grade A recruits Assistant Managers for the Securities and Exchange Board of India in several streams
(General, Legal, IT, Research, Official Language, Engineering).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Securities and Exchange Board of India |
| Stages | Phase I (two papers) → Phase II (two papers) → interview |
| Cut-offs | Phase I: Paper 1 30%, Paper 2 40%, aggregate 40% (recent notice) |

## Official sources

- SEBI official website (careers): <https://www.sebi.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**Phase I:** Paper 1: 80 questions, 60 minutes, English, quantitative aptitude, reasoning and general awareness.
Paper 2: 50 questions, 100 marks, 40 minutes, stream-specific. Phase I totals 200 marks. **−1/4** per wrong answer.

**Phase II:** Paper 1: English descriptive (3 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes). Paper 2: 100 objective questions,
100 marks, 90 minutes, stream-specific. Then the interview.

## Syllabus

Shared modules for Paper 1. Stream papers cover securities markets, finance, accounting, economics and
management (General stream) or the stream's own discipline.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Read SEBI's own investor-education material and annual reports for the securities-market topics.
2. Descriptive English is scored in Phase II. Practise it weekly.
