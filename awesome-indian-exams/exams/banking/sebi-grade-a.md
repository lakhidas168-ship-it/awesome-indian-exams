# SEBI Grade A (Assistant Manager)

SEBI Grade A recruits Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) staff for the Securities and Exchange Board of India
across seven streams: General, Legal, Information Technology, Research, Official Language, Engineering
(Electrical) and Engineering (Civil). The current cycle is the Officer Grade A 2025 notification (110 posts).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) |
| Cycle | Officer Grade A 2025 — applications Oct 30 – Nov 28, 2025; Phase I Jan 10, 2026; Phase II Feb 21, 2026 |
| Vacancies | 110 total (General 56 · Legal 20 · Information Technology 22 · Research 4 · Official Language 3 · Engineering (Electrical) 2 · Engineering (Civil) 3) |
| Stages | Phase I (two 100-mark papers) → Phase II (two 100-mark papers) → Phase III interview |
| Age (as on Sep 30, 2025) | Must not have exceeded 30 years (born on or after Oct 01, 1995); relaxations: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) 3 years, PwBD 10 years, Ex-servicemen 5 years |
| Qualification | Stream-specific. General stream: a two-year Master's degree/PG Diploma in any discipline, or a Bachelor's in Law, or a Bachelor's in Engineering, or CA/CFA/CS/Cost Accountant. Each other stream requires its own degree (see the notification) |
| Cut-offs | Phase I: Paper 1 30%, Paper 2 40%, aggregate 40% (screening only, not counted for final selection). Phase II: Paper 1 30%, Paper 2 40%, weighted aggregate 50% (1/3 Paper 1 + 2/3 Paper 2); about 3× vacancies shortlisted for interview |
| Final weightage | Phase II 85% + interview 15% |
| Negative marking | 1/4 of the marks for each wrong answer (both Phase I papers; Phase II multiple-choice questions only) |

## Exam pattern

**Phase I (screening, Jan 10, 2026).** Paper 1, all streams: multiple-choice questions on General Awareness
(including some financial-sector questions), English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning — 100 marks,
60 minutes, 30% cut-off (no sectional cut-off). Paper 2, stream-specific multiple-choice — 100 marks, 40 minutes,
40% cut-off; for the General stream the subject is Commerce, Accountancy, Management, Finance, Costing,
Companies Act and Economics, and for every other stream it is the stream's specialised subject. Separate
paper cut-offs plus an aggregate cut-off of 40% are needed to reach Phase II. **−1/4** per wrong answer in both
papers. Phase I marks are used only for shortlisting and are not counted in the final selection.

**Phase II (Feb 21, 2026).** Paper 1, all streams: English descriptive test of drafting skills — 100 marks,
60 minutes, 30% cut-off, 1/3 weight in the Phase II aggregate. Paper 2, stream-specific — 100 marks, 40%
cut-off, 2/3 weight:
- General stream: multiple-choice, 90 minutes.
- Legal stream: 70 one-mark multiple-choice questions (60 minutes) plus 3 descriptive questions of 10 marks each (60 minutes).
- Information Technology stream: multiple-choice on logic-flow completion, debugging, syntax understanding, program dry-run outputs and data analysis, 180 minutes.
- Research stream: descriptive test of practical, in-depth knowledge and drafting in economics, financial markets and statistics, 120 minutes.
- Official Language stream: translation and noting tasks, 90 minutes.
- Engineering (Electrical) and Engineering (Civil) streams: multiple-choice on the specialised subject, 40 minutes each.

**−1/4** per wrong multiple-choice answer in Phase II Paper 2. A weighted aggregate of 50% (1/3 Paper 1 + 2/3
Paper 2) is needed besides each paper's cut-off; candidates equal to three times the vacancies are shortlisted
for interview. All question papers except the English test are set bilingually in Hindi and English.

**Interview (Phase III).** Candidates may answer in Hindi or English. Final selection weights Phase II 85% and
interview 15%; for the Legal, Engineering (Electrical) and Engineering (Civil) streams, up to 3.75 marks of that
interview weight is awarded for relevant post-qualification experience.

## Syllabus

Phase I Paper 1: General Awareness (including the financial sector), English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and
Reasoning. Phase I Paper 2 and Phase II Paper 2 take the stream's specialised subject — for the General stream,
Commerce & Accountancy, Management, Finance, Costing, the Companies Act, 2013 and Economics. Phase II Paper 1
(all streams) is English Writing Skills: precis writing, essay writing and comprehension. The full paper-wise
syllabus, with indicative topic weightages, is in the annexure to the notification.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Read SEBI's own investor-education material and its annual reports for the securities-market topics — they
   are the authority the paper follows.
2. Phase I is only a screen; selection is decided by Phase II (85%) plus interview, so prepare the stream
   subject in depth from the first day.
3. Phase II Paper 1 is descriptive English for every stream. Write precis, essays and comprehension answers
   weekly and get them reviewed.
4. Track the SEBI careers page for the call letters, cut-offs and any corrigendum; the notification states that
   corrigenda are published only on sebi.gov.in.
