SEBI Grade A recruits Assistant Managers for the Securities and Exchange Board of India in the General,
Legal, Information Technology, Research, Official Language, Engineering (Electrical) and Engineering (Civil)
streams (2025 notification).

## Exam pattern

**Phase I (screening, Jan 10, 2026):** Paper 1 (all streams): multiple-choice questions on General Awareness
(including the financial sector), English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning — 100 marks,
60 minutes, 30% cut-off. Paper 2: multiple-choice questions on the stream's subject (General stream:
Commerce, Accountancy, Management, Finance, Costing, Companies Act and Economics) — 100 marks,
40 minutes, 40% cut-off. Aggregate 40% needed besides each paper's cut-off. **−1/4** per wrong answer in
both papers. Phase I marks are used only to shortlist for Phase II.

**Phase II (Feb 21, 2026):** Paper 1 (all streams): English descriptive test of drafting skills — 100 marks,
60 minutes, 30% cut-off, 1/3 weight in the Phase II aggregate. Paper 2 (stream-specific) — 100 marks,
40% cut-off, 2/3 weight: General stream multiple-choice (90 minutes); Legal stream 70 one-mark
multiple-choice questions plus 3 descriptive questions of 10 marks (60 + 60 minutes); IT stream
multiple-choice on logic, debugging, syntax and data analysis; Research stream descriptive test in
economics, financial markets and statistics (180 minutes); Official Language stream translation/noting
tasks (120 minutes); Engineering (Electrical) and Engineering (Civil) streams multiple-choice on the
specialised subject (40 minutes each). **−1/4** per wrong multiple-choice answer. Weighted aggregate
50% needed besides each paper's cut-off; about three times the vacancies are shortlisted for interview.
All papers except English are bilingual (Hindi and English).

**Interview:** in Hindi or English. Final selection weights Phase II 85% and interview 15% (Legal and
Engineering streams: up to 3.75 marks of the interview weight for relevant post-qualification experience).

## Syllabus

Phase I Paper 1: General Awareness (including the financial sector), English, Quantitative Aptitude and
Reasoning. Phase I Paper 2 and Phase II papers: the stream's specialised subject (General stream: Commerce,
Accountancy, Management, Finance, Costing, Companies Act and Economics). The detailed paper-wise syllabus
is in the annexure to the notification — see the notification PDF.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Read SEBI's own investor-education material and annual reports for the securities-market topics.
2. Descriptive English is scored in Phase II. Practise it weekly.
