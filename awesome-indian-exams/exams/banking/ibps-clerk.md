IBPS Clerk (Customer Service Associate) recruits clerks for the participating public sector banks. Its prelims
modules are shared with IBPS PO, IBPS RRB, SBI Clerk and RBI Assistant.

## Exam pattern

Per the CRP Clerks XIV notice (section D; penalty per section E):

**Prelims (objective, online):** 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes (20 minutes per section, separately timed), **−0.25** per wrong answer. Candidates must clear each section's cut-off; shortlisted candidates appear for the Mains.

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| English Language | 30 |
| Numerical Ability | 35 |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 |

**Mains (objective, online):** 190 questions, 200 marks, 160 minutes total, separately timed sections, **−0.25** per wrong answer. Minimum scores apply per test and on the total; only Mains marks count for the final merit (state/UT-wise cut-offs, then provisional allotment).

| Test | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| General / Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 minutes |
| General English | 40 | 40 | 35 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability and Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 minutes |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 45 minutes |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **160 minutes** |

## Syllabus

Reasoning (puzzles, seating, syllogism, inequality, coding, blood relations), quantitative aptitude
(simplification, series, arithmetic, data interpretation), English (reading comprehension, cloze, error detection),
general and financial awareness, and computer basics.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Clear each section's cut-off, not just the total: bank exams time and score sections separately.
2. Practise puzzles, seating arrangement and data interpretation daily. They decide most reasoning and quant marks.
3. Build banking and financial awareness for mains from RBI and PIB releases.
