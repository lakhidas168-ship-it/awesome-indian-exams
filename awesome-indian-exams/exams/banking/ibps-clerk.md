# IBPS Clerk

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

IBPS Clerk (Customer Service Associate) recruits clerks for the participating public sector banks. Its prelims
modules are shared with IBPS PO, IBPS RRB, SBI Clerk and RBI Assistant.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) |
| Stages | Prelims (qualifying) → Mains (merit) → language proficiency test |
| Qualification | Graduate degree |

## Official sources

- IBPS official website (notifications, results): <https://www.ibps.in>

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes (20 minutes per section), **−0.25** per wrong answer.

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| English Language | 30 |
| Numerical Ability | 35 |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 |

**Mains:** 190 questions, 200 marks, separately timed sections: reasoning (and computer aptitude), English,
quantitative aptitude, and general/financial awareness. The notification has the section-wise marks and time.

## Syllabus

Reasoning (puzzles, seating, syllogism, inequality, coding, blood relations), quantitative aptitude
(simplification, series, arithmetic, data interpretation), English (reading comprehension, cloze, error detection),
general and financial awareness, and computer basics.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Clear each section's cut-off, not just the total: bank exams time and score sections separately.
2. Practise puzzles, seating arrangement and data interpretation daily. They decide most reasoning and quant marks.
3. Build banking and financial awareness for mains from RBI and PIB releases.
