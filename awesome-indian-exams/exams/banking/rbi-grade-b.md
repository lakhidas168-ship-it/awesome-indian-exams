# RBI Grade B (Officer, DR General)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

RBI Grade B recruits officers for the Reserve Bank of India. Phase II tests economics, finance and management
at a depth closer to UPSC than to bank clerical exams.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Reserve Bank of India |
| Stages | Phase I (screening) → Phase II (three papers) → interview |
| Marks | Phase I 200 · Phase II 300 · interview 75 |

## Official sources

- RBI official website (Opportunities@RBI): <https://www.rbi.org.in>

## Exam pattern

**Phase I:** objective, 200 marks, on General Awareness, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and
Reasoning, with sectional cut-offs.

**Phase II:** three separately timed papers: Economic and Social Issues (objective and descriptive), English
(descriptive writing), and Finance and Management (objective and descriptive). The interview follows. The notification
has the time and negative marking.

## Syllabus

Phase I: the shared modules, with a heavy general awareness section. Phase II: the Indian economy, growth and
development, social issues, and finance (financial system, markets, RBI's role) plus management (leadership,
motivation, communication, ethics).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Phase II wins the selection. Start ESI and FM early from RBI's own reports and the Economic Survey.
2. Practise descriptive answers and essays weekly.
