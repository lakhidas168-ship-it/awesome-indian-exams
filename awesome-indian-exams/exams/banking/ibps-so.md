# IBPS SO (Specialist Officer)

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

IBPS SO recruits specialist officers (IT, Agricultural Field Officer, Law, HR/Personnel, Marketing, Rajbhasha)
for the participating public sector banks. Mains tests professional knowledge of the chosen field.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) |
| Stages | Prelims → Mains (professional knowledge) → interview |
| Qualification | Degree in the relevant field (see the notification) |

## Official sources

- IBPS official website (notifications, results): <https://www.ibps.in>

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** objective, with English, reasoning, and quantitative aptitude or general awareness depending on
the post. **Mains:** professional knowledge of the chosen specialisation. Then the interview. Negative marking
applies. The notification has the post-wise pattern.

## Syllabus

Prelims: the shared banking modules. Mains: the professional syllabus of the post (for example networking,
databases and security for IT; agronomy and agri-finance for AFO; banking law for Law Officer).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Your mains subject is your degree subject: revise it from standard textbooks and NPTEL.
2. Prelims needs only qualifying marks. Don't over-invest there.
