IBPS SO recruits specialist officers for the participating public sector banks in six posts: IT Officer,
Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR/Personnel Officer and Marketing
Officer (all Scale I). Selection is Prelims → Mains → interview, and only Mains marks count with the
interview for the final merit.

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** 100 questions, 125 marks, 80 minutes, with 20 minutes per section. Two variants:

| Section (IT / AFO / HR / Marketing) | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| English Language | 25 | 25 | 20 min |
| Reasoning | 25 | 25 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 25 | 20 min |
| Professional Knowledge | 25 | 50 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **125** | **80 min** |

For Law Officer and Rajbhasha Adhikari, Quantitative Aptitude is replaced by General Awareness
with special reference to the Banking Industry (25 questions, 25 marks, 20 min); the other three
sections are identical. Candidates must clear the cut-off in each of the four tests to be shortlisted
for the Mains.

**Mains:** 150 objective questions (200 marks, 125 min) plus a 2-question paper worth 25 marks in
30 minutes — 225 marks in 155 minutes in total. All Mains tests except Professional Knowledge are
qualifying; merit for interview shortlisting is on Professional Knowledge scores. The descriptive
paper is in English for IT/AFO/HR/Marketing/Law posts and in English and Hindi for Rajbhasha
Adhikari.

**Negative marking:** 0.25 of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer in
the objective tests (Prelims and Mains); blank answers carry no penalty.

**Interview:** 100 marks, minimum 40% (35% for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD). Final selection combines Mains
and interview scores in the ratio 80:20.

## Syllabus

Prelims: the shared banking modules (English, reasoning, quantitative aptitude, or general awareness
with special reference to banking for Law/Rajbhasha posts) plus professional knowledge of the post.
Mains: the professional syllabus of the post (for example networking, databases and security for IT;
agronomy and agri-finance for AFO; banking law for Law Officer), with the remaining Mains tests
qualifying only.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Your mains subject is your degree subject: revise it from standard textbooks and NPTEL, since
   Mains merit rests on Professional Knowledge.
2. Prelims marks do not count toward the final merit; focus on clearing the sectional cut-offs by
   practicing the shared modules: [English Language](../../modules/english-language.md), [Reasoning](../../modules/reasoning.md),
   [Quant Aptitude](../../modules/quant-aptitude.md), and [General Awareness](../../modules/general-awareness.md).
