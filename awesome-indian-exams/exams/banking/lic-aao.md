# LIC AAO (Assistant Administrative Officer)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Verified against the official notification fetched on 2026-09-30.

LIC AAO recruits Assistant Administrative Officers (Generalist) for the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) |
| Total Vacancies | 350 (Generalist, 32nd Batch) |
| Stages | Preliminary Examination (qualifying) → Main Examination (objective + descriptive) → Interview → Medical Examination |
| Age Limit | 21 to 30 years as on 01.08.2025 (born between 02.08.1995 and 01.08.2004 inclusive; relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD as per rules) |
| Qualification | Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized Indian University/Institution (result declared on or before 01.08.2025) |
| Application Fee | Rs. 700/- + transaction charges + GST for general/others; Rs. 85/- + transaction charges + GST for SC/ST/PwBD |

## Exam pattern

Selection is a three-tiered process: Preliminary Exam (Phase I), Main Exam (Phase II), and Interview. Marks in Prelims are not counted for final ranking.

### Phase I: Preliminary Examination
*Objective test, online, no negative marking.*

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| English Language | 30 | 30* | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **70** | **1 hour** |

*\*Qualifying nature; marks not counted for ranking.*

### Phase II: Main Examination
*Objective (300 marks) and Descriptive (25 marks) tests, online, no negative marking.*

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Reasoning Ability | 30 | 90 | 40 min |
| General Knowledge, Current Affairs | 30 | 60 | 20 min |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 30 | 90 | 40 min |
| Insurance and Financial Market Awareness | 30 | 60 | 20 min |
| **Total Objective** | **120** | **300** | **2 hours** |
| English Language (Descriptive) | 2 | 25* | 30 min |

*\*Qualifying nature; marks not counted for ranking.*

## Syllabus

- **Reasoning Ability & Quantitative Aptitude:** Standard banking-level modules.
- **General Knowledge & Current Affairs:** National and international current affairs, economic updates.
- **Data Analysis & Interpretation:** Tables, graphs, data interpretation sets.
- **Insurance & Financial Market Awareness:** Insurance principles, IRDAI regulations, financial markets, LIC history and products.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Master quantitative aptitude, reasoning, and data interpretation using shared banking modules.
2. Study insurance principles, financial markets, and IRDAI updates from official regulatory portals and LIC publications.
3. Practice descriptive writing (emails, reports, precis) regularly to clear the qualifying English paper.
4. Read daily current affairs from PIB and standard financial newspapers.
