# LIC AAO (Assistant Administrative Officer)

> **Evidence status: 🔴 unverified.** The facts below are based on historical patterns. An official notification for the current cycle was not successfully fetched this run. Always read the current notice before you apply or pay a fee.

LIC AAO recruits Assistant Administrative Officers (Generalist and Specialist) for the Life Insurance
Corporation of India.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Life Insurance Corporation of India |
| Stages | Prelims (qualifying) → Mains (objective + qualifying descriptive) → interview |
| Qualification | Graduate degree (Specialist posts: see the notification) |

## Official sources

- LIC careers (official): <https://licindia.in>

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** qualifying, with reasoning, quantitative aptitude and English.

**Mains:** an objective test (300 marks) on reasoning, general knowledge and current affairs, data analysis and
interpretation, and insurance and financial market awareness; plus a qualifying descriptive English test (essay and
letter). Final merit uses the mains objective marks and the interview. The notification has time and negative
marking.

## Syllabus

Shared banking-style modules, plus insurance and financial market awareness (insurance principles, IRDAI,
products, recent developments).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Insurance and financial market awareness is the unique section. Build it from IRDAI and LIC's own publications.
2. The descriptive test only needs a pass, but don't skip practice.
