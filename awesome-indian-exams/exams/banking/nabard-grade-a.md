# NABARD Grade A (Assistant Manager)

NABARD Grade A recruits Assistant Managers for the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development. The
2025 advertisement (No. 05/Grade A/2025-26) fills 91 posts across three services: Rural Development Banking
Service (RDBS — a General discipline plus thirteen specialised ones), Legal Service, and Protocol & Security
Service. Economic and social issues and agriculture and rural development (ESI and ARD) are the core of the
RDBS Generalist paper.

## Exam pattern

**Phase I (Preliminary) — online objective, 200 questions, 200 marks, 120 minutes (composite time for all tests
together), bilingual in Hindi and English except the English test.** It has eight tests. The first five are the
*qualifying* tests — you must clear each one, but they do not decide the Mains call; the last three are the
*merit* section, and shortlisting for the Mains is based on the merit-section marks alone.

| Test | Questions | Marks | Section |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Test of Reasoning | 20 | 20 | Qualifying |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | Qualifying |
| Computer Knowledge | 20 | 20 | Qualifying |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 20 | 20 | Qualifying |
| Decision Making | 10 | 10 | Qualifying |
| General Awareness | 20 | 20 | Merit |
| Economic & Social Issues (focus on Rural India) | 40 | 40 | Merit |
| Agriculture & Rural Development (emphasis on Rural India) | 40 | 40 | Merit |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | 120 minutes |

**Phase II (Mains) — online.** Two papers, common to every discipline in structure:

| Paper | Type | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Paper I — General English | Descriptive, typed | 3 | 100 | 90 minutes |
| Paper II — ESI & ARD (RDBS General) or the stream-specific subject | Objective | 30 | 50 | 30 minutes |
| Paper II — same paper | Descriptive, attempt 4 of 6 | 6 | 50 | 90 minutes |

For the **RDBS General** discipline, Paper II is Economic & Social Issues and Agriculture & Rural Development;
for the specialised disciplines and for Legal it is that discipline's own paper, with the same 50 + 50 split.
Descriptive answers are typed on a keyboard, in English or Hindi (Remington/Inscript keyboards). The
specialised-discipline and Legal Paper II syllabus is uploaded separately on the website.

**Phase III — Psychometric Test (mandatory):** multiple-choice, 90 minutes. Skipping it makes you ineligible for
the interview.

**Negative marking:** one fourth (1/4) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer, in
both **Phase I and Phase II**. A blank answer carries no penalty.

**Shortlisting and merit:** cut-offs are applied in two stages — on the individual tests and on the total. The
calling ratio to qualify for the Mains is a maximum of 1:25 and for the interview a maximum of 1:3 (the bank may
reduce it). Scores across sessions are equated, and test-wise and total scores are reported to two decimal
places. Final selection and ranking is on the Mains and interview taken together for RDBS/Legal, and on the
online examination and interview together for Protocol & Security.

## Syllabus

The notification calls its syllabus **indicative, not exhaustive**, and warns that questions can cover current
developments and Acts even when a topic is not listed.

**Phase I:** Reasoning, English Language, Computer Knowledge, Quantitative Aptitude, Decision Making, General
Awareness, Economic & Social Issues, and Agriculture & Rural Development.

**Phase II Paper I (English, all disciplines):** essay, précis writing, comprehension, and business/office
correspondence — a test of writing, expression and understanding of the topic.

**Phase II Paper II — ESI & ARD (RDBS General):**
- *Economic & Social Issues:* structure and reforms of the Indian economy; globalisation and privatisation;
  inflation and its impact; poverty alleviation and employment-generation programmes; population; agriculture
  and food security; rural credit and banking; industrial and labour policy; international funding institutions,
  the IMF, the World Bank and the WTO; social structure and social justice for SC/ST and other backward classes;
  education, health and environment; and current economic and social issues.
- *Agriculture & Rural Development:* agronomy and cropping systems; soil and water conservation; irrigation
  management; farm and agri engineering; plantation and horticulture; animal husbandry and poultry; fisheries;
  forestry; agricultural extension and Krishi Vigyan Kendras; ecology and climate change; agricultural finance
  and marketing; and rural development — the rural economy, rural labour, handicrafts, Panchayati Raj, and
  programmes such as MGNREGA, NRLM (Aajeevika), rural drinking water, Swachh Bharat, rural housing and PURA.

The full illustrative syllabus is in the notification itself.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **ESI and ARD decide the RDBS General result.** They are the entire merit section of Phase I and Paper II of
   the Mains. Prepare them from official sources — the Economic Survey, the Union Budget and the Ministry of
   Agriculture.
2. **Clear every Phase I qualifying test.** Reasoning, English, Computer Knowledge, Quantitative Aptitude and
   Decision Making must each be cleared, even though only the merit section counts for the Mains call.
3. **Practise typed descriptive writing.** Both Phase II papers are typed on a keyboard; Paper I is 100 marks of
   English descriptive writing and Paper II has a 50-mark descriptive part (attempt 4 of 6 questions).
4. **Write ESI/ARD answers weekly** against the clock — the descriptive part is where most candidates lose marks.
5. **Attempt the Psychometric Test.** It is mandatory and skipping it disqualifies you from the interview.
