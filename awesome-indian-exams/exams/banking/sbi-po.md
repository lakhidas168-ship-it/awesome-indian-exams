# SBI PO (Probationary Officer)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The official notification is hosted on `sbi.bank.in`, which is not yet in the repo's official-domain allow-list. The page content is based on the provided notification.

SBI PO recruits probationary officers (Junior Management Grade Scale-I) for the State Bank of India. It is
one of India's most sought-after banking exams. Selection runs in three phases: prelims (qualifying),
mains, then a psychometric test, group exercise and interview.

## Exam pattern

**Prelims (Phase I):** online objective test, 100 marks, 1 hour, separate timing per section. No sectional
cut-off; about 10 times the vacancies per category are shortlisted for mains on aggregate prelims marks.

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| English Language | 40 | 20 minutes |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 30 | 20 minutes |
| Total | 100 | 1 hour |

**Mains (Phase II):** online objective test for 200 marks plus a descriptive test for 30 marks (typed on
the computer right after the objective test). Grand total 230 marks, 3 hours 30 minutes. Each candidate
must score the minimum qualifying marks in every section including the descriptive paper; about 3 times the
category-wise vacancies are shortlisted for Phase III on aggregate mains marks.

| Test | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Reasoning & Computer Aptitude | 40 | 60 | 50 minutes |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 30 | 60 | 45 minutes |
| General Awareness / Economy / Banking Knowledge | 60 | 60 | 45 minutes |
| English Language | 40 | 20 | 40 minutes |
| Objective total | 170 | 200 | 3 hours |
| Descriptive: Communication Skills (email; situation analysis; report or precis writing) | 3 tasks | 30 | 30 minutes |

**Negative marking (prelims and mains objective):** 1/4th of the marks assigned to a question is deducted
for each wrong answer; no penalty for unattempted questions.

**Phase III:** psychometric test (personality profiling shared with the interview panel) plus Group
Exercise (20 marks) and Personal Interview (30 marks), total 50 marks.

**Final merit:** prelims marks are not counted. Mains (230) and Phase III (50) are normalized to 75 and 25
respectively, out of 100; selection is from the top of the category-wise merit list.

## Syllabus

The notification specifies test areas, not a detailed topic-wise syllabus:

- **Prelims:** English Language; Quantitative Aptitude; Reasoning Ability.
- **Mains objective:** Reasoning & Computer Aptitude; Data Analysis & Interpretation; General / Economy /
  Banking Awareness; English Language.
- **Mains descriptive:** communication skills — email, situation analysis, and report or precis writing.
- **Phase III:** personality profiling (psychometric), group exercise and interview.

For topic-level depth (puzzles, seating, syllogism, reading comprehension, banking awareness and the
rest), use the shared modules below; the notification itself is the final word on what each phase tests.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Clear each mains section's minimum qualifying score, not just the aggregate: bank exams time and score
   sections separately.
2. Practise puzzles, seating arrangement and data interpretation daily. They decide most reasoning and
   quant marks.
3. Build banking, economy and general awareness for mains from RBI and PIB releases.
4. Practise the descriptive tasks weekly: one email, one situation analysis, and one report or precis.
