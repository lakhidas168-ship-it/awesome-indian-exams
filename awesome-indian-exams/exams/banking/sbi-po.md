# SBI PO (Probationary Officer)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

SBI PO recruits probationary officers for the State Bank of India. It is one of India's most sought-after
banking exams. Its modules are the same as IBPS PO's, with its own marks split.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | State Bank of India |
| Stages | Phase I prelims (qualifying) → Phase II mains → Phase III (psychometric test, group exercise, interview) |
| Qualification | Graduate degree |

## Official sources

- SBI careers (official): <https://sbi.co.in>

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** 100 marks, 60 minutes, **−0.25** per wrong answer. In the recent pattern English carries 40 marks,
and Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning 30 marks each.

**Mains:** Data Analysis and Interpretation; Reasoning and Computer Aptitude; General/Economy/Banking Awareness;
English Language; plus a descriptive English test. Then Phase III. The notification has the section-wise marks and
time.

## Syllabus

Reasoning (puzzles, seating, syllogism, inequality, input-output), quantitative aptitude and data
interpretation, English (reading comprehension, para jumbles, cloze), banking and economy awareness, computer
aptitude, and descriptive writing (letter and essay).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Clear each section's cut-off, not just the total: bank exams time and score sections separately.
2. Practise puzzles, seating arrangement and data interpretation daily. They decide most reasoning and quant marks.
3. Build banking and financial awareness for mains from RBI and PIB releases.
4. Practise one descriptive letter or essay a week for mains.
