# CSEET (CS Executive Entrance Test)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from ICSI's restructuring announcement
> (dated 10.04.2026, effective from the June 2026 session), the official CSEET FAQs, and the official
> restructured CSEET syllabus — all published on the ICSI CSEET pages and fetched on 2026-09-28.

CSEET is the entry test for the Company Secretary course: passing it lets a 10+2 student register for
the CS Executive Programme. Graduates, postgraduates, CA Final / CMA Final pass students and CS
Foundation pass students are exempt and register for Executive directly.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI) |
| Sessions | Three a year — February, June, October (first week of the month) |
| Current cycle | October 2026 session (e-admit cards out); February 2027 registration closes 31.10.2026 |
| Papers | 4 papers, 100 marks each (400 total), over four days |
| To pass | 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate, in one sitting (no carry-forward) |
| Mode | Centre-based, afternoon session |
| Eligibility | Indian national who has passed or is appearing in 10+2 or equivalent |
| Fees | ₹7,500 registration (includes induction programme and online classes) + ₹1,500 exam fee per session |

## Exam pattern

Three subjective papers (3 hours each, 2:30 PM–5:45 PM) plus one OMR paper (2 hours, 2:30 PM–4:45 PM),
each with 15 minutes of question-paper reading time. No negative marking in the OMR paper.
E-marksheets only; the result stays valid one year, registration three years.

| Day | Paper | Marks | Pattern | Duration |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Day 1 | Business Communication | 100 | Subjective | 3 hours |
| Day 2 | Fundamentals of Accounting | 100 | Subjective | 3 hours |
| Day 3 | Economic and Business Environment | 100 | Subjective | 3 hours |
| Day 4 | Business Laws (60) and Management (40) | 100 | OMR-based | 2 hours |

## Syllabus

- **Paper 1 — Business Communication:** essentials of English (grammar, vocabulary, common errors,
  comprehension and summarising); communication concepts, media, listening and barriers; business
  correspondence (letters, reports, office notes, emails, notices, agenda and minutes); common business
  terminologies.
- **Paper 2 — Fundamentals of Accounting:** accounting concepts and principles; journal, ledger, trial
  balance and rectification of errors; bank reconciliation; depreciation; final accounts of sole
  proprietors; partnership and LLP accounts; company accounts (shares and debentures); accounts of
  non-profit organisations.
- **Paper 3 — Economic and Business Environment:** Part A Economics (50) — demand and supply, national
  income, Union Budget, Indian financial markets, Indian economy; Part B Business Environment (50) —
  entrepreneurship, business environment and ease of doing business, NITI Aayog / MCA / SEBI / RBI /
  IBBI / CCI / NCLT, global organisations, environmental governance, AI in business, corporate
  governance.
- **Paper 4 — Business Laws and Management:** elements of company law, contracts, partnership and LLP,
  sale of goods, negotiable instruments (60); management concepts, functions (planning, organising,
  staffing, directing, coordinating, reporting, budgeting), Fayol and Taylor principles (40).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Start with **Paper 2 accounting** — it is entirely new for most 10+2 non-commerce students and
   carries 100 subjective marks (see [Accounting and business basics](../../modules/accounting-basics.md)).
2. Build **Paper 1 English** daily: grammar and vocabulary plus one comprehension passage, using ICSI's
   free study material.
3. Cover **Paper 3** from the Union Budget, NCERT economics and ICSI material; track regulators (RBI,
   SEBI, MCA) through their own sites.
4. Learn **Paper 4** as bare-acts logic (contracts, NI Act, company basics) plus management functions;
   practise the OMR paper against the clock since it is 100 marks in 2 hours.
