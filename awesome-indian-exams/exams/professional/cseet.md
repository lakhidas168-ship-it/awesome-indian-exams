# CSEET (CS Executive Entrance Test)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

CSEET is the entry test for the Company Secretary course.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI) |
| Total | 140 questions, 200 marks |
| To pass | 40% aggregate (80 of 200), per recent notices |
| Mode | Centre-based from the June 2026 session (remote proctoring discontinued) |

## Official sources

- ICSI (official): <https://www.icsi.edu>

## Exam pattern

Four subjects, 35 questions each, with a mix of 1- and 2-mark questions: Business Communication, Legal
Aptitude and Logical Reasoning, Economic and Business Environment, and Current Affairs. The notification has the
subject list and marks.

ICSI's CSEET study material (free on the ICSI website).
