# CA Foundation

CA Foundation is the entry level of the Chartered Accountancy course under ICAI's New Scheme of Education
and Training. It is open to candidates who have appeared in or passed the Class 12 examination, and it is
held three times a year (January, May, September).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) |
| Papers | 4 papers, 100 marks each (400 marks total) |
| Paper duration | Papers 1-2: 3 hours each; Papers 3-4: 2 hours each |
| To pass | Minimum 40 marks in each paper and 50% in aggregate (all 4 papers, in one sitting) |
| Frequency | Three sessions a year: January, May, September |
| Eligibility | Registered for the Foundation Course with the Board of Studies and appeared in or passed Class 12 |

## Exam pattern

Descriptive-cum-objective examination of 400 marks across four papers:

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Time | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accounting | 100 | 3 hours | Descriptive (written) |
| 2 | Business Laws | 100 | 3 hours | Descriptive (written) |
| 3 | Quantitative Aptitude (business mathematics, logical reasoning, statistics) | 100 | 2 hours | Objective (OMR), negative marking |
| 4 | Business Economics | 100 | 2 hours | Objective (OMR), negative marking |

- Papers 1 and 2 get 15 minutes of advance reading time (1:45 PM to 2:00 PM); there is no advance reading time for Papers 3 and 4.
- In Papers 3 and 4, 1/4 mark is deducted for each wrong answer; darkening more than one circle for a question counts as a wrong answer.
- Pass: minimum 40 marks in each paper and 50% aggregate across all four papers in one sitting. There is no exemption in any paper at Foundation level.
- Candidates scoring 70% or above are declared "Pass with Distinction".
- Medium: English or Hindi (OMR answer sheets for Papers 3 and 4 are in English only).

## Syllabus

As per the ICAI Foundation syllabus under the New Scheme of Education and Training:

1. Accounting
2. Business Laws
3. Quantitative Aptitude (business mathematics, logical reasoning, statistics)
4. Business Economics

ICAI's Foundation study material for each paper is free on the ICAI website (see Free resources).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Study ICAI's own Board of Studies material for each paper first: it is free and sets the standard.
2. Practise with ICAI revision test papers, suggested answers and past question papers for your session.
3. For Papers 3 and 4, drill MCQs under timed conditions and skip questions you are unsure of, since each wrong answer costs 1/4 mark.
