# CA Foundation

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

CA Foundation is the entry level of the Chartered Accountancy course. It can be taken after Class 12 and is
held several times a year.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) |
| Papers | 4 papers, 100 marks each, 3 hours each |
| To pass | 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate |

## Official sources

- ICAI (official): <https://www.icai.org>

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Subject | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accounting | Descriptive |
| 2 | Business Laws | Descriptive |
| 3 | Quantitative Aptitude (maths, logical reasoning, statistics) | Objective, negative marking |
| 4 | Business Economics | Objective, negative marking |
