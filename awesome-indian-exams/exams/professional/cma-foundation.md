# CMA Foundation

CMA Foundation is the entry level of the Cost and Management Accountancy course (Syllabus 2022),
conducted by the Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI) twice a year in June and December
terms. Current term: December 2026 examination on Sunday, 13 December 2026.

## Exam pattern

The examination is conducted in offline OMR-based objective multiple-choice question (MCQ) mode across two 2-hour sessions on a single examination day. There is no negative marking.

| Session | Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning (10:00 AM – 12:00 PM) | Paper 1 | Fundamentals of Business Laws and Business Communication (FBLC) | 50 MCQs | 100 | 2 hours | None |
| Morning (10:00 AM – 12:00 PM) | Paper 2 | Fundamentals of Financial and Cost Accounting (FFCA) | 50 MCQs | 100 | 2 hours | None |
| Afternoon (2:00 PM – 4:00 PM) | Paper 3 | Fundamentals of Business Mathematics and Statistics (FBMS) | 50 MCQs | 100 | 2 hours | None |
| Afternoon (2:00 PM – 4:00 PM) | Paper 4 | Fundamentals of Business Economics and Management (FBEM) | 50 MCQs | 100 | 2 hours | None |

- Total marks: 400 (4 papers × 100 marks).
- Question weightage: Each paper contains 50 multiple choice questions carrying 2 marks each.
- Sessions: Papers 1 and 2 are conducted together in the morning session (100 MCQs, 200 marks, 120 minutes); Papers 3 and 4 are conducted together in the afternoon session (100 MCQs, 200 marks, 120 minutes). Pace yourself at roughly 1.2 minutes per question.
- Qualifying / Pass standard: Minimum 40% marks in each individual paper (40/100) and an aggregate of at least 50% across all four papers (200/400) in a single sitting.

## Syllabus

Under Syllabus 2022, the Foundation curriculum comprises four compulsory papers:

1. **Paper 1: Fundamentals of Business Laws and Business Communication (FBLC)**
   - Section A: Fundamentals of Business Laws
   - Section B: Business Communication
2. **Paper 2: Fundamentals of Financial and Cost Accounting (FFCA)**
   - Section A: Fundamentals of Financial Accounting
   - Section B: Fundamentals of Cost Accounting
3. **Paper 3: Fundamentals of Business Mathematics and Statistics (FBMS)**
   - Section A: Fundamentals of Business Mathematics
   - Section B: Fundamentals of Business Statistics
4. **Paper 4: Fundamentals of Business Economics and Management (FBEM)**
   - Section A: Fundamentals of Business Economics
   - Section B: Fundamentals of Management

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Cover the four papers from ICMAI's own Foundation study material, starting with Paper 2 (accounting) and Paper 3 (maths and statistics), which reward daily problem-solving practice.
2. Drill OMR-style MCQs under the real pace: 100 questions per 2-hour session leaves about a minute per question; since there is no negative marking, attempt every question.
3. Solve past term suggested answers and practice test papers from the ICMAI examination section (<https://icmai.in/ClntStudents/Suggested_Answers>) to benchmark timing and accuracy.
4. Complete the online examination application before the deadline (14 October 2026 for the December 2026 term).
