# CMA Foundation

CMA Foundation is the entry level of the Cost and Management Accountancy course (Syllabus 2022),
conducted by the Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI) twice a year in June and December
terms. Current term: December 2026 examination on Sunday, 13 December 2026.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI) |
| Papers | 4 papers, 100 marks each (400 total); each paper is 50 MCQs × 2 marks |
| Mode | Offline OMR-based MCQ at exam centres; no negative marking |
| Current term | December 2026 — Sunday 13 December 2026: Papers 1–2 from 10 AM–12 noon, Papers 3–4 from 2 PM–4 PM |
| Eligibility | Register after Class 10; appear after passing 10+2 (or recognised equivalent). Register by 31 January for the June term, 31 July for the December term |
| Exam fee (Dec 2026) | ₹1,500 inland / US$100 overseas; apply online only by 14 October 2026 (15–21 October with ₹500 late fee) |
| Pass rule | Minimum 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate (CMA Prospectus, Syllabus 2022) |

## Exam pattern

All four papers are objective (MCQ), answered on OMR sheets at centres — the December 2026 notice
confirms offline OMR centre-based mode with no negative marking. The whole examination is held on one
day in two 2-hour sessions: Papers 1 and 2 together in the morning session (100 MCQs, 200 marks) and
Papers 3 and 4 together in the afternoon session (100 MCQs, 200 marks), so pace yourself at roughly a
minute per question. The four papers are:

1. Paper 1: Fundamentals of Business Laws and Business Communication (FBLC) — 100 marks
2. Paper 2: Fundamentals of Financial and Cost Accounting (FFCA) — 100 marks
3. Paper 3: Fundamentals of Business Mathematics and Statistics (FBMS) — 100 marks
4. Paper 4: Fundamentals of Business Economics and Management (FBEM) — 100 marks

To pass, a candidate needs minimum 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate across the four papers
(per the CMA Prospectus, Syllabus 2022). The December 2026 result is due by 7 January 2027.
Detailed instructions come with the admit card — read them rather than older summaries.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Cover the four papers from ICMAI's own Foundation study material, starting with Paper 2
   (accounting) and Paper 3 (maths and statistics), which reward daily practice.
2. Drill OMR-style MCQs under the real pace: 100 questions per 2-hour session leaves about a minute
   per question, and with no negative marking every question should be attempted.
3. Work through previous Foundation question papers in the Examination section of icmai.in, then apply
   online before the form deadline (14 October 2026 for the December 2026 term).
