# BPSC Combined Competitive Examination

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

BPSC's Combined Competitive Examination recruits Bihar's administrative, police and allied
officers.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Bihar Public Service Commission |
| Stages | Preliminary (objective) → Main (descriptive) → interview |

## Official sources

- Commission's official website (notifications, syllabus, previous papers): <https://bpsc.bih.nic.in>

## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** one General Studies paper, 150 objective questions, 150 marks. From the 71st CCE, **+1** correct
and **−1/3** wrong. It is only for screening. **Mains:** descriptive papers (general Hindi, qualifying; essay; general
studies), then an interview. The notification has the details.

## Syllabus

General studies as in UPSC CSE (polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and
technology, current affairs), with **state-specific** history, geography, economy and culture. The main exam adds
essays, a language paper and GS papers. The commission's notification has the exact syllabus.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of every state PSC.
2. Add the state's own history, geography, economy and schemes from official state sources.
3. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: each commission has its own style.
