# JPSC Combined Civil Services

officers.

## Exam pattern

**Preliminary (screening only; its marks are not counted for the final merit):** two compulsory
objective papers, **General Studies Paper-I** and **General Studies Paper-II**, of **200 marks**
each (400 marks in all), **2 hours** each, with **100 objective questions of 2 marks** each; all
questions are compulsory. **There is no negative marking**; a question with more than one answer
is treated as wrong and gets no marks, and a blank question carries no penalty. The papers are set
in Hindi and English. The Main-exam selection list is about **5 times** the advertised vacancies.

**Main (written), six compulsory papers, no optional subjects:**

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---|
| I | General Hindi and General English (two sections of 50 marks; qualifying only — minimum 30 out of 100, not counted for merit) | 100 | 3 hours |
| II | Language and Literature (one of the 15 languages listed in the notification) | 150 | 3 hours |
| III | Social Sciences (History and Geography, equal weightage) | 200 | 3 hours |
| IV | Indian Constitution and Polity, Public Administration and Good Governance | 200 | 3 hours |
| V | Indian Economy, Globalization and Sustainable Development | 200 | 3 hours |
| VI | General Sciences, Environment and Technology Development | 200 | 3 hours |
| | **Total marks (papers counted for merit)** | **950** | |

Paper-I is qualifying only: its marks are not counted for the merit list. The **interview** is
**100 marks**. Written (950 marks) + interview (100 marks) = **grand total 1,050 marks**.

## Syllabus

- **Preliminary General Studies Paper-I:** Indian history, geography, polity and governance,
  economy and sustainable development, science and technology, national and international
  current events, and miscellaneous general awareness (human rights, environment, urbanization,
  sports, disaster management, poverty and unemployment, awards, international agencies).
- **Preliminary General Studies Paper-II:** Jharkhand-specific — state history and movements,
  society, culture and identity, folk literature, music and sites, Jharkhandi literature and
  authors, land laws such as CNT and SPT, geography, industry, state schemes, forest and
  environment, disaster management, and state current events.
- **Main:** Paper-II is one language and literature chosen from the notification's list; Papers
  III–VI expand the general-studies subjects above in descriptive form. The exact topic list is
  in the Combined Civil Services syllabus linked under Official sources.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of every state PSC.
2. Add Jharkhand's own history, geography, economy and schemes from official state sources:
   the prelims Paper-II is Jharkhand-specific.
3. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: each commission has its own style.
