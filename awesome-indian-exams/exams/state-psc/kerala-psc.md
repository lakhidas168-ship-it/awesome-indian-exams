# Kerala PSC exams (LDC, KAS and others)

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** The commission's site returned no readable content through the
> evidence-fetch tool this run, so the status cannot be upgraded yet. The facts below were read directly
> from official commission documents (gazette notification, KAS Special Rules, KAS prelims syllabus) and
> each section names the document it comes from. Confirm details in the linked notification before applying.

Kerala PSC recruits for most Kerala government posts, from Last Grade Servants and LDC to the Kerala
Administrative Service (KAS), through rolling Extraordinary Gazette notifications. The latest gazette live
at the time of writing is dated 31/08/2026 (Category Nos. 130/2026 to 150/2026, last date 07-10-2026).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Kerala Public Service Commission |
| Notification style | Rolling Extraordinary Gazette notifications, each with its own category numbers, eligibility and last date |
| How to apply | Online only through One Time Registration on the commission website; no application fee |
| Selection chain | Written/OMR/Online test (needs a separate confirmation on your profile) → ranked list → advice |
| Ranked-list validity | Minimum one year; continues till a new list or three years, whichever is earlier |
| KAS entry | Three streams in 1:1:1 ratio — Stream 1 open (21–32 years), Stream 2 government servants (21–40 years), Stream 3 gazetted officers (up to 50 years); bachelor's degree for all |

## Exam pattern

Most posts: a single Written/OMR/Online screening test whose syllabus and medium are fixed in that post's
notification. Candidates must confirm on their profile that they will write the test — only confirmed
candidates can download admission tickets (per the 31/08/2026 gazette notification, e.g. Category
No. 133/2026).

KAS (per the KAS Special Rules): the scheme of examination is set by the commission in consultation with
the government, on the pattern, rigour and standards of the UPSC Civil Services examination of the prior
year — i.e. preliminary screening, main examination and interview, with separate ranked lists for each of
the three streams. Appointees undergo 18 months of training and 2 years of probation.

## Syllabus

Per-post tests follow the syllabus printed in that post's notification (commonly general knowledge with
Kerala-specific content, simple arithmetic and mental ability, plus English/Malayalam).

The official KAS preliminary syllabus has two papers. Paper I (General Studies I) covers Indian and Kerala
history, world history from the mid-18th century, Kerala's cultural heritage, the Constitution,
governance and international relations, reasoning plus simple arithmetic, and geography. Paper II
(General Studies II) covers the economy (including Kerala's economy and the Kerala model of development),
science and technology (including ICT, space, energy and environment), current events, English language
proficiency, and Malayalam (or Kannada/Tamil) language proficiency.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of every state PSC.
2. Add Kerala's own history, geography, economy, culture and schemes from official state sources.
3. For KAS, work through the official prelims syllabus topic by topic using NCERTs and PIB.
4. Solve the commission's own previous papers: <https://www.keralapsc.gov.in/index.php/previous-question-papers>
