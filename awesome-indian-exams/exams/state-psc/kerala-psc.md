# Kerala PSC exams (LDC, KAS and others)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below was checked on 2026-09-29 against documents fetched
> from keralapsc.gov.in this run: the notifications index, the 31/08/2026 Gazette page, a sample post
> notification (Cat. No. 133/2026), the KAS Special Rules and KAS Preliminary syllabus, and the commission's
> application, previous-paper and post-wise-syllabus pages.

Kerala PSC recruits for most Kerala government posts, from Last Grade Servants and Lower Division Clerks to
the Kerala Administrative Service (KAS), through rolling Extraordinary Gazette notifications. The latest
batch live when this was checked is dated 31/08/2026 (Category Nos. 130/2026 to 150/2026, last date
07-10-2026).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Kerala Public Service Commission |
| Notification style | Rolling Extraordinary Gazette notifications, each with its own category numbers, eligibility and last date |
| How to apply | Online only, through One Time Registration on the commission website; no application fee |
| Selection chain | Written/OMR/Online test (needs a separate confirmation on your One Time Registration profile) → ranked list → advice |
| Ranked-list validity | At least one year; continues until a new list is published after that year, or until three years, whichever is earlier |
| KAS entry | Three streams in the proportion 1:1:1 — Stream 1 open (21–32 years), Stream 2 from government departments (21–40 years), Stream 3 from gazetted officers (up to 50 years); a bachelor's degree for all three |

## Exam pattern

For most posts the route is direct recruitment: register once, apply to the category, then sit a
Written/OMR/Online test if the commission holds one for that post, followed by a ranked list and advice
against vacancies. The sample notification (Cat. No. 133/2026) states that when a Written/OMR/Online test is
held, candidates must submit a confirmation to write it through their One Time Registration profile; only
confirmed candidates can generate and download admission tickets (in the last 15 days before the test), and
applications without a confirmation within the stipulated period are rejected. Candidates are also expected
to read the general conditions that form Part II of the Gazette notification.

KAS (per the KAS Special Rules): the scheme of examination is decided by the commission in consultation with
the government, and follows the pattern, rigour and standards of the UPSC Civil Services examination held the
year before the KAS exam. Separate ranked lists are prepared for the three streams, and recruitment to the
KAS (Junior Time Scale) is in the proportion 1:1:1 across them. Officers undergo 18 months of training and
are on probation for two years on duty within a continuous period of three years.

## Syllabus

Each post's test follows the syllabus printed in its own notification and listed in the commission's
post-wise syllabus index; general knowledge with Kerala-specific content, simple arithmetic and mental
ability, and English/Malayalam are common across posts.

The official KAS Preliminary syllabus has two papers. Paper I (General Studies I) covers Indian and Kerala
history, the history of the world from the mid-18th century, Kerala's cultural heritage, the Constitution,
public administration, the political system, governance, social justice and international relations,
reasoning, mental ability and simple arithmetic, and geography. Paper II (General Studies II) covers the
economy and planning (including Kerala's economy and the Kerala model of development), science and
technology (including ICT, space, energy and environment), current events, and language proficiency in
English or Malayalam (or Kannada or Tamil).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Cover the shared UPSC-style GS base once — it carries most state PSC papers (see the
   [overlap map](../../resources/overlap-map.md)).
2. Add Kerala's own history, geography, economy, culture and schemes from official state sources.
3. For KAS, work through the official preliminary syllabus topic by topic using NCERTs and PIB.
4. Solve the commission's own previous question papers (link below) for the real question style.
