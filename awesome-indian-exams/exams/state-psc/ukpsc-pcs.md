# UKPSC Combined State Civil Services

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from the Uttarakhand Combined State
> Civil/Upper Subordinate Services Examination 2026 notification (Advt A-1/E-1/2026-27, published
> 09-09-2026, 81 pages), fetched from psc.uk.gov.in on 2026-09-28.

UKPSC's Combined State Civil/Upper Subordinate Services exam recruits Uttarakhand's officers
(for example Deputy Collector, police, finance, education and other Group B posts). The 2026
cycle advertises **67 posts** (the count may rise or fall; see the notification).

## Exam pattern

**Preliminary (objective, screening only):** two papers. Its marks are **not** counted in the final
merit; it only shortlists candidates for the Main exam. No calculator.

| Paper | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Paper 1: General Studies (1 mark each) | 150 | 150 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2: General Aptitude Test (1.5 marks each) | 100 | 150 | 2 hours |

- **Negative marking:** one-fourth (1/4) of the marks for every wrong answer (including multiple answers to one question).
- **Merit** is on Paper 1 (General Studies) only. Paper 2 is qualifying: minimum **33%** for every category.
- At least **one-third** of the 150 GS questions are on Uttarakhand.

**Main (written):** all eight papers compulsory, no calculator. Minimum **35%** in General Hindi is mandatory.

| Paper | Time | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| General Hindi | 3 hours | 150 |
| Essay | 3 hours | 150 |
| General Studies I (heritage and culture, world history and geography, society) | 3 hours | 200 |
| General Studies II (governance, constitution, polity, social justice, international relations) | 3 hours | 200 |
| General Studies III (technology, economic development, biodiversity, environment, security, disaster management) | 3 hours | 200 |
| General Studies IV (ethics, integrity, aptitude) | 3 hours | 200 |
| General Studies V (knowledge of Uttarakhand) | 3 hours | 200 |
| General Studies VI (knowledge of Uttarakhand) | 3 hours | 200 |
| Written total | | 1500 |

**Interview/personality test: 150 marks. Grand total (written + interview): 1650 marks.**
Main-exam centres are Haldwani and Haridwar.

## Syllabus

- **Prelims GS:** Indian history, culture and national movement; polity and governance; geography;
  economy; science and technology; environment; current affairs — with at least one-third of questions
  on Uttarakhand. The notification lists the full unit-wise syllabus.
- **Prelims GAT (qualifying, 33%):** general aptitude as in the notification.
- **Mains:** General Hindi, essay, four general GS papers (I–IV as above) and **two full papers on
  Uttarakhand** (GS V and VI: state history, geography, economy, polity, schemes, culture). The
  commission's notification has the exact unit-wise syllabus.
- State-specific Uttarakhand content decides much of this exam: roughly a third of prelims GS plus
  400 of 1500 mains marks.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of prelims GS and mains GS I–IV.
2. Add Uttarakhand's own history, geography, economy, polity and schemes from official state sources —
   this is the highest-weight state-specific block (two mains papers plus a third of prelims GS).
3. Practise Hindi writing and essays weekly; General Hindi needs 35% or the mains fails.
4. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: each commission has its own style.
   The −1/4 penalty rewards accuracy.
