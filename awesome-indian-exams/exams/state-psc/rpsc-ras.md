# RPSC RAS/RTS (Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official (2026-09-29).** The numbers below were checked against the current RPSC 2026-cycle documents fetched this run: the advertisement (Advt. No. 02/2026-27), the Preliminary and Main scheme/syllabus issued by press note dated 09-01-2026, and the commission's exam dashboard.

RPSC's RAS/RTS exam recruits Rajasthan's administrative, police, accounts and allied services officers. The 2026-cycle advertisement names **607 posts** — **192** in the State Services and **415** in the Subordinate Services.

## Exam pattern

**Preliminary:** one objective paper, "General Knowledge and General Science" — **200 marks**, **150 multiple-choice questions** carrying equal marks, **3 hours**. There is a **negative marking of 1/3 mark** for each wrong answer. It is a screening test only: the prelims marks of candidates who qualify do not count towards the final order of merit. The standard is that of a bachelor's degree.

**Main:** four descriptive/analytical papers — General Studies I, General Studies II, General Studies III, and General Hindi & General English — **200 marks and 3 hours each** (800 marks in all). The question papers include brief, medium and long-answer/descriptive questions, and General Hindi and General English are at senior-secondary level. About **15 times** the number of vacancies are admitted to the Main examination.

**Interview:** candidates are selected through the competitive examination and an interview; the commission may apply scaling/moderation/normalisation while evaluating answer scripts.

## Syllabus

General studies (polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and technology, current affairs), with a strong **Rajasthan** component: the history, art, culture, literature, tradition and heritage of Rajasthan, and the state's geography, economy and current affairs. The Main examination adds three General Studies papers and a General Hindi & General English paper, including essays. The commission issued the exact scheme and syllabus by press note dated 09-01-2026 (see Official sources).
