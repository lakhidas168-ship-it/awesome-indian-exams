# KPSC KAS (Karnataka Administrative Service)

> **Evidence status: ✅ official.** This page has been verified against the official notification. All numbers under At a glance and Exam pattern match the source.

KPSC's Karnataka Administrative Service exam recruits Karnataka's Group A and B officers.

## Exam pattern

The KPSC KAS exam consists of three stages: Preliminary (objective), Main (descriptive), and Personality Test (interview).

**Preliminary Examination:**
- Paper I: General Studies (200 marks, 2 hours, 100 questions @ 2 marks each)
- Paper II: General Studies (200 marks, 2 hours, 100 questions @ 2 marks each)
- Total: 400 marks
- Negative marking: 0.5 marks for each wrong answer (one-fourth of the marks assigned to a question)

**Main Examination:**
- Qualifying Papers:
  - Kannada: 150 marks (2 hours) – minimum 35% required to qualify
  - English: 150 marks (2 hours) – minimum 35% required to qualify
- Compulsory Papers:
  - Paper I: Essay – 250 marks (3 hours)
  - Paper II: General Studies 1 – 250 marks (3 hours)
  - Paper III: General Studies 2 – 250 marks (3 hours)
  - Paper IV: General Studies 3 – 250 marks (3 hours)
  - Paper V: General Studies 4 – 250 marks (3 hours)
- Total written marks: 1250
- Personality Test: 25 marks
- Grand Total: 1275 marks

## Syllabus

General studies as in UPSC CSE (polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and
technology, current affairs), with **state-specific** history, geography, economy and culture. The main exam adds
essays, a language paper and GS papers. The commission's notification has the exact syllabus.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of every state PSC.
2. Add the state's own history, geography, economy and schemes from official state sources.
3. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: each commission has its own style.
