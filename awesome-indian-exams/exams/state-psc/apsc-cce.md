## Exam pattern

**Prelims:** Paper-I General Studies I and Paper-II General Studies II. Objective multiple-choice. Paper-II is qualifying. Negative marking applies.

**Mains:** Conventional essay type. Six papers: Paper 1 (Essay) and Papers 2–6 (General Studies). Interview/Personality Test follows.

## Syllabus

Prelims General Studies I and II cover general studies (polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and technology, current affairs) with Assam-specific history, geography, economy and culture. The Main written exam consists of an essay paper plus five General Studies papers.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of every state PSC.
2. Add Assam's own history, geography, economy and schemes from official state sources.
3. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website.
