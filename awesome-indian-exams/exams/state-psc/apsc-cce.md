officers (Assam Civil Service Jr. Grade, Assam Police Service Jr. Grade and others, per the
advertisement's vacancy table).

## Exam pattern

**Prelims (screening only; marks not counted for final merit):** Paper-I General Studies I (200 marks,
2 hours) and Paper-II General Studies II (200 marks, 2 hours), objective multiple-choice, total 400.
Paper-II is **qualifying at 33%**. Four alternatives per question; **−1/4** mark per wrong answer;
marking more than one answer counts as wrong.

**Mains:** candidates 11–12 times the notified vacancies per category are admitted to the written
part. Six papers, conventional essay type: Paper 1 Essay (250 marks, 3 hours), Papers 2–6 General
Studies (250 marks each, 3 hours each). Answers may be written in English or Assamese. The Main
examination is held at Guwahati only. Interview/Personality Test calls go in a 1:3 ratio against
advertised vacancies; the interview carries **180 marks** with no qualifying marks. Final ranking is
written (1500) + interview (180) = **1680**.

## Syllabus

Prelims General Studies I and II follow the Assam Public Services Combined Competitive Examination
(Amendment) Rules, 2019 (the notification points there for the exact syllabi): general studies as in
UPSC CSE (polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and technology, current
affairs) with Assam-specific history, geography, economy and culture. The Main written exam is an
essay paper plus five General Studies papers. The commission's notification and the CCE Rules have
the exact syllabus.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of every state PSC.
2. Add Assam's own history, geography, economy and schemes from official state sources.
3. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: each commission has its own style.
