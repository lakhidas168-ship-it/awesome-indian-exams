MPSC's State Services (Rajyaseva) examination recruits officers for Group A and Group B
posts across Maharashtra's civil administration, including the Maharashtra Civil Service (Executive Branch),
Maharashtra Police Service, Maharashtra Finance & Accounts Service, Tehsildar, and allied state services.
On the commission's official portal, it is administered under the Maharashtra Civil Services
Gazetted examinations framework.

## Exam pattern

The Maharashtra Civil Services Gazetted (State Services / Rajyaseva) examination is conducted in three successive stages:

1. **Preliminary Examination (Objective Screening):**
   - Comprises two objective multiple-choice papers: General Studies (Paper 1) and Civil Services Aptitude Test / CSAT (Paper 2).
   - Designed strictly as a screening test to shortlist candidates for the Main examination; marks obtained in Prelims are not counted in the final merit ranking.
   - Paper 2 (CSAT) functions as a qualifying paper with a minimum threshold (typically 33%).
   - Negative marking is applied for incorrect answers in objective papers per commission rules.

2. **Main Examination (Conventional Descriptive):**
   - The commission restructured the Main examination into a descriptive, conventional written pattern aligned with the UPSC Civil Services Examination format.
   - Includes qualifying language papers (Marathi and English), an Essay paper, and General Studies papers covering Indian and Maharashtra polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and technology, and ethics.
   - Candidates write subjective answers evaluated on analytical clarity, contextual precision, and depth of understanding.
   - Note: The commission periodically issues corrigendums and updates regarding question paper structure and instructions on its official notices board; candidates must consult the specific notification for their examination year.

3. **Interview / Personality Test:**
   - Candidates clearing the Main written cut-off are summoned for a personal interview before the commission board to assess administrative temperament, leadership qualities, awareness of public issues, and problem-solving aptitude.

## Syllabus

The syllabus balances all-India foundational knowledge with in-depth Maharashtra state-specific topics:

- **General Studies (Common Foundation):**
  - **Indian Polity & Governance:** Constitution of India, political system, Panchayati Raj, public policy, rights issues, and judicial mechanisms.
  - **Indian & World Geography:** Physical, social, and economic geography of India and the world; climate change, environmental ecology, biodiversity.
  - **History of India & National Movement:** Ancient, medieval, and modern Indian history, with special focus on the socio-religious reform movements and freedom struggle.
  - **Indian Economy & Development:** Macroeconomic indicators, sustainable development, poverty alleviation, banking and monetary policy, budget, and agriculture.
  - **General Science & Technology:** Recent scientific advancements, space, IT, defense, biotechnology, and everyday scientific principles.
  - **Current Affairs:** Events of state, national, and international significance.

- **Maharashtra State-Specific Component:**
  - **History of Maharashtra:** Role of Maharashtra in 19th and 20th century reform movements (including Jyotirao Phule, Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj, and other social reformers), Satyashodhak Samaj, and the Samyukta Maharashtra movement.
  - **Geography of Maharashtra:** Physiography, rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Bhima basins), agro-climatic zones, soils, mineral distribution, forests, and demographic profile.
  - **Economy of Maharashtra:** State industrial profile, agrarian economy, infrastructure, co-operative movement in Maharashtra, and state budget priorities.
  - **State Administration & Schemes:** Administrative machinery of Maharashtra, rural and urban local self-government institutions, and prominent welfare schemes.

- **Descriptive & Language Components (Mains):**
  - **Essay Writing:** Reflection on contemporary social, economic, administrative, and philosophical themes.
  - **Language Papers (Marathi & English):** Précis writing, essay, comprehension of unseen passages, translation, and grammatical usage.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Build core GS fundamentals:**
   Master the foundational subjects (Indian Polity, Modern History, Geography, Economy, and General Science) using NCERT textbooks and the free shared modules. Establish clear conceptual clarity before moving to state-specific material.

2. **Master the Maharashtra state component:**
   Read Maharashtra State Board (Balbharati) textbooks for state history and geography. Study the Maharashtra State Economic Survey and state government portal releases for regional policies, agricultural initiatives, and social development schemes.

3. **Develop consistent descriptive answer writing:**
   Practice daily answer writing for descriptive GS and essay questions. Focus on structured introductions, balanced multi-dimensional arguments, relevant government data, and constructive conclusions.

4. **Practice CSAT and language papers:**
   Regularly solve comprehension, numerical aptitude, and logical reasoning sets to clear the CSAT qualifying threshold comfortably. Practice Marathi and English grammar, précis writing, and translation exercises.

5. **Solve official past question papers:**
   Download previous years' question papers from MPSC's official repository (`/previous_question_papers/21`). Solve them under timed exam conditions to benchmark preparation and understand the commission's question trends.
