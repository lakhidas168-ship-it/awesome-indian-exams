MPSC's State Services (Rajyaseva) exam recruits Maharashtra's Group A
and B officers. It is now grouped under the Maharashtra Civil Services Gazetted examinations
framework on the commission's website.

## Exam pattern

An objective **preliminary** exam screens candidates, a descriptive **main**
exam follows, and an **interview** closes the process. The commission has published a
**revised syllabus**, a **revised Main-exam pattern with question-paper structure and
question-paper-specific instructions**, and descriptive-type instructions for the
Maharashtra Gazetted Civil Services Main Examination (see the Important Notices on
mpsc.gov.in). The notification for your year sets the number of papers, marks, time,
negative marking, qualifying rules and interview marks — the commission has also announced
further Main-exam structural changes taking effect from 2027, so confirm the paper-wise
structure for your attempt in the notification itself.

## Syllabus

General studies as in UPSC CSE (polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and
technology, current affairs), with **state-specific** history, geography, economy and culture. The main exam adds
essays, language papers and GS papers. The commission's notification and its Syllabus of
Examination page have the exact syllabus.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of every state PSC.
2. Add the state's own history, geography, economy and schemes from official state sources.
3. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: each commission has its own style.
