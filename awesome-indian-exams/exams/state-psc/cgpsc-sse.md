# CGPSC State Service Examination

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from the State Service Examination 2025
> advertisement (Advt 06/2025/Exam dated 26-11-2025, 41 pages, with Parishisht-1 exam scheme in Hindi
> and English) plus its corrigendum dated 12-12-2025, both fetched from psc.cg.gov.in on 2026-09-28.

CGPSC's State Service Examination recruits Chhattisgarh's administrative and allied officers (Deputy
Collector, Deputy SP, finance, commercial tax, employment, agriculture and other services). The 2025
cycle advertises **239 posts** (advertisement as amended by the corrigenda; see the notification for
the post-wise table).

## Exam pattern

**Preliminary (objective, screening only):** two compulsory papers of 2 hours each, multiple-choice
with four options. Its marks are **not** counted in the final merit; it only shortlists candidates
for the Main exam.

| Paper | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Paper 1: General Studies (2 marks each) | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2: Aptitude Test (2 marks each) | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |

- **Negative marking:** one-third (1/3) of the marks for every wrong answer, in both papers.
- **Minimum qualifying marks** in each paper: **33%** for unreserved, **23%** for reserved categories.
- Paper 2 (Aptitude) is qualifying only; the mains shortlist is on the **Paper 1 General Studies**
  merit alone.

**Main (written, conventional descriptive):** all seven papers compulsory, 3 hours and 200 marks
each. Answers (except the language paper) in Hindi or English only, not mixed within a paper.

| Paper | Time | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| Paper I: Language | 3 hours | 200 |
| Paper II: Essay (four essays, two from each of two sections — national/international issues and Chhattisgarh issues, about 750 words each) | 3 hours | 200 |
| Paper III: General Studies I | 3 hours | 200 |
| Paper IV: General Studies II | 3 hours | 200 |
| Paper V: General Studies III | 3 hours | 200 |
| Paper VI: General Studies IV | 3 hours | 200 |
| Paper VII: General Studies V | 3 hours | 200 |
| Written total | | 1400 |

- Each GS paper (III–VII) has five sections: 22 × 2-mark (30 words), 13 × 4-mark (60 words),
  8 × 8-mark (100 words), one 10-mark choice question, and two 15-mark choice questions — 200 marks.
- **Interview/personality test: 100 marks. Grand total (written + interview): 1500 marks.**
- Absence in any mains paper or the interview means disqualification.

## Syllabus

- **Prelims GS:** general studies with a Chhattisgarh component (state history, geography, economy,
  culture); **Prelims Aptitude:** qualifying reasoning/numeracy test. The notification's
  Parishisht-2 has the exact unit-wise syllabus.
- **Mains:** language, essay (one section fully on Chhattisgarh issues), and five GS papers covering
  history, polity, geography, economy, science, environment and current affairs with a strong
  Chhattisgarh share — including state welfare schemes. Parishisht-3 has the exact syllabus.
- State-specific Chhattisgarh content (history, geography, minerals, agriculture, tribal culture,
  government schemes) decides much of this exam.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of prelims GS and mains GS papers.
2. Add Chhattisgarh's own history, geography, economy, minerals, tribal culture and government
   schemes from official state sources — this is the highest-weight state-specific block (a full
   essay section plus much of the GS papers).
3. Practise Hindi/English essay and answer writing weekly to the word limits (30/60/100/125/175
   words per section); the −1/3 prelims penalty rewards accuracy.
4. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: each commission has its own style.
