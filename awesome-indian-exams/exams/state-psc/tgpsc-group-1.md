## Exam pattern

**Preliminary Test (objective, screening).** General Studies and Mental Ability, 150 questions,
150 marks, 2½ hours; held in English, Telugu and Urdu. It shortlists candidates for the Main
examination at 50 times the number of vacancies in each Multi-Zone, and its marks are **not
counted** for final ranking.

**Main examination (descriptive).** General English is a qualifying paper only (150 marks,
3 hours, Secondary School Certificate standard; minimum 40% for OC / EWS / Meritorious
Sportsperson, 35% for BC and 30% for SC / ST / PH). The merit list is built from six papers of
150 marks each, 3 hours each:

| Paper | Subject | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| Paper-I | General essay — three essays, one from each section, 50 marks each | 150 |
| Paper-II | History, culture and geography | 150 |
| Paper-III | Indian society, constitution and governance | 150 |
| Paper-IV | Economy and development | 150 |
| Paper-V | Science & technology and data interpretation | 150 |
| Paper-VI | Telangana movement and state formation | 150 |
| **Total counted** | | **900** |

**Qualifying marks in the Main examination:** 40% of the aggregate for OC / EWS / Meritorious
Sportsperson, 35% for BC and 30% for SC / ST / PH. Candidates must appear in all papers; absence
in any paper disqualifies them. After the Main examination the commission does certificate
verification (with web options for post and Multi-Zone) and medical fitness checks; selection
follows Main-examination merit.

## Syllabus

- **Preliminary:** the 13 topics of Annexure-II — current affairs (regional, national and
  international); international relations; general science and India's achievements; environment
  and disaster management; economic and social development of India; world, Indian and Telangana
  geography; Indian history and cultural heritage; Indian constitution and polity; governance and
  public policy; Telangana state policies; Telangana society, culture, arts and literature; social
  exclusion and inclusive policies; logical reasoning, analytical ability and data interpretation.
- **Main:** Indian history (modern period), Telangana history and heritage, India and Telangana
  geography; Indian society, constitution and governance; Indian and Telangana economy,
  development and environment; science and technology and data interpretation; the Telangana
  movement and state formation (1948–2014). The six papers other than General English may be
  answered in English, Telugu or Urdu, and the chosen medium applies to all of them.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once, then add Telangana-specific history, movement,
   geography, economy and policies from official state sources.
2. Practise descriptive writing early: the Main exam is six 3-hour written papers, and Paper-I
   alone needs three essays in 3 hours.
