# TGPSC (Telangana) Group 1

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

TGPSC Group 1 recruits Telangana's Deputy Collectors, DSPs and other senior officers.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Telangana Public Service Commission |
| Stages | Preliminary (objective) → Main (descriptive) → selection |

## Official sources

- Official website: <https://www.tgpsc.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

A UPSC-style objective preliminary exam for screening, then descriptive main papers (general English, essay,
general studies with a strong Telangana component). The notification has the details.

## Syllabus

General studies with Telangana's history, movement, geography, economy and policies.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules, then Telangana-specific topics from official state sources.
