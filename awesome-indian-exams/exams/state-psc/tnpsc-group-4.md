# TNPSC Group 4

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from TNPSC Notification No. 07/2025
> (Combined Civil Services Examination – IV, dated 25.04.2025), Addendum 7B/2025 (dated 26.09.2025),
> the commission's scheme-of-examination page, syllabus code 496, and the 2026 Annual Planner —
> all fetched from tnpsc.gov.in on 2026-09-28.

TNPSC Group 4 (CCSE-IV) recruits Village Administrative Officers, Junior Assistants, Typists,
Steno-Typists (Grade III), Forest Guards and Forest Watchers, and similar posts across Tamil Nadu.
Selection is through **one objective written exam** plus certificate verification and counselling.
There is no mains and no interview.

## Exam pattern

One OMR paper at SSLC standard: **200 questions, 300 marks, 3 hours.**

| Part | Subject | Questions | Marks | Medium |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| A | Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test | 100 | 150 | Tamil only |
| B | General Studies | 75 | 150 (Parts B and C together) | Tamil and English |
| C | Aptitude and Mental Ability | 25 | | Tamil and English |

- Parts B and C are evaluated only if the candidate scores at least 40% (60 of 150) in Part A.
- Minimum **90 of 300** overall to qualify for selection (all communities).
- The notification prescribes no negative marking.
- Merit is the total of Parts A, B and C. Ties are broken by higher qualification (higher
  typewriting/shorthand grade first for Typist/Steno-Typist posts), then seniority in age, then
  the earlier-submitted application.
- Differently-abled candidates who studied only English may take General English (SSLC,
  100 questions) instead of the Tamil test, with the same 40% rule.

## Syllabus

- **General Studies (75 questions):** General Science 5 · Geography 5 · History, Culture of India
  and Indian National Movement 10 · Indian Polity 15 · Indian Economy and Development
  Administration in Tamil Nadu 20 · History, Culture, Heritage and Socio-Political Movements of
  Tamil Nadu 20 (includes Thirukkural and the Tamil freedom movement).
- **Aptitude and Mental Ability (25 questions):** aptitude 15 — simplification, percentage, HCF,
  LCM, ratio and proportion, simple and compound interest, area, volume, time and work;
  reasoning 10 — logical reasoning, puzzles, dice, visual reasoning, alphanumeric reasoning,
  number series.
- **Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test (100 questions):** grammar (இலக்கணம்) 25, vocabulary
  (சொல்லகராதி) 15, writing skills (எழுதும் திறன்) 15, literature and Tamil scholars
  (இலக்கியம் — Thirukkural, didactic works, Tamil savants) 15, plus comprehension, translation
  and official-usage units — see the syllabus PDF for the full unit-wise split.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Secure Part A Tamil first: it gates the rest of the paper (40%) and carries half the marks.
   Use SSLC Tamil textbooks.
2. Cover General Studies from NCERT Class 6–10 plus Tamil Nadu state-board books — the two
   Tamil Nadu-specific units alone carry 40 of the 75 GS questions.
3. Practise aptitude and reasoning daily with the shared modules below; then solve the
   commission's own previous papers from tnpsc.gov.in in 3-hour timed sittings (there is no
   negative marking, so attempt all 200).
