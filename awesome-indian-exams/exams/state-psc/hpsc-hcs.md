HPSC's Haryana Civil Services (Executive Branch) and Allied Services exam recruits Haryana's
officers (HCS Ex. Br., DSP, ETO, AETO, Tehsildar/Naib Tehsildar and others). The 2025 cycle
(Advt. No. 22/2026) advertised 102 posts.

## Exam pattern

**Preliminary (screening only, 200 marks):** two objective (multiple-choice) papers, two hours
each, set bilingually in English and Hindi. Paper I General Studies (100 marks) decides the
result; Paper II CSAT (100 marks) is qualifying with 33% minimum. Each wrong answer costs
one-fourth (0.25) mark; each question has five options (A–E, darken E if skipping) and a
fully blank answer also costs 0.25. Prelims marks do not count in final merit; 12 times the
advertised vacancies (category-wise, by Paper I merit with CSAT 33%) enter the Main exam.

| Paper | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---|
| Paper I: General Studies | 100 | 2 hours |
| Paper II: Civil Services Aptitude Test (qualifying, 33%) | 100 | 2 hours |

**Main written (600 marks):** six conventional (descriptive) papers of 100 marks each, three
hours each, answered in Hindi or English (language papers excepted). To reach the viva a
candidate needs 45% in aggregate of the written papers and at least 33% each in English and
Hindi.

| Paper | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Paper I: English (including English Essay) | 100 |
| Paper II: Hindi in Devnagri script (including Hindi Essay) | 100 |
| Paper III: General Studies I | 100 |
| Paper IV: General Studies II | 100 |
| Paper V: General Studies III | 100 |
| Paper VI: General Studies IV | 100 |

**Personality Test / Viva-voce (75 marks):** three times the advertised posts (by Main merit)
are interviewed. Final merit is Main (600) + Viva (75) = 675, with ties broken by Main marks,
then age.

## Syllabus

Per the 07.01.2026 Gazette notification: the Prelims covers General Studies (general science,
national/international current events, Indian history and national movement, Indian and world
geography, Indian culture/polity/economy, mental ability, and Haryana's economy, people,
institutions and language) plus a CSAT (comprehension, communication, reasoning, decision
making, mental ability, Class-X numeracy and data interpretation). The Main covers English
with essay (precis, comprehension, usage, grammar), Hindi with essay in Devnagri script
(translation, letter/precis, explanation, composition), and GS I–IV (history/culture/geography
with Haryana issues; polity/governance/IR; economy/science/environment/security; ethics,
integrity and aptitude with case studies). The Gazette PDF above has the full topic lists.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the shared UPSC GS modules once. They cover most of the HCS Prelims and GS I–IV.
2. Add Haryana's own history, geography, economy and schemes from official state sources —
   both Prelims GS and every GS paper carry "related issues of Haryana".
3. Practise descriptive answer writing and the English/Hindi essay papers; the 45% aggregate
   plus 33%-per-language bar decides who reaches the viva.
4. Solve the commission's own previous papers from its website: HCS has its own five-option
   OMR style with a penalty even for blank answers.
