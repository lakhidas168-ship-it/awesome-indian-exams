# AIIMS NORCET (Nursing Officer)

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

NORCET recruits Nursing Officers for AIIMS and other central institutes.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | AIIMS New Delhi |
| Stages | Preliminary CBT → Main CBT (recent cycles) |
| Qualification | B.Sc Nursing, or diploma plus experience (see the notice) |

## Official sources

- AIIMS exams (official): <https://www.aiimsexams.ac.in>

## Exam pattern

Computer-based, in two stages in recent cycles, mostly nursing subjects plus some general aptitude and
general knowledge. Question counts, time and negative marking are in the notice.

## Syllabus

The B.Sc Nursing curriculum (medical-surgical, community health, paediatric, obstetric, mental health
nursing, fundamentals), plus general aptitude.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Nursing subjects decide the rank. Cover them fully before the aptitude part.
