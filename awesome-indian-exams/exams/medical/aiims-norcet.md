institutes/hospitals.

## Exam pattern

Two successive computer-based stages. Negative marking: **−1/3** per wrong answer in both stages.

| Stage | Duration | Questions | Marks | Syllabus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage I (Prelim)** | 90 min | 100 | 100 | 80 Nursing, 20 GK/Aptitude |
| **Stage II (Main)** | 180 min | 160 | 160 | 160 Nursing (Case-scenario) |

*   **Stage I (Qualifying):** 5 sections of 20 questions / 18 minutes each (not revisitable). Qualifying: 50th percentile (UR/EWS), 45th (OBC), 40th (SC/ST).
*   **Stage II (Merit):** 4 sections of 40 questions / 45 minutes each (not revisitable). Qualifying: 50% (UR/EWS), 45% (OBC), 40% (SC/ST).

## Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the essential qualification (B.Sc. Nursing / GNM curriculum).
*   **Nursing Subjects:** Fundamentals of Nursing, Medical-Surgical Nursing, Community Health Nursing, Child Health Nursing, Mental Health Nursing, Midwifery and Obstetrical Nursing, Nursing Research, Management in Nursing.
*   **General Knowledge & Aptitude (Stage I only):** General awareness, quantitative aptitude, reasoning, and English language.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1.  **Understand the Core:** Review the B.Sc. Nursing / GNM curriculum. Focus on clinical case-scenario questions for Stage II.
2.  **Practice Time Management:** The exam uses a strict, non-revisitable section-based timer. Practice solving questions within the allotted time per section.
3.  **Use Standard Textbooks:** Refer to standard nursing textbooks used in your B.Sc./GNM course.
4.  **Stay Updated:** Monitor the official AIIMS examinations portal for any updates or notices regarding the exam.
