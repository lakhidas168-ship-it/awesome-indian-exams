# FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

FMGE is the screening test that Indian citizens with a foreign medical degree must pass to register as doctors
in India. It is held twice a year.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) |
| Total | 300 questions, 300 marks, in two parts |
| Negative marking | None |
| Pass mark | 150 of 300 (see the bulletin) |

## Official sources

- NBEMS official website: <https://natboard.edu.in>

## Exam pattern

Two parts of 150 questions, **2 hours 30 minutes each**. The pre-clinical and para-clinical subjects carry
100 marks and the clinical subjects 200. One mark per question, no negative marking.

## Syllabus

The Indian MBBS curriculum, all subjects.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Pass mark, not rank, is the target: aim for consistent coverage of all subjects.
2. Clinical subjects carry two-thirds of the marks.
