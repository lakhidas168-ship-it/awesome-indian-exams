# NEET PG

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

NEET PG is the entrance for MD, MS and PG diploma seats. It is taken by MBBS graduates after internship.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) |
| Mode | Computer-based, single shift, 3 hours 30 minutes |
| Total | 180 questions, 720 marks (revised from 200 questions for 2026) |

## Official sources

- NBEMS official website: <https://natboard.edu.in>

## Exam pattern

Five **separately timed sections** of 36 questions each. When a section's time ends, the test moves on
and you cannot go back. Marking: **+4** correct, **−1** wrong, 0 unanswered.

## Syllabus

The MBBS curriculum: pre-clinical, para-clinical and clinical subjects, as notified by NBEMS.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Practise with timed sections: you cannot return to earlier ones.
2. Past NBEMS papers and image-based questions.
