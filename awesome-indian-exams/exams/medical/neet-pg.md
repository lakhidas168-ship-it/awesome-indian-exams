# NEET PG

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary (checked 2026-09-28).** Every number below matches the NBEMS NEET-PG 2026
> Information Bulletin and NBEMS notices (exam date, single shift, 180 questions, 720 marks, five timed sections,
> +4/−1 marking, cut-offs). The 2026 bulletin itself is linked from the NBEMS exam page but hosted on Google Drive,
> which is not an official domain, so this page cannot be marked `official`. Always read the current notice before
> you apply or pay a fee.

NEET PG is the single entrance for MD, MS, PG diploma, post-MBBS DNB, direct 6-year DrNB and NBEMS diploma seats.
It is taken by MBBS graduates (or provisional pass holders with NMC/state registration) who have completed, or will
complete by the cut-off date, one year of internship.

## Exam pattern

Five **separately timed sections** (Groups A–E) of **36 questions** each, **42 minutes** per section. When a section's
time ends, the next starts automatically and you cannot go back. Marking: **+4** correct, **−1** wrong, 0 unanswered.
Qualifying cut-offs for 2026 (out of 720): General/EWS 262 (50th percentile), General-PwBD 244 (45th percentile),
SC/ST/OBC 226 (40th percentile).

## Syllabus

Subjects of the MBBS curriculum (pre-clinical, para-clinical and clinical), as per NMC/erstwhile MCI regulations, as
notified by NBEMS. AIIMS New Delhi and other AIIMS, PGIMER Chandigarh, JIPMER Puducherry, NIMHANS Bengaluru and
SCTIMST Trivandrum are not covered by NEET-PG admissions.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Practise with timed sections of 36 questions in 42 minutes: you cannot return to earlier ones.
2. Revise the full MBBS curriculum with weightage to clinical subjects, plus image-based questions.
3. Use the NBEMS demo test on natboard.edu.in before exam day to learn the computer-based format.
