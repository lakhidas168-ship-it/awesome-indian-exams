# SSC Junior Hindi Translator (JHT)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

SSC JHT recruits Junior Hindi Translators, Junior Translators and Senior Hindi Translators for central
government offices.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| Stages | Paper 1 (CBT) → Paper 2 (descriptive) |
| Qualification | Master's in Hindi or English with the other language at degree level, or as notified |

## Official sources

- SSC official website (notices, syllabus, answer keys): <https://ssc.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**Paper 1:** 200 objective questions, 200 marks, 2 hours, **−0.25** per wrong answer, in General Hindi and
General English. **Paper 2:** descriptive, with translation passages (Hindi to English and English to Hindi) and
essays in both languages.

## Syllabus

Hindi and English grammar, vocabulary and comprehension; translation and essay writing.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Translate one passage each way every day, and have it checked.
