# SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

SSC CHSL recruits Class 12 pass candidates for posts such as Lower Division Clerk, Junior Secretariat Assistant
and Data Entry Operator in central ministries and departments. Its Tier 1 has the same four subjects as SSC CGL, so the
two are best prepared together.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| Stages | Tier 1 (CBT, qualifying) → Tier 2 (CBT) → document verification |
| Qualification | Class 12 pass (some posts need specific subjects; see notice) |

## Official sources

- SSC official website (notices, syllabus, answer keys): <https://ssc.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**Tier 1:** computer-based, 60 minutes, 100 questions, 200 marks, **−0.50** per wrong answer.

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| English Language | 25 | 50 |
| General Intelligence | 25 | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 50 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 50 |

**Tier 2:** computer-based, conducted in two sessions on one day. Session I (mathematical abilities, reasoning,
English, general awareness and a computer-knowledge module) is compulsory. Session II is a skill or typing test,
depending on the post. Sections 1, 2 and Module I of Section 3 carry **−1** per wrong answer. The notice has the
section-wise marks.

## Syllabus

Class 10–12 level: arithmetic and elementary maths, verbal and non-verbal reasoning, English grammar,
vocabulary and comprehension, static GK and current affairs, and basic computer knowledge for Tier 2.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare the four SSC modules once (quant, reasoning, English, GA). They are the same for CGL, CHSL, MTS and CPO.
2. Practise typing daily from the start if your target post has a typing or skill test.
3. Solve official answer keys and question papers from ssc.gov.in under time. The −0.50 and −1 penalties reward accuracy.
