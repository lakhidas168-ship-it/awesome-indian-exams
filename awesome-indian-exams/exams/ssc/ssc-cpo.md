# SSC CPO (Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs)

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

SSC CPO recruits Sub-Inspectors for Delhi Police and the Central Armed Police Forces (BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB).
Its Paper 1 subjects are the same four as SSC CGL Tier 1, at graduate level.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| Stages | Paper 1 (CBT) → PET/PST → Paper 2 (CBT, English) → medical examination |
| Qualification | Graduate degree |

## Official sources

- SSC official website (notices, syllabus, answer keys): <https://ssc.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**Paper 1:** computer-based, objective, in four sections: General Intelligence and Reasoning, General
Knowledge and General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English Comprehension.

**Paper 2:** computer-based, objective, English Language and Comprehension only.

Both papers have negative marking. The number of questions, time and penalty per wrong answer are in the current
notice. Sources disagree on the penalty, so it is not stated here until it is checked officially.

## Syllabus

Graduate-level reasoning, GK and current affairs, quantitative aptitude (arithmetic, algebra, geometry,
mensuration, trigonometry, data interpretation) and English (grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, cloze test).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with SSC CGL Tier 1: the subjects are the same.
2. Give Paper 2 English extra time: it is a full paper on its own.
3. Train for the PET early. It is qualifying but eliminates many candidates.
