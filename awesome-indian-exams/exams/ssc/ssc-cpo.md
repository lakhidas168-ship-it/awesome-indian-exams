# SSC CPO (Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs)

SSC CPO recruits Sub-Inspectors (Executive) in Delhi Police (male and female) and Sub-Inspectors (GD) in the
Central Armed Police Forces. Selection runs through two computer-based papers with physical tests and a medical
examination in between, and every stage is mandatory. All numbers below are from the SSC CPO 2025 notice.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| Stages | Paper 1 (CBT) → PST/PET → Paper 2 (CBT, English) → detailed medical examination |
| Qualification | Bachelor's degree from a recognized university (must be possessed on or before the closing date for applications) |
| Age | 20–25 years as on 01.08.2025 (born 02.08.2000–01.08.2005; relaxations for reserved categories as per the notice) |

## Exam pattern

**Paper 1:** computer-based, 200 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours, with a 30-minute sectional timer for each of the
four parts below. Parts 1–3 are set in Hindi and English. **−0.25** per wrong answer.

| Part | Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| I | General Intelligence and Reasoning | 50 | 50 |
| II | General Knowledge and General Awareness | 50 | 50 |
| III | Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 |
| IV | English Comprehension | 50 | 50 |

Minimum qualifying marks in Paper 1 (before NCC bonus): UR 30%, OBC/EWS 25%, all other categories 20%.
Candidates who clear Paper 1 are shortlisted for the PST/PET, which is qualifying.

**Paper 2:** computer-based, English Language and Comprehension only, 200 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours, in four
parts with a 30-minute sectional timer for each part. **−0.25** per wrong answer. The same minimum qualifying
marks (UR 30%, OBC/EWS 25%, others 20%) apply.

Scores are normalized if the CBT is held in multiple shifts. NCC certificate holders get bonus marks in each
paper: 10 for a 'C' certificate, 6 for 'B', 4 for 'A'. There is no re-evaluation of scores.

## Syllabus

- **General Intelligence and Reasoning:** verbal and non-verbal reasoning — analogies, classification, series,
  coding-decoding, syllogisms, statement-conclusion, space visualization, Venn diagrams, critical thinking.
- **General Knowledge and General Awareness:** current events, everyday science, and India and its neighbours —
  history, culture, geography, economy, polity, the Constitution, scientific research.
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** whole numbers, decimals and fractions, percentage, ratio and proportion, averages,
  interest, profit and loss, discount, time and work, time and distance, algebra, geometry, mensuration,
  trigonometry, data interpretation.
- **English (Paper 1 comprehension and Paper 2):** error recognition, fill in the blanks, vocabulary, spellings,
  grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, idioms, comprehension. Full topic lists are in paras 12.8.1
  and 12.8.2 of the notice linked above.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with SSC CGL Tier 1: the subjects are the same.
2. Give Paper 2 English extra time: it is a full 200-mark paper on its own.
3. Train for the PET early. It is qualifying but eliminates many candidates.
