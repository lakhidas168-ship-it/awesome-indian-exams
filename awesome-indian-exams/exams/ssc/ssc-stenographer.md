# SSC Stenographer (Grade C and D)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

SSC Stenographer recruits Grade C and Grade D stenographers for central ministries and departments.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| Stages | CBT (merit) → shorthand skill test (qualifying) |
| Qualification | Class 12 pass |

## Official sources

- SSC official website (notices, syllabus, answer keys): <https://ssc.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

**CBT:** 200 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours, **−0.25** per wrong answer, in three sections: General
Intelligence and Reasoning, General Awareness, and English Language and Comprehension (the English section is the
largest). **Skill test:** shorthand dictation at the speed set for each grade, transcribed on computer. It is
qualifying: final selection uses the CBT marks.

## Syllabus

Reasoning, general awareness, and English grammar, vocabulary and comprehension.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Shorthand takes months: practise dictation daily from the start.
2. English carries the most CBT questions.
