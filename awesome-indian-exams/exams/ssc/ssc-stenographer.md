## Exam pattern

**Computer Based Examination:** objective multiple-choice, set in English and Hindi, 200 questions,
200 marks, 2 hours with a sectional timer (30 minutes each for Part I and Part II, 60 minutes for
Part III). Candidates eligible for scribe get 2 hours 40 minutes (40 + 40 + 80). **−0.25** per wrong
answer. Scores are normalised across shifts where applicable. Minimum qualifying marks in the CBT:
30% (UR), 25% (OBC/EWS), 20% (all other categories).

| Part | Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| I | General Intelligence and Reasoning | 50 | 50 |
| II | General Awareness | 50 | 50 |
| III | English Language and Comprehension | 100 | 100 |

**Skill Test in Stenography:** only CBT-shortlisted candidates appear (category-wise, for the
post(s) applied for: Grade 'C', Grade 'D', or both). One 10-minute dictation in English or Hindi
(as opted in the application) at 100 words per minute for Grade 'C' and 80 words per minute for
Grade 'D', transcribed on computer. It is mandatory but qualifying: the Commission fixes
category-wise qualifying standards per post, and final selection is on CBT merit among those who
qualify the skill test.

| Post | Language | Transcription time | Scribe-eligible time |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Grade 'D' | English | 50 minutes | 70 minutes |
| Grade 'D' | Hindi | 65 minutes | 90 minutes |
| Grade 'C' | English | 40 minutes | 55 minutes |
| Grade 'C' | Hindi | 55 minutes | 75 minutes |

## Syllabus

- **General Intelligence and Reasoning:** verbal and non-verbal: analogies, similarities and differences, space visualisation, problem solving, analysis, judgement, decision making, visual memory, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning and number series, classification, abstract ideas and symbols.
- **General Awareness:** environment and society, current events, everyday observation and science as expected of an educated person, plus India and neighbouring countries (sports, history, culture, geography, economy, polity including the Constitution, scientific research). No maps/graphs/diagrams/statistics component for VH candidates with 40%+ visual disability.
- **English Language and Comprehension:** vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, correct usage, and writing ability.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Shorthand takes months: practise dictation daily from the start.
2. English carries the most CBT questions.
