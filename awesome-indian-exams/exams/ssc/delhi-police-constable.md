# Delhi Police Constable (Executive)

SSC conducts the recruitment of Constables (Executive) Male and Female for Delhi Police, under an MoU
between Delhi Police and SSC. Selection is through a Computer-Based Examination, then a qualifying
Physical Endurance and Measurement Test conducted by Delhi Police in Delhi, followed by medical
examination. Final merit is on CBE marks, subject to qualifying PE&MT.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Staff Selection Commission for Delhi Police |
| Stages | CBE → PE&MT (qualifying) → medical examination |
| Qualification | 10+2 (Senior Secondary) pass from a recognized Board as on 21-10-2025; relaxable to 11th pass for sons/daughters of Delhi Police personnel/MTS and for Delhi Police bandsmen/buglers/mounted constables/drivers/dispatch riders; male candidates must hold a valid LMV driving licence (not learner) as on the PE&MT date |
| Age | 18–25 years as on 01-07-2025 (born 02-07-2000 to 01-07-2007; relaxations for reserved/ESM/departmental/wards/sportspersons/widows as per notice) |

| Vacancies (2025 notice) | 7565 tentative (4408 Male + 285 Male Ex-Servicemen (Others) + 376 Male Ex-Servicemen (Commando) + 2496 Female) |
| Medium | English and Hindi only |

## Exam pattern

**CBE:** one objective-type multiple-choice paper, 100 questions, 100 marks, 90 minutes, **−0.25** per wrong
answer. Multi-shift scores are normalized per the Commission's 02-06-2025 formula for merit and cut-off.
Question paper is of Matriculation level.

| Part | Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| A | General Knowledge / Current Affairs | 50 | 50 |
| B | Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| C | Numerical Ability | 15 | 15 |
| D | Computer Fundamentals, MS Excel, MS Word, Communication, Internet, WWW and Web Browsers etc. | 10 | 10 |
| | Total (90 minutes, no sectional timing stated) | 100 | 100 |

PE&MT is qualifying only: race (1600 m), long jump, high jump, then height/chest measurement; standards
differ by sex and age group and are in paras 12.12–12.19 of the notice.

## Syllabus

As in para 12.10 of the 2025 notice (Matriculation level):

- **General Knowledge / Current Affairs:** general awareness, current events, everyday observation and
  experience in their scientific aspect; India and neighbouring countries, especially sports, history,
  culture, geography, Indian economy, general polity, Indian Constitution, scientific research. No special
  study of any discipline required.
- **Reasoning:** analytical aptitude, observing and distinguishing patterns, mainly non-verbal: analogies,
  similarities and differences, spatial visualization and orientation, visual memory, discrimination,
  observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasons, figural classification, arithmetic and
  non-verbal series, coding and decoding.
- **Numerical Ability:** number systems; whole numbers, decimals, fractions and relationships; fundamental
  arithmetical operations; percentages; ratio and proportion; averages; interest; profit and loss;
  discount; mensuration; time and distance; ratio and time; time and work.
- **Computer Fundamentals:** word processing basics; MS Excel (spreadsheet elements, cell editing,
  functions and formulas); communication and email basics; internet, WWW and web browsers (services,
  URL, HTTP, FTP, websites, blogs, browsing software, search engines, chat, video conferencing,
  e-banking).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. GK is half the paper: daily current affairs from PIB plus NCERT static GK (history, geography, polity,
   economy, science).
2. Reasoning (25) and arithmetic (15) next: practise from NCERT Class 6–10 maths and daily sets.
3. Computer fundamentals (10): NCERT/computer-awareness module plus hands-on MS Word/Excel, email and
   browser basics.
4. Mocks from official SSC question papers/answer keys on ssc.gov.in; the −0.25 penalty rewards accuracy.
5. Start the 1600 m running practice on day one — PE&MT is qualifying but eliminatory.
