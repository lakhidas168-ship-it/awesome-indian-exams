# AP EAPCET

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below comes from the AP EAPCET 2026 instruction booklets
> (Engineering, and Agriculture & Pharmacy) on the official CETS portal, fetched on 2026-09-29. Always read the
> current notice before you apply or pay a fee.

AP EAPCET (Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test) is Andhra Pradesh's single state entrance
for engineering, agriculture and pharmacy seats. For 2026 it is conducted by Jawaharlal Nehru Technological
University, Kakinada, on behalf of APSCHE.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | JNT University Kakinada, on behalf of APSCHE |
| Purpose | Admission to engineering, agriculture and pharmacy courses in Andhra Pradesh |
| Stages | Computer-based test → counselling and seat allotment |
| Mode | Computer-based test, online only |
| 2026 exam dates | Engineering: 12–15 May 2026 and 18 May 2026 · Agriculture & Pharmacy: 19–20 May 2026 |
| Sessions | Two a day, 9:00 am–12:00 noon and 2:00 pm–5:00 pm; no sessions on 16 and 17 May 2026. You cannot choose your date or session. |
| Medium | English and Telugu. Urdu-medium candidates who want translation help are allotted the Kurnool centre only. |
| Registration fee (2026) | Rs. 800 open category · Rs. 750 BC · Rs. 700 SC/ST |
| Eligibility | Intermediate (10+2) with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry (engineering) or the relevant group subjects for the agriculture/pharmacy courses; engineering Diploma holders also eligible; at least 45% taken together in the specified subjects (40% for reserved categories) |

## Exam pattern

One online (computer-based) paper: **3 hours**, **160 objective multiple-choice questions of one mark each**
(**160 marks**). There is **no negative marking**, and nothing is deducted for leaving a question blank.

| Stream | Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Engineering | Mathematics | 80 | 80 |
| Engineering | Physics | 40 | 40 |
| Engineering | Chemistry | 40 | 40 |
| Agriculture & Pharmacy | Biology (Botany 40 + Zoology 40) | 80 | 80 |
| Agriculture & Pharmacy | Physics | 40 | 40 |
| Agriculture & Pharmacy | Chemistry | 40 | 40 |

To qualify you need **25% of the maximum marks**; there is no minimum qualifying mark for SC/ST candidates.
Because the test runs in several sessions, scores are normalised, and the rank gives **75% weightage to the
normalised EAPCET marks and 25% to the 10+2 marks** in the relevant group subjects.

## Syllabus

The Andhra Pradesh intermediate (Class 11–12) syllabus, printed subject-wise in Annexure-I of each booklet:
Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry for engineering; Biology (Botany and Zoology), Physics and Chemistry for
agriculture and pharmacy. The stream-wise syllabus PDFs are linked from the EAPCET homepage.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Engineering:** Mathematics alone is half the paper (80 of 160). Finish the intermediate Maths syllabus
   first, then Physics, then Chemistry.
2. **Agriculture and pharmacy:** Biology is half the paper (40 Botany + 40 Zoology). Read NCERT Class 11–12
   Biology closely, then Physics and Chemistry.
3. There is no negative marking, so train to **attempt all 160 questions within 3 hours** — never leave a blank.
4. Take the free official mock test (Engineering and Agriculture & Pharmacy) on the EAPCET homepage to get used
   to the computer-based interface.
