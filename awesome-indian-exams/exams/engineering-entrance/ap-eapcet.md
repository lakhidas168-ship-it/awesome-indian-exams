# AP EAPCET

> **Evidence status: ✅ official.** Every number below was verified against the AP EAPCET 2026 instruction
> booklets (Engineering and Agriculture & Pharmacy streams) on 2026-09-27.

AP EAPCET is Andhra Pradesh's entrance for engineering, agriculture and pharmacy seats in the state. The 2026
exam was conducted by JNT University Kakinada on behalf of APSCHE.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | JNT University Kakinada on behalf of APSCHE |
| Stages | Computer-based test → counselling |
| 2026 exam dates | Engineering: 12-05-2026 to 15-05-2026 and 18-05-2026; Agriculture & Pharmacy: 19-05-2026 to 20-05-2026 (two sessions daily: 9.00 AM–12.00 noon and 2.00 PM–5.00 PM; no sessions on 16 and 17 May 2026) |
| Qualification | Intermediate (10+2) with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry (engineering), or relevant subjects for agriculture/pharmacy courses; engineering Diploma holders also eligible; at least 45% in the specified subjects taken together (40% for reserved categories) |
| Registration fee (2026) | Rs. 800 open category, Rs. 700 SC/ST, Rs. 750 BC |
| Medium | English and Telugu (Urdu-medium candidates get translation help at the Kurnool centre only) |

## Exam pattern

Online (computer-based) test, **3 hours**, **160 objective (multiple-choice) questions, 1 mark each, 160 marks**.
There is **no negative marking**, and no deduction for unattempted questions.

| Stream | Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Engineering | Mathematics | 80 | 80 |
| Engineering | Physics | 40 | 40 |
| Engineering | Chemistry | 40 | 40 |
| Agriculture & Pharmacy | Biology (Botany 40 + Zoology 40) | 80 | 80 |
| Agriculture & Pharmacy | Physics | 40 | 40 |
| Agriculture & Pharmacy | Chemistry | 40 | 40 |

Qualifying mark is **25%** of the maximum marks; there is no minimum qualifying mark for SC/ST candidates.
Ranks are computed with **75% weightage to the normalized EAPCET marks and 25% to the 10+2 marks**. Because the
test runs in multiple sessions, scores are normalized before ranking.

## Syllabus

The Andhra Pradesh intermediate (Class 11–12) syllabus, given subject-wise in Annexure-I of each instruction
booklet: Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry for engineering; Biology (Botany + Zoology), Physics and Chemistry
for agriculture and pharmacy. The stream-wise syllabus PDFs are linked from the EAPCET homepage above.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. For engineering, **Mathematics is half the paper (80 of 160)**. Finish the intermediate Maths syllabus first,
   then Physics, then Chemistry.
2. For agriculture/pharmacy, **Biology is half the paper (40 Botany + 40 Zoology)**. Read NCERT Class 11–12
   Biology line by line.
3. There is no negative marking, so practise to **attempt all 160 questions in 3 hours**; never leave blanks.
4. Take the official mock test on the EAPCET homepage to get used to the computer-based format.
