# AP EAPCET

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

AP EAPCET is Andhra Pradesh's entrance for engineering, agriculture and pharmacy seats in the state,
conducted by a state university for APSCHE.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | APSCHE (conducting university varies) |
| Stages | Computer-based test → counselling |

## Official sources

- Official website: <https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

A computer-based test in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry for engineering (Biology replaces Mathematics for
agriculture and pharmacy). The bulletin has the questions, time and marking.

## Syllabus

Andhra Pradesh intermediate (Class 11–12) syllabus, NCERT-aligned.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Mathematics carries the most weight for engineering.
