# MHT CET (PCM)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below was verified against the MHT-CET 2026 information
> brochure and the official syllabus and marking scheme on 2026-09-28. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

MHT CET is Maharashtra's entrance for engineering, pharmacy and agriculture seats in the state. This page
covers the PCM group.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | State CET Cell, Maharashtra |
| PCM paper | 150 questions, 200 marks, 180 minutes, computer-based |
| Negative marking | None |
| Who can appear | Indian nationals who have passed or are appearing in HSC/12th or equivalent |
| Age limit | None |
| Attempts | Two; the better total percentile counts |

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Questions | Marks per question | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mathematics (10 from Class 11 + 40 from Class 12) | 50 | 2 | 100 | 90 min |
| Physics + Chemistry (10 + 40 each) | 100 | 1 | 100 | 90 min |

About 20% of the questions come from the Class 11 syllabus and 80% from Class 12. There is no negative marking.

The 180 minutes run in two fixed halves: the first 90 minutes cover Physics and Chemistry (auto-submitted),
then 90 minutes for Mathematics. Each question has four options with one correct answer.

## Syllabus

SCERT Maharashtra Std XI–XII syllabus: the whole Std XII syllabus plus the selected Std XI chapters listed
in the official syllabus document. Questions are mainly application-based, with difficulty at par with JEE
Main for the PCM subjects.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. With no negative marking, attempt every question.
2. Class 12 carries about 80% of the marks: prioritise it.
3. Practise application-based MCQs at JEE Main level for Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry.
