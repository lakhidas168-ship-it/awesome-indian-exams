# JEE Main

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

JEE Main is the national entrance test for B.E./B.Tech at NITs, IIITs and other centrally funded institutes, and
the qualifying test for JEE Advanced (IITs). It is held in two sessions a year. Paper 1 is for B.E./B.Tech; Papers 2A
and 2B are for B.Arch and B.Planning.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency (NTA) |
| Sessions | Two per year (January and April); the better score counts |
| Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) | 75 questions, 300 marks, 3 hours, computer-based |

## Official sources

- JEE Main official website (information bulletin): <https://jeemain.nta.nic.in>

## Exam pattern

**Paper 1:** Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, 25 questions each, **all compulsory**.

| Per subject | Questions | Type |
|---|---:|---|
| Section A | 20 | Multiple choice |
| Section B | 5 | Numerical value |

Marking: **+4** correct, **−1** wrong, for both MCQs and numerical questions in the 2026 pattern (numerical questions
used to have no negative marking). Total 300 marks.

## Syllabus

NCERT Class 11–12 Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. NTA publishes the exact chapter list in the information
bulletin.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. NCERT Class 11–12 first: most questions are NCERT concepts, and the chemistry is close to NCERT wording.
2. Then NCERT exemplar problems for depth, and timed full papers in the last months.
3. Numerical questions now carry −1: treat them like MCQs and skip guesses.
