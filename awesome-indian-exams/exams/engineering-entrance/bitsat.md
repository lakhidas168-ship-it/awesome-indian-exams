# BITSAT

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

BITSAT is BITS Pilani's own entrance test for its Pilani, Goa and Hyderabad campuses. It is known for its speed
and its bonus questions.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS) Pilani |
| Mode | Computer-based, 3 hours, 130 questions, 390 marks |
| Sessions | Usually two; the better score counts |

## Official sources

- BITS admissions (official): <https://www.bitsadmission.com>

## Exam pattern

**+3** correct, **−1** wrong. No sectional time limits.

| Part | Questions |
|---|---:|
| Physics | 30 |
| Chemistry | 30 |
| English Proficiency | 10 |
| Logical Reasoning | 20 |
| Mathematics (or Biology for B.Pharm) | 40 |

Candidates who answer all 130 questions with time left can attempt **12 bonus questions**. After that, the first 130
answers are locked.

## Syllabus

NCERT Class 11–12 PCM (or PCB), plus English proficiency and logical reasoning.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Speed matters most: 130 questions in 180 minutes.
2. Use the English and reasoning modules: they are easy marks next to PCM.
