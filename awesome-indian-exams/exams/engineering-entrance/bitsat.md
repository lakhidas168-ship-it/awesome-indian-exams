# BITSAT

BITSAT is BITS Pilani's own computer-based entrance test for its Integrated First Degree programmes (B.E.,
M.Sc., B.Pharm.) at the Pilani, K K Birla Goa and Hyderabad campuses. It is known for its speed and its
bonus questions.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS) Pilani |
| Mode | Computer-based, 3 hours, 130 questions, 390 marks |
| Sessions | Two (Session-1, Session-2); a candidate may appear at most twice and the higher score counts |
| Eligibility (Class 12) | 75% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (or Biology for B.Pharm, B.E. Environmental and Sustainability, M.Sc. Biological Sciences), with at least 60% in each; only 2025/2026 pass-outs (see brochure for full conditions) |

## Exam pattern

**+3** correct, **−1** wrong. No sectional time limits; un-attempted questions score zero.

| Part | Questions |
|---|---:|
| Physics | 30 |
| Chemistry | 30 |
| English Proficiency | 10 |
| Logical Reasoning | 20 |
| Mathematics (all B.E./M.Sc./B.Pharm) or Biology (B.Pharm, B.E. Environmental and Sustainability, M.Sc. Biological Sciences) | 40 |

Candidates who answer all 130 questions with time left can attempt **12 extra questions** (three each from
Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology and Logical Reasoning). Once extra questions are opted for, the
first 130 answers are locked and cannot be corrected.

## Syllabus

NCERT Class 11–12 PCM (or PCB for the programmes above), plus English proficiency and logical reasoning.
The official BITSAT-2026 syllabus (Annexure-1 of the brochure) lists the full topic-wise break-up; the
brochure advises NCERT textbooks for its contents.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Speed matters most: 130 questions in 180 minutes.
2. Use the English and reasoning modules: they are easy marks next to PCM.
