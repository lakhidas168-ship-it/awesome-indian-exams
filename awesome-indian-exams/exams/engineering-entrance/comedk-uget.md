# COMEDK UGET

> **Evidence status: ✅ official.** Every number below is from the COMEDK UGET 2026 Information Brochure
> (notified 03 Feb 2026), fetched from comedk.org on 2026-09-28.

COMEDK UGET is the entrance test for B.E. seats in the engineering colleges participating in the COMEDK
process (around 150 colleges). Its scores are valid for admissions in the academic year 2026-27 only.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) |
| Exam date | Saturday, 09 May 2026 |
| Mode | Computer-based test (CBT), English medium; session and slot are on the Test Admission Ticket |
| Total | 180 MCQs, 180 marks (Physics 60, Chemistry 60, Mathematics 60) |
| Negative marking | None; 1 mark per correct answer |
| Qualification | 2nd PUC or 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, English and Chemistry (or Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science or Electronics); 45% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (40% for SC, ST and OBC candidates of Karnataka); pass each subject individually; all subjects from one board |
| Application fee | Rs 1,950 + transaction charges (COMEDK UGET only) |

## Exam pattern

One computer-based paper of 180 one-mark multiple-choice questions, with no negative marking:

| Subject | Questions |
|---|---:|
| Physics | 60 |
| Chemistry | 60 |
| Mathematics | 60 |
| Total | 180 questions, 180 marks |

The medium is English only. The merit rank is generated from the entrance-test score only. The exam may run
in multiple sessions with different question sets; scores are then expressed as percentiles normalized across
sessions. Diploma candidates are not eligible (no lateral entry), and a candidate scoring zero is assigned no
rank.

## Syllabus

The test follows the CBSE syllabus: about 33% from Class 11 (2024-25) and about 67% from Class 12
(2025-26). The unit-wise detail is in COMEDK's official syllabus PDFs linked under Official sources above
(Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Master Class 11-12 Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics from the NCERT textbooks (the CBSE base of the
   paper), giving extra weight to Class 12 topics.
2. Since there is no negative marking, practise to attempt all 180 questions: timed MCQ sets, accuracy first,
   then speed.
3. Use COMEDK's official mock tests (available in candidate login after successful payment) to get used to the
   computer-based format.
