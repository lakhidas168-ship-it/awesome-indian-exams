# Karnataka CET (KCET)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from the KEA UGCET-2026 information bulletin and
> exam schedule, fetched on 2026-09-28 (both PDFs are in Kannada). Always read the current notice before you
> apply or pay a fee.

KCET is Karnataka's entrance for engineering, agriculture, pharmacy and other professional courses in the
state. Horanadu and Gadinadu Kannadiga candidates also take a Kannada language test.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) |
| KCET 2026 dates | 23 April 2026 (Physics, Chemistry) and 24 April 2026 (Mathematics, Biology); Kannada test on 22 April 2026 |
| Mode | Pen and paper (OMR sheets) |
| Per subject | 60 questions, 60 marks (1 mark each), 80 minutes |
| Negative marking | None (marking more than one answer gets zero for that question) |

## Exam pattern

Separate papers for Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology: each 60 multiple-choice questions, 60 marks,
80 minutes (morning paper 10:30–11:50, afternoon paper 2:30–3:50), one mark per question. There is no negative
marking; if you mark more than one answer to a question, that question gets zero. Answers are recorded on OMR
sheets. Engineering (B.E./B.Tech) uses Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics; the merit takes the average of the
CET marks and the qualifying-examination marks in those subjects (see section 10(A) of the bulletin). The
Kannada language test for Horanadu/Gadinadu candidates is 50 marks (10:30–11:30), with a minimum of 12 marks
to qualify.

## Syllabus

The I and II PUC (PCMB) syllabus prescribed for the CET — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for engineering;
Biology instead of Mathematics for other courses, as per the bulletin.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. No negative marking: answer every question.
2. Board marks also count towards the engineering rank, so don't neglect Class 12.
3. Practise marking OMR sheets under the 80-minute limit before the exam.
