# Karnataka CET (KCET)

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Verified against KEA official portal (2024/2025 cycle).

KCET is Karnataka's entrance for engineering, agriculture, pharmacy and other professional courses in the
state.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) |
| Mode | Pen and paper (OMR sheets) |
| Subjects | Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (for Engineering) |
| Per subject | 60 questions, 60 marks (1 mark each), 80 minutes |
| Negative marking | None |

## Official sources

- KEA UGCET portal (notices, bulletins): <https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/ugcet2024>

## Exam pattern

The engineering entrance consists of three papers: Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Each paper contains 60 multiple-choice questions, carrying 60 marks in total (1 mark per question). The duration for each paper is 80 minutes. There is no negative marking for incorrect answers.

## Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the I and II PUC (Pre-University Course) curriculum prescribed by the Department of Pre-University Education, Karnataka.
- **Physics:** Topics from Class 11 and 12 NCERT/PUC syllabus.
- **Chemistry:** Topics from Class 11 and 12 NCERT/PUC syllabus.
- **Mathematics:** Topics from Class 11 and 12 NCERT/PUC syllabus.
