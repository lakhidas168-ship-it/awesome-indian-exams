# MAH MBA/MMS CET

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from the MAH-MBA/MMS CET 2026 Information
> Brochure (for Academic Year 2026-27), fetched from cetcell.mahacet.org on 2026-09-27.

MAH MBA/MMS CET is Maharashtra's online entrance for first-year MBA/MMS seats in the state's management
institutes, including government institutes, university departments and unaided institutes covered by the
2015 Act. Admission after the test runs through the Centralised Admission Process (CAP).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | State CET Cell, Maharashtra (Commissioner, State CET Cell is the Competent Authority) |
| Mode | Online computer-based test, in English; 150 minutes, no sectional time limit |
| Total | 200 questions, 200 marks, no negative marking |
| 2026 cycle (per brochure) | Online registration 10-Jan-2026 to 12-Feb-2026 (payment up to 13-Feb-2026); admit card and exam dates notified later on the official site |
| Qualification | 3-year bachelor's degree in any discipline (final-year candidates may appear); 50% aggregate, 45% for backward-class / EWS / PwD candidates of Maharashtra State only |
| Result | Percentile from raw marks across shifts; candidates appearing in both attempts count the better percentile |

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| Logical Reasoning | 75 |
| Abstract Reasoning | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 |
| Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension | 50 |

One mark per question, no negative marking. Each question has five options. The test is in English only,
and any question may be attempted at any point within the 150 minutes. Candidates may have to be at the
venue for about 4 hours including verification and login formalities.

## Syllabus

Per the brochure, three content heads (section topics in our own words):

- Logical / Abstract Reasoning (100 marks): how quickly and accurately you think; questions based on
  figures and diagrams plus verbal reasoning.
- Quantitative Aptitude (50 marks): working with numbers and numerical calculation; arithmetic problems
  involving ratio and proportion, percentage and similar topics; quantitative reasoning and interpretation
  of tables, common graphs and charts.
- Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (50 marks): passages with questions on their contents;
  grammar, vocabulary, sentence completion, synonyms, antonyms and comprehension of passages.
