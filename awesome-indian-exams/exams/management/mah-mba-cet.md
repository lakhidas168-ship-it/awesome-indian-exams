# MAH MBA/MMS CET

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

MAH MBA CET is Maharashtra's entrance for MBA/MMS seats in the state, including well-known Mumbai colleges.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | State CET Cell, Maharashtra |
| Mode | Computer-based, 150 minutes, no sectional time limit |
| Total | 200 questions, 200 marks, no negative marking |

## Official sources

- State CET Cell, Maharashtra (official): <https://cetcell.mahacet.org>

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions |
|---|---:|
| Logical Reasoning | 75 |
| Abstract Reasoning | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 |
| Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension | 50 |

One mark per question, no negative marking.

## Syllabus

Reasoning-heavy aptitude: logical, abstract, quantitative and verbal.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. 200 questions in 150 minutes with no penalty: speed and attempting everything decide the score.
2. Half the paper is reasoning.
