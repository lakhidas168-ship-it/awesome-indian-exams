# NMAT by GMAC

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

NMAT by GMAC is the entrance for MBA/PGDM programmes at NMIMS and several other Indian and overseas business
schools. It is run by GMAC, the council that also owns the GMAT. Candidates book their own test slot inside a long
test window and may retake the test.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | GMAC (Graduate Management Admission Council) |
| Mode | Computer-based, at a test centre or online |
| Paper | 108 questions in 120 minutes, three timed sections |
| Negative marking | None reported |
| Attempts | Up to three in a cycle (one test and two retakes), as reported |
| Registration | Opened 20 August 2026; last date reported as 10 October 2026 |

## Official sources

- NMAT on mba.com (registration, handbook, official practice): <https://www.mba.com/exams/nmat>
- GMAC's NMAT information page: <https://www.gmac.com/resources/learners/how-to-apply/exams-preparation/nmat-exam>

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Language skills | 36 | 28 minutes |
| Quantitative skills | 36 | 52 minutes |
| Logical reasoning | 36 | 40 minutes |

Candidates choose the order of the sections before they start. Each section has its own time limit. Scores are scaled;
check the current NMAT handbook for the score range and the retake rules.

## Syllabus

- **Language skills:** reading comprehension, sentence correction, fill in the blanks, para-jumbles, vocabulary.
- **Quantitative skills:** arithmetic, number properties, algebra, probability, data interpretation, data sufficiency.
- **Logical reasoning:** critical reasoning, analytical puzzles, deductions, verbal reasoning.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Take the free official practice test on mba.com first, to see the interface and your weak section.
2. Quant has the most time per question: build speed there with the shared quant and data interpretation modules.
3. Use the section-order choice: start with your strongest section.
4. Plan the retakes: book the first attempt early in the window so two retakes still fit.
