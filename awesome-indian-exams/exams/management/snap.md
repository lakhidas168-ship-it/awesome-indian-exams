# SNAP

> **Evidence status: 🟢 official.** Every number below is from the SNAP 2026 test-structure page,
> eligibility page, homepage and the SNAP 2026 Bulletin PDF on snaptest.org, all fetched on 2026-09-28.

SNAP (Symbiosis National Aptitude Test) is the computer-based entrance for admission to the MBA
programmes of Symbiosis International (Deemed University). One paper, 60 questions in 60 minutes,
with up to three attempts and the best score counting.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Symbiosis International (Deemed University) |
| Mode | Computer-based test (CBT), 60 minutes, 60 questions, 60 marks |
| 2026 cycle (per bulletin) | Registration and payment close 25.11.2026; tests on 13.12.2026, 19.12.2026 and 26.12.2026; result 12.01.2027 |
| Attempts | Up to three tests; the higher (best) score counts, with no normalisation of scores |
| Qualification | Graduate in any discipline from a recognised university with at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST); some programmes carry extra criteria — see the notification |
| Fees (per bulletin/site) | SNAP test registration ₹2,550 per test; programme registration ₹1,000 per programme; government taxes extra; both fees mandatory and non-refundable |
| After the test | Shortlisted candidates attend the Group Exercise and Personal Interaction (GE-PI) run separately by each institute |

## Exam pattern

Objective test; each question has four responses. **+1** per correct answer, **25% negative marks**
(**−0.25**) per wrong answer. Sections may be attempted in any order.

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| General English | 10 | 10 |
| Analytical and Logical Reasoning | 20 | 20 |
| Quantitative, Data Interpretation and Data Sufficiency | 20 | 20 |
| Ethics, Morality and Values | 10 | 10 |
| Total | 60 | 60 |

Duration is 60 minutes (compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour for PwD candidates). Timings of each
test are printed on the admit card. Admit cards are available on snaptest.org from 07.12.2026 (Test 1),
11.12.2026 (Test 2) and 18.12.2026 (Test 3).

## Syllabus

SIU publishes the four section names above rather than a detailed topic-wise syllabus. In the
bulletin's words, schedules and the nature/structure of the test can change at SIU's discretion, so
treat the test-structure page as the syllabus authority. Roughly: English usage and comprehension;
analytical and logical reasoning; arithmetic, data interpretation and data sufficiency; and the
Ethics, Morality and Values section (new for 2026, with official preparation material on
snaptest.org).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Speed first: 60 questions in 60 minutes means one question a minute — drill timed sets.
2. Cover the three shared modules below (English, reasoning, quant/DI) from free sources.
3. Use the official EMV preparation material on snaptest.org for the Ethics, Morality and Values section.
4. Revise with the admit-card instructions (photo ID in original, photo pasted on the card) so test day holds no surprises.
