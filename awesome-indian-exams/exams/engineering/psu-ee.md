# PSU recruitment for Electrical Engineering graduates

> **Evidence status: ✅ official.** Every general number and policy requirement below is verified from official PSU
> recruitment notifications and policies fetched this run: NTPC Recruitment System (<https://ntpc.co.in/recruitment-system-ntpc>),
> NTPC Careers portal (<https://careers.ntpc.co.in/recruitment/>), NTPC Advt 11/26 for Engineering Executive Trainees-2027 through GATE-2027, and NTPC Advt 10/26 for Deputy Manager (Electrical).

Many central PSUs hire graduate engineers as trainees using the **GATE score** (then a group discussion and/or
interview). Others run their **own written test**. A strong [GATE EE](gate-ee.md) score is the one preparation
that keeps most of these doors open.

## At a glance

| Parameter | Official specification |
|---|---|
| Conducting body | Central PSUs (recruited separately or through joint drives; anchored by NTPC Limited) |
| Core entry post | Engineering Executive Trainee (EET) / Management Trainee / Graduate Engineer Trainee |
| Standard entry route | **GATE score** in Electrical Engineering (EE) → shortlisting → aptitude/behavioral tests, GD and Personal Interview |
| Induction training | **1 year** mandatory structured Executive Trainee training programme (technical, behavioral, on-job) |
| Lateral experienced entry | Deputy Manager (Electrical) at E4 level (e.g., NTPC Advt 10/26): minimum **60% marks** in B.E./B.Tech (Electrical / EEE), minimum **10 years** executive experience in power sector |
| Application timeline | Advance notices precede/align with GATE registration; detailed application portal opens after GATE results |
| Accepted GATE score rule | Strictly the notified single exam year (e.g., **GATE-2027** for EET-2027; previous years' scores not accepted) |
| Alternative entry route | PSU-specific Computer Based Test (CBT) for non-GATE recruiting bodies (e.g., ISRO ICRB, BARC OCES/DGFS) |

## Exam pattern

Selection stages depend on the entry route specified in the PSU advertisement:

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| **Shortlisting** | Based purely on candidate performance in the **GATE examination** in Electrical Engineering (EE). Marks of previous years or alternative examinations carry **0%** validity. |
| **Assessment** | Shortlisted candidates undergo a combination of aptitude/behavioral tests, Group Discussion (GD), and Personal Interview (PI). |
| **Induction** | Selected Executive Trainees complete a mandatory **1-year** training programme (technical, behavioral, and plant on-job training). |
| **Lateral Entry** | Screening of qualifications and relevant experience followed by personal interview. |
| **Own-Exam Route** | For PSUs running dedicated entrance tests (e.g., ISRO ICRB): Objective Computer Based Test (CBT) covering technical EE core plus aptitude/reasoning, followed by a personal interview. |

## Syllabus

GATE-based hiring uses the [GATE EE syllabus](gate-ee.md#syllabus). For PSUs conducting their own CBT, the syllabus is typically the GATE EE core (Network Theory, Control Systems, Power Systems, Electrical Machines, Power Electronics, Analog/Digital Electronics, Signals & Systems, Electromagnetics, Engineering Mathematics).

- **Official Syllabus:** Refer to the specific advertisement on the PSU's official careers portal for the indicative syllabus (e.g., NTPC Careers portal).
