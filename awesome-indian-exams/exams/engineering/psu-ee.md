> Rows in the PSU table marked *checked 29.09.2026* were re-fetched from their official portals on 2026-09-29.

Many central PSUs hire graduate engineers as trainees using the **GATE score** (then a group discussion and/or
interview). Others run their **own written test**. A strong [GATE EE](gate-ee.md) score is the one preparation
that keeps most of these doors open.

## Exam pattern

Selection stages depend on the entry route specified in the PSU advertisement:

- **GATE-based recruitment (standard PSU ET scheme, e.g. NTPC EET):**
  - **Stage 1 (Shortlisting):** Shortlisting is based purely on candidate performance in the **GATE examination** in Electrical Engineering (EE). Marks of previous years or alternative examinations carry **0%** validity (strictly one designated GATE year per advertisement, e.g. GATE-2027 for Advt 11/26).
  - **Stage 2 (Assessment & Selection):** Shortlisted candidates undergo a combination of aptitude/behavioral tests, Group Discussion (GD), and Personal Interview (PI), followed by a pre-employment medical examination.
  - **Induction Training:** Selected Executive Trainees complete a mandatory **1-year** training programme (technical, behavioral, and plant on-job training) before regular placement.
- **Experienced lateral entry (e.g. NTPC Advt 10/26 benchmark):**
  - **Eligibility & Quota:** **40 posts** for Deputy Manager (Electrical) at E4 level. Requires B.E./B.Tech in Electrical / Electrical & Electronics with minimum **60% marks** and minimum **10 years** post-qualification executive experience in the power sector.
  - **Selection:** Screening of qualifications and relevant experience followed by personal interview.
- **PSU own-examination route:**
  - For PSUs/bodies running dedicated entrance tests (such as ISRO ICRB or BARC OCES/DGFS): An objective Computer Based Test (CBT) covering technical EE core plus aptitude/reasoning, followed by a personal interview.
