# NHPC Junior Engineer (Electrical)

> **Evidence status: ⚠️ secondary.** The official website (nhpcindia.com) is currently unreachable for direct document retrieval. This page is maintained based on previously verified information.

NHPC Limited is a Government of India hydropower PSU. It recruits Junior Engineers in the Electrical, Civil,
Mechanical and E&C disciplines through its own Computer-Based Test (CBT).

## Exam pattern

The selection test is a single objective Computer-Based Test (CBT): **200 questions carrying 200 marks**, held
over **180 minutes (3 hours)**.

| Part | Subject | Questions |
|---|---|---:|
| I | Concerned discipline (Electrical Engineering) | 140 |
| II | General Awareness | 30 |
| III | Reasoning | 30 |
| **Total** | | **200** |

- **Marking:** one mark for every correct MCQ; a **negative marking of ¼ mark (0.25)** is deducted for every
  wrong MCQ attempted; no marks are awarded or deducted for unattempted questions.
- **Qualifying marks:** 40% of total marks for General/OBC/Gen-EWS and 35% for SC/ST/PwBD.

## Syllabus

The advertisement prescribes the qualification and the paper pattern but does not publish a topic-wise syllabus.
The 140 discipline questions are at the level of the three-year Diploma in Electrical Engineering, so use the
shared [Electrical engineering core module](../../modules/ee-core.md) as the study map. The other 60 questions are
split equally between:

- [General Awareness](../../modules/general-awareness.md) — current affairs, history, geography, polity and general science.
- [Reasoning](../../modules/reasoning.md) — verbal and non-verbal reasoning.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. **Know the paper:** 200 MCQs in 180 minutes with −0.25 per wrong answer, so accuracy matters as much as speed.
2. **Discipline first (140 marks):** work through the [EE core module](../../modules/ee-core.md) at diploma level —
   circuits, electrical machines, power systems, measurement and basic electronics.
3. **General Awareness (30 marks):** follow the [General Awareness module](../../modules/general-awareness.md).
4. **Reasoning (30 marks):** practise the [Reasoning module](../../modules/reasoning.md) for speed and accuracy.
5. **Mock the pattern:** attempt a full 200-question paper in 180 minutes before the exam.
