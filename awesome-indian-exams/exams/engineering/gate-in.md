# GATE Instrumentation Engineering (IN)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

GATE Instrumentation Engineering decides M.Tech/ME/PhD admission and is the recruitment filter for many PSUs. It overlaps heavily with GATE EE and EC.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | IISc Bengaluru and seven IITs for NCB-GATE (IIT Madras organises GATE 2027) |
| Exam dates (announced) | 6, 7, 13, 14, 20 and 21 February 2027; paper-wise schedule on the official site |
| Who can apply | Students in the 3rd year or higher of a UG programme, and graduates; no age limit |
| Score validity | 3 years |

## Official sources

- GATE 2027 official site (IIT Madras): <https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in>

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| General Aptitude (common to all papers) | 10 | 15 |
| Engineering Mathematics + Instrumentation Engineering | 55 | 85 |
| **Total** | **65** | **100** |

- Question types: **MCQ** (one correct), **MSQ** (one or more correct, no partial credit), **NAT** (numerical).
- Negative marking for **MCQ only**: −1/3 (1-mark) and −2/3 (2-mark). MSQ and NAT have none.
- 3 hours, computer-based, virtual scientific calculator only.

## Syllabus

Sections (a topic summary; the official syllabus PDF is the authority):

1. **Engineering Mathematics.**
2. **Electrical Circuits and Machines.**
3. **Signals and Systems.**
4. **Control Systems.**
5. **Analog Electronics.**
6. **Digital Electronics.**
7. **Measurements.**
8. **Sensors and Industrial Instrumentation:** transducers for displacement, velocity, force, pressure, flow, temperature, level.
9. **Communication and Optical Instrumentation.**

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with the GATE EE core, then add sensors and optical instrumentation.
2. Solve the official papers of past years (the organising IIT's site publishes them with answer keys).
