# GATE Instrumentation Engineering (IN)

GATE Instrumentation Engineering decides M.Tech/ME/PhD admission and is the recruitment filter for many PSUs. It overlaps heavily with GATE EE and EC.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | IISc Bengaluru and seven IITs for NCB-GATE (IIT Madras organises GATE 2027) |
| Exam dates (announced) | 6, 7, 13, 14, 20 and 21 February 2027; paper-wise schedule on the official site |
| Who can apply | Students in the 3rd year or higher of a UG programme, and graduates; no age limit |
| Score validity | 3 years |

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| General Aptitude (common to all papers) | 10 | 15 |
| Subject: paper-specific Engineering Mathematics (about 13 marks) + Instrumentation Engineering | 55 | 85 |
| **Total** | **65** | **100** |

- Question types: **MCQ** (one correct), **MSQ** (one or more correct, no partial credit), **NAT** (numerical).
  Questions carry 1 or 2 marks.
- Negative marking for **MCQ only**: −1/3 (1-mark) and −2/3 (2-mark). MSQ and NAT have none.
- 3 hours, computer-based, in English; virtual scientific calculator on screen only (physical calculators banned).

## Syllabus

Sections (a topic summary; the official syllabus PDF is the authority):

1. **Engineering Mathematics:** linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, complex variables, probability and statistics, numerical methods.
2. **Electricity and Magnetism:** Coulomb's and Gauss's laws, capacitance, Biot-Savart and Ampere's laws, Faraday's law, inductance, magnetic circuits, Maxwell's equations.
3. **Electrical Circuits and Machines:** network theorems, transient and phasor analysis, resonance, filters, two-port networks; transformers, synchronous machines, buck/boost converters, rectifiers, inverters, PWM control.
4. **Signals and Systems:** sampling, Fourier series, Laplace, Fourier and z-transforms, DFT/FFT, LTI systems, convolution and correlation, IIR/FIR basics.
5. **Control Systems:** feedback, signal flow graphs, Bode plots, Routh/Nyquist stability, root loci, state-space; actuators, P/PI/PID and cascade/feedforward/ratio controllers, lead-lag compensators, DCS and PLC basics.
6. **Analog Electronics:** diodes, BJT/MOSFET circuits, op-amp applications, noise and interference, shielding and grounding.
7. **Digital Electronics:** combinational and sequential logic, IC families, ADC/DAC types and specs, embedded systems and microcontrollers, data acquisition, IoT basics.
8. **Measurements:** SI units, errors and uncertainty, bridges, V/I/power measurement, instrument transformers, DVM/DMM, DSO, spectrum analyzer.
9. **Sensors and Industrial Instrumentation:** resistive, capacitive, inductive, piezoelectric, electromagnetic and Hall-effect sensors with signal conditioning; transducers for displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, torque, vibration, shock, pressure, flow, temperature, level, pH, conductivity and viscosity.
10. **Communication and Optical Instrumentation:** AM/FM, PCM, multiplexing, digital modulation (ASK/PSK/FSK/QAM), transmitter/receiver architectures, 4-20 mA loops; optical sources and detectors, interferometry, fiber-optic sensing, NIR/UV-VIS spectrophotometry.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare with the GATE EE core, then add sensors and optical instrumentation.
2. Solve the official papers of past years (the organising IIT's site publishes them with answer keys).
