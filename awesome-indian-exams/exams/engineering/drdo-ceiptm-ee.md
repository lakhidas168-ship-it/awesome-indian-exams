# DRDO CEPTAM (Electrical Engineering)

> **Evidence status: secondary.** The official DRDO recruitment portal was fetched on 2026-09-29. While the portal is official, no specific CEPTAM recruitment notification for Electrical Engineering was active at the time of verification.

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) through its Centre for Personnel Talent Management (CEPTAM) recruits Senior Technical Assistant (STA-B) and Technician (Tech-A) posts for engineering diploma and ITI holders.

## At a glance

| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Conducting Body** | Centre for Personnel Talent Management (CEPTAM), DRDO |
| **Exam Level** | National |
| **Stages** | Tier I (Screening) · Tier II (Selection / Trade Test) |
| **Eligibility** | Diploma / Degree / ITI in respective discipline as notified |
| **Official Portal** | <https://www.drdo.gov.in> |

## Official sources

- DRDO vacancies portal: <https://drdo.gov.in/drdo/en/offerings/vacancies>

## Exam pattern

The DRDO CEPTAM recruitment exam generally comprises:

- **Tier I (Screening):** Objective/CBT covering Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, General Awareness, General Science, and English/General Engineering (as per notification).
- **Tier II (Selection):** Trade-specific test (Electrical Engineering).

*Note: Please refer to the specific recruitment advertisement for exact question counts, marks, time duration, and negative marking rules.*

## Syllabus

- **Quantitative Aptitude & Reasoning:** Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, logical reasoning.
- **General Awareness & Science:** Current affairs, general science, history, geography.
- **Technical (Electrical):** Core Electrical Engineering subjects ([EE Core](../../modules/ee-core.md)).
