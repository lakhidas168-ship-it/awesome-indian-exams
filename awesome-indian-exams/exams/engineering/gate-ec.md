# GATE Electronics and Communication (EC)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

GATE Electronics and Communication decides M.Tech/ME/PhD admission and is the recruitment filter for many PSUs. Many electronics and telecom PSU and research posts use it.

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| General Aptitude (common to all papers) | 10 | 15 |
| Engineering Mathematics + Electronics and Communication | 55 | 85 |
| **Total** | **65** | **100** |

- Question types: **MCQ** (one correct), **MSQ** (one or more correct, no partial credit), **NAT** (numerical).
- Negative marking for **MCQ only**: −1/3 (1-mark) and −2/3 (2-mark). MSQ and NAT have none.
- 3 hours, computer-based, virtual scientific calculator only.

## Syllabus

Sections (a topic summary; the official syllabus PDF is the authority):

1. **Engineering Mathematics:** linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, vector analysis, complex analysis, probability and statistics.
2. **Networks, Signals and Systems:** circuit analysis, two-port networks, LTI systems, Fourier/Laplace/z transforms, sampling.
3. **Electronic Devices:** semiconductor physics, junctions, BJT, MOSFET, device fabrication basics.
4. **Analog Circuits:** diode circuits, amplifiers, op-amps, oscillators, filters.
5. **Digital Circuits:** number systems, combinational and sequential logic, data converters, memories, computer organisation basics.
6. **Control Systems:** transfer functions, stability, frequency response, compensators, state-space.
7. **Communications:** random processes, analog and digital modulation, information theory, error control.
8. **Electromagnetics:** Maxwell's equations, wave propagation, transmission lines, waveguides, antennas.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Networks and signals first: they feed control and communications.
2. Solve the official papers of past years (the organising IIT's site publishes them with answer keys).
