# ISRO Scientist/Engineer 'SC' (ICRB)

> **Evidence status: ✅ official.** Every fact below is taken from ISRO's official recruitment portal and Advt. No. ISRO:ICRB:03(EMC):2026 dated 27-08-2026, fetched on 2026-10-02.

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) recruits Scientist/Engineer 'SC' (Group 'A', Level 10 of the Pay Matrix) through its ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB). Each advertisement specifies the engineering disciplines open in that cycle. The current cycle, **ISRO:ICRB:03(EMC):2026 dated 27-08-2026**, is for **Electronics, Mechanical and Computer Science**.

**Electrical graduates:** The 2026 cycle does not have a separate Electrical Engineering post. Electrical & Electronics Engineering graduates are eligible to apply under the Electronics post (BE-001 / BE-001A). Always check the official advertisement for the specific cycle to confirm if your degree is accepted.

## Exam pattern

The written test / CBT consists of two parts:

| Part | Content | Questions | Marks | Duration | Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Part 'A' | Discipline-specific (core engineering) | 80 MCQ | 80 | 90 minutes | +1 for correct, -1/3 for wrong |
| Part 'B' | Aptitude / ability tests | 15 MCQ | 20 | 30 minutes | No negative marking |

Qualifying marks:
- **Unreserved:** 50% in written test, 50% in interview, 60% aggregate.
- **PwBD:** 40% in written test, 40% in interview, 50% aggregate.

The final selection is based on 50% weightage to written-test marks and 50% to interview marks.

## Syllabus

The written test is based on the standard undergraduate curriculum of the notified discipline. For the current cycle, this includes Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, and Computer Science. Candidates should refer to the [Electronics and communication core](../../modules/ece-core.md), [Mechanical engineering core](../../modules/me-core.md), or [Computer science core](../../modules/cs-core.md) modules as applicable. Part 'B' covers general aptitude; use the [Reasoning](../../modules/reasoning.md) module.
