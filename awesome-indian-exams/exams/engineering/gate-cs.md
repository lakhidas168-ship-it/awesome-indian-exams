# GATE Computer Science and Information Technology (CS)

GATE Computer Science and Information Technology (CS) is used for M.Tech/ME/PhD admission and is a recruitment filter for many PSUs.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | IISc Bengaluru and seven IITs for NCB-GATE (IIT Madras organises GATE 2027) |
| Exam dates | See the official site for the schedule |
| Who can apply | Students in the 3rd year or higher of a UG programme, and graduates; no age limit |
| Score validity | 3 years |

## Official sources

- GATE 2027 official site: <https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in>
- Question paper pattern: <https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/question_paper_pattern>

## Exam pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| General Aptitude (GA) | 10 | 15 |
| Subject Questions (CS) | 55 | 85 |
| **Total** | **65** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 3 hours.
- **Question types:** MCQ (Multiple Choice Question), MSQ (Multiple Select Question), NAT (Numerical Answer Type).
- **Negative marking:** Only for MCQs. 1/3 mark deducted for 1-mark MCQs, 2/3 mark for 2-mark MCQs. No negative marking for MSQ or NAT.
- **Mode:** Computer Based Test (CBT).

## Syllabus

The official syllabus for CS includes:

1. **Engineering Mathematics:** Discrete Mathematics, Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability and Statistics.
2. **Digital Logic.**
3. **Computer Organisation and Architecture.**
4. **Programming and Data Structures.**
5. **Algorithms.**
6. **Theory of Computation.**
7. **Compiler Design.**
8. **Operating System.**
9. **Databases.**
10. **Computer Networks.**

For detailed topics, see the [official syllabus PDF](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/CS_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Build a strong foundation in Discrete Mathematics, Data Structures, and Algorithms.
2. Study core subjects (OS, DBMS, Computer Networks, Architecture).
3. Practice with official past year papers available on the GATE 2027 website.
