## Exam pattern

For DTE the selection process is the **Written Test / Computer Based Test (CBT) alone**, carrying **100%
weightage** (there is no interview and no skill or trade test for this post). A Pre-Employment Medical
Examination follows.

**CBT:** 2 hours, objective with four options per question, **170 questions** in all, **1 mark** each, and a
penalty of **−¼** for every wrong or multiple answer. The test is held on a single day. PwBD candidates are
allowed compensatory time as per Government of India directives.

| Part | Questions | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Part-I — Technical Knowledge Test (TKT) | 120 | Discipline-specific questions (Electrical for DTE) |
| Part-II — Aptitude Test (AT) | 50 | English vocabulary, verbal comprehension, quantitative aptitude, reasoning ability, data sufficiency and interpretation, numerical ability, general awareness |
| **Total** | **170** | |

**Qualifying criteria** (applied category-wise): Unreserved/EWS — minimum **30%** in each part separately and
**40%** in aggregate; reserved vacancies — minimum **25%** in each part separately and **30%** in aggregate.

## Syllabus

The advertisement does not print a topic list for Part-I; it states that the 120 questions are "specific
questions from the respective discipline" — for DTE, diploma-level Electrical engineering. Prepare it from the
shared [electrical engineering core](../../modules/ee-core.md) and [engineering mathematics](../../modules/engineering-mathematics.md).

Part-II topics listed above map to the shared [quantitative aptitude](../../modules/quant-aptitude.md),
[reasoning](../../modules/reasoning.md), [English language](../../modules/english-language.md) and
[general awareness](../../modules/general-awareness.md) modules.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Cover the diploma-level Electrical core — circuits, electrical machines, power systems, measurements,
   power electronics and basic electronics — from the [EE core module](../../modules/ee-core.md). It is the
   whole of Part-I.
2. Add [engineering mathematics](../../modules/engineering-mathematics.md) at diploma depth.
3. Practise Part-II daily in small slots: quantitative aptitude and reasoning carry the bulk; keep a short
   general-awareness and English-vocabulary habit.
4. Take full 170-question, 2-hour timed mocks and respect the −¼ penalty. The qualifying cut-offs are low, so
   accuracy across both parts matters more than the number of attempts.
