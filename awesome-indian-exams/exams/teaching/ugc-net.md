# UGC NET

> **Evidence status: 🔴 unverified.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

UGC NET decides eligibility for Assistant Professor posts and the Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and PhD
admission in 80+ humanities, social science, commerce and other subjects. It is held twice a year.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency (NTA) for the University Grants Commission |
| Mode | Computer-based, one 3-hour sitting for both papers |
| Total | 150 questions, 300 marks, no negative marking |

## Official sources

- UGC NET official website (information bulletin): <https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in>

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Content | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Paper 1 | Teaching and research aptitude (common to all) | 50 | 100 |
| Paper 2 | The candidate's subject | 100 | 200 |

Two marks per question, no negative marking, 3 hours in total without a break between the papers.

## Syllabus

Paper 1: teaching aptitude, research aptitude, comprehension, communication, mathematical and logical
reasoning, data interpretation, ICT, people and environment, higher education system. Paper 2: the UGC subject
syllabus, published per subject.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Paper 1 is common and scoring: prepare it once, well.
2. No negative marking: answer everything.
