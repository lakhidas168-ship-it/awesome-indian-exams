# NVS teacher and staff recruitment

Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti recruits teachers and staff for the residential Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas
(653 JNVs; teachers stay on campus and share residential duties such as house mastership). The latest
cycle, Recruitment Notification 01/2025, is a joint KVS+NVS direct recruitment conducted by CBSE, with
online applications from 14 November 2025 (10:00) to 4 December 2025 (23:50). Selected candidates may be
posted anywhere in India.

## Exam pattern

**Tier 1 (qualifying, OMR, 2 hours, no sectional timing):** 100 questions for 300 marks — General Reasoning
20 (60 marks), Numeric Ability 20 (60), Basic Computer Literacy 20 (60), General Knowledge 20 (60), English
10 (30), one other Modern Indian Language 10 (30). Each question carries 3 marks; 1 mark is deducted per
wrong answer. MTS has its own Tier 1 (General Awareness 20/60, Computer Operation 40/120, English 20/60,
MIL 20/60). Tier 1 shortlists for Tier 2 in a 1:10 ratio of notified vacancies.

**Tier 2 (subject knowledge, 2.5 hours):** 70 questions for 100 marks — 60 objective (1 mark each, 0.25
deducted per wrong answer) + 10 descriptive (40 marks).

**After Tier 2:** teaching posts including PGT and TGT go to a 100-mark interview (final merit 85% Tier 2 +
15% interview; shortlist 1:3). JSA goes to a typing skill test (NVS: 30 w.p.m. English or 25 w.p.m. Hindi).
Lab Attendant and MTS are selected on Tier 2 marks only, with no interview or skill test.

## Syllabus

Tier 1: reasoning, numeric ability, basic computer literacy, general knowledge, English and one Modern
Indian Language (Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil,
Telugu or Urdu). Tier 2: the subject of the post (detailed post-wise syllabus is on the KVS/NVS websites
under the Recruitment heading, per the notification).

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Clear Tier 1 first: it is qualifying but competitive (1:10 shortlist) — practise reasoning, numeric
ability and computer literacy for speed at +3/−1 marking.
2. Prepare the Tier 2 subject from NCERT textbooks (Class 6–10 level for TGT, higher level for PGT).
3. TGT aspirants: qualify CTET Paper-II before the application closing date — it is mandatory.
4. Read the current notification for the post-wise syllabus and interview/skill-test rules before applying.
