# NVS teacher and staff recruitment

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti recruits teachers and staff for the residential Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas. The
latest cycle was held jointly with KVS, with the same tiered pattern.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (with KVS in the latest cycle) |
| Stages | Tier 1 (qualifying) → Tier 2 → interview/skill test, depending on the post |

## Official sources

- Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (official): <https://navodaya.gov.in>

## Exam pattern

Same tiered structure as KVS in the latest joint cycle: a qualifying Tier 1 objective test with negative
marking, then a post-specific Tier 2. The notice has the details.

## Syllabus

As for KVS: reasoning, general awareness, languages, pedagogy, and the subject taught.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Prepare KVS and NVS together: one preparation, two applications.
