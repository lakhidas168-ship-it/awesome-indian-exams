# CUET UG

> **Evidence status: 🔴 unverified.** The official notification for the current cycle could not be fetched.
> Always read the current notice before you apply or pay a fee.

CUET UG is the common entrance for undergraduate admission to central universities (such as Delhi University,
BHU and JNU) and many state and private universities that accept its scores.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency (NTA) |
| Mode | Computer-based |

## Official sources

- NTA official website: <https://nta.ac.in>

## Exam pattern

See the official notification for the current exam pattern, including number of questions, marks, time, and negative marking.

## Syllabus

Domain subjects typically follow the NCERT Class 12 syllabus. Language tests assess reading comprehension and
vocabulary. The General Test covers GK, current affairs, reasoning and basic maths. See the official notification for the definitive syllabus.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Check each target university's subject requirements before you choose papers.
2. Class 12 NCERT books are the base for the domain subjects.
