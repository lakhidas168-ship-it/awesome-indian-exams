CUET UG (Common University Entrance Test Undergraduate) is the national-level common entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to undergraduate programmes in central universities (such as Delhi University, Banaras Hindu University, JNU, etc.) and participating state, deemed, and private universities across India.

## Exam pattern

CUET UG 2026 is conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode. The test structure consists of three sections:

1. **Section IA & IB (Languages):** 13 Indian languages in Section IA and additional languages in Section IB. Candidates choose languages to test reading comprehension, vocabulary, and literary aptitude. 50 questions per test, out of which 40 are to be attempted.
2. **Section II (Domain-Specific Subjects):** 23 domain subjects mapped closely to the NCERT Class 11–12 syllabus. Candidates can choose up to a maximum number of subjects (typically up to 5 subjects including languages and General Test). 50 questions per test, all compulsory/as per subject format.
3. **Section III (General Aptitude Test):** General Knowledge, Current Affairs, General Mental Ability, Numerical Ability, Quantitative Reasoning (simple application of basic mathematical concepts: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, statistics), and Logical and Analytical Reasoning. 60 questions, out of which 50 are to be attempted.

**Marking Scheme:**
- **Correct answer:** 5 marks (+5)
- **Incorrect answer:** -1 mark (-1)
- **Unanswered / Unattempted / Marked for Review:** 0 marks (0)
- **Duration:** 60 minutes for each test paper.

## Syllabus

- **Domain Subjects:** Strictly aligned with the NCERT Class 11 and Class 12 syllabus across science, commerce, and humanities disciplines.
- **Languages:** Reading comprehension (factual, literary, narrative passages), literary aptitude, and vocabulary.
- **General Test:** General knowledge, current affairs, quantitative reasoning, numerical ability, logical and analytical reasoning.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Check the specific undergraduate program eligibility and subject requirements of your target universities (e.g., Delhi University, BHU) before selecting domain test papers.
2. Master NCERT Class 11 and 12 textbooks thoroughly for all chosen domain subjects.
3. Practice reading comprehension passages and vocabulary for language sections.
4. Solve quantitative reasoning and logical reasoning problems using standard free resources and mock tests.
5. Review previous years' question papers and mock tests on the NTA portal to build speed and accuracy under CBT conditions.
