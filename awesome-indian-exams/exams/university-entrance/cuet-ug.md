# CUET UG

> **Evidence status: 🔴 unverified.** The official CUET UG portal is currently unreachable or not providing direct access to the latest information bulletin. CUET UG (Common University Entrance Test Undergraduate) is the national-level common entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to undergraduate programmes in central universities and participating state, deemed, and private universities across India.

## Exam pattern

The exam pattern is subject to change as per the latest official notification. Historically, it consists of:

- **Section IA & IB (Languages):** Reading comprehension and literary aptitude.
- **Section II (Domain-Specific Subjects):** Subjects mapped to the NCERT Class 11–12 syllabus.
- **Section III (General Aptitude Test):** General knowledge, current affairs, quantitative reasoning, and logical reasoning.

*Note: Please refer to the official information bulletin on the NTA website for the exact number of questions, marking scheme, and duration for the current cycle.*

## Syllabus

The syllabus is generally based on the NCERT curriculum for Class 11 and 12 for domain subjects. For the General Test, it covers:
- General Knowledge & Current Affairs
- General Mental Ability
- Numerical Ability
- Quantitative Reasoning (Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Statistics)
- Logical and Analytical Reasoning

*Official syllabus documents are typically released as part of the Information Bulletin on the official portal.*
