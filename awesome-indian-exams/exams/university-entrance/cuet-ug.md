# CUET UG

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

CUET UG is the common entrance for undergraduate admission to central universities (such as Delhi University,
BHU and JNU) and many state and private universities that accept its scores.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency (NTA) |
| Mode | Computer-based |
| Subjects | Up to 5, including at least one language; 37 subjects offered in 2026 |

## Official sources

- CUET UG official website (information bulletin): <https://cuet.nta.nic.in>

## Exam pattern

Every test (language, domain subject or General Test) has **50 questions, all compulsory, in 60 minutes**.
Marking: **+5** correct, **−1** wrong.

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| Section I | Language tests (13 languages in 2026) |
| Section II | Domain subjects (23 in 2026), based on the Class 12 syllabus |
| Section III | General Test (general knowledge, current affairs, reasoning, numerical ability) |

Choose subjects to match the admission rules of the university programmes you want.

## Syllabus

Domain subjects follow the NCERT Class 12 syllabus. Language tests assess reading comprehension and
vocabulary. The General Test covers GK, current affairs, reasoning and basic maths.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Check each target university's subject requirements before you choose papers.
2. Class 12 NCERT books are the syllabus for the domain subjects.
