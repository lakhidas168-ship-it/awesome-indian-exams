# UCEED

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

UCEED is the entrance for B.Des at the IITs (Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Indore, Roorkee and others)
and some other institutes. IIT Bombay organises it.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | IIT Bombay |
| Total | 300 marks: Part A 200 + Part B 100 |

## Official sources

- UCEED official website: <https://www.uceed.iitb.ac.in>

## Exam pattern

**Part A** (computer-based, 2 hours, 200 marks): numerical answer, multiple select and multiple choice
questions (57 in 2026). **Part B** (pen and paper, 1 hour, 100 marks): one sketching question and one design aptitude
question. Negative marking applies to some Part A question types.

## Syllabus

Visualisation and spatial ability, observation and design sensitivity, environmental and social awareness,
analytical and logical reasoning, language, creativity, drawing.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Solve UCEED's official past papers, which are published with answer keys.
2. Sketch daily for Part B.
