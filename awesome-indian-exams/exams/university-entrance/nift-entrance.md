# NIFT entrance (B.Des, B.FTech)

NIFT's entrance admits students to design (B.Des, M.Des), fashion technology (B.FTech) and management
programmes at its campuses.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Institute of Fashion Technology |
| B.Des | GAT (computer-based) + CAT (paper-based) → situation test |
| B.FTech | GAT only |

## Official sources

- NIFT (official): <https://www.nift.ac.in>

## Exam pattern

**GAT** (General Ability Test): 100 objective questions in 120 minutes for B.Des, on English comprehension,
quantitative ability, analytical and logical reasoning, general knowledge and current affairs.
**CAT** (Creative Ability Test): 3 hours, drawing and design-thinking tasks. B.Des candidates who qualify take a
**situation test** (hands-on model making).

## Syllabus

GAT: aptitude modules. CAT and the situation test: creativity, observation, visualisation, material handling.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Sketch daily from observation. CAT and the situation test reward practice, not theory.
