# CUET PG

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

CUET PG is the common entrance for master's programmes at central universities and many
other universities.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency |
| Stages | Computer-based test → university-wise admission |

## Official sources

- CUET PG on the NTA exams portal: <https://exams.nta.nic.in/cuet-pg/>

## Exam pattern

Subject-specific computer-based papers, chosen to match each programme's requirements. The information bulletin has
the number of questions, time and marking.

## Syllabus

The subject's undergraduate syllabus, as listed per test paper in the bulletin.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Check each university's test-paper code before registering: the programme decides the paper.
