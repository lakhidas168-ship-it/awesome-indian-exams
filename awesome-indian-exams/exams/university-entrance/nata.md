# NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture)

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** The facts below were cross-checked against several non-official sources on
> 2026-09-27. Confirming them against the official notification is a hive task. Always read the current notice
> before you apply or pay a fee.

NATA is the aptitude test for B.Arch admission, accepted by colleges across India. It is held several times
in a season.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | Council of Architecture |
| Total | 200 marks, 3 hours |

## Official sources

- NATA official website: <https://www.nata.in>

## Exam pattern

**Part A:** Drawing and Composition Test, 90 minutes, 80 marks, three questions. **Part B:** computer-based
adaptive MCQs, 120 marks, on aptitude, reasoning, observation, architectural awareness and problem solving.

## Syllabus

Drawing, composition, visual perception, mathematics and general aptitude, architectural awareness.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Drawing practice every day for Part A.
2. NCERT Class 11–12 maths helps the aptitude part.
