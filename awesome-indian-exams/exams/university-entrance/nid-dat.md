# NID DAT

The Design Aptitude Test (DAT) is the entrance examination for admission to the Bachelor of Design (B.Des.) and Master of Design (M.Des.) programmes at the National Institute of Design (NID).

## At a glance

| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exam** | Design Aptitude Test (DAT) |
| **Stages** | DAT Prelims, DAT Mains |
| **Conducting Body** | National Institute of Design (NID) |
| **Frequency** | Annual |

## Official sources

- <https://admissions.nid.edu/>
- <https://admissions.nid.edu/NIDA2027/download/BDES_IntegratedPathway_AdmissionsHandbook2027_28.pdf>

## Exam pattern

The admission process consists of two stages:

1. **DAT Prelims**: A written test.
2. **DAT Mains**: A studio test and/or interview (for shortlisted candidates).

*Note: The specific number of questions, marks, and duration for each section are detailed in the official Admissions Handbook.*

## Syllabus

The syllabus for DAT covers various aspects of design aptitude, including:

- **DAT Prelims**: Focuses on design awareness, visual sensitivity, drawing skills, observation, and analytical ability.
- **DAT Mains**: Involves hands-on studio tests, portfolio review, and personal interviews to assess creativity, problem-solving, and communication skills.

For the complete and detailed syllabus, refer to the [official Admissions Handbook](https://admissions.nid.edu/NIDA2027/download/BDES_IntegratedPathway_AdmissionsHandbook2027_28.pdf).
