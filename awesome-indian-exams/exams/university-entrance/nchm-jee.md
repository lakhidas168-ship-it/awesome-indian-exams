# NCHM JEE (hotel management)

NCHM JEE is the entrance for the B.Sc. in Hospitality and Hotel Administration (HHA) at the Institutes of
Hotel Management (IHMs) affiliated to the National Council for Hotel Management and Catering Technology
(NCHMCT). It is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.
Admission is on the All India Rank in NCHM JEE, followed by a centralised web-based online counselling in
which seat and institute are allotted on a merit-cum-choice basis.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency |
| Programme | B.Sc. in Hospitality and Hotel Administration (HHA) at NCHMCT-affiliated IHMs |
| Mode | Computer Based Test (CBT) |
| Stages | Computer-based test → centralised web-based online counselling (merit-cum-choice) |
| Marks | 200 (five sections) |
| Duration | 3 hours (11:00 AM to 2:00 PM) |
| Age limit | No age bar |
| Eligibility | Pass in the 10+2 (Senior Secondary) system or its equivalent from a recognised board with English as one of the subjects; those appearing in the qualifying exam may apply provisionally |
| 2025 exam date | 27 April 2025 (Sunday) |

The dates above are from the NCHM JEE 2025 cycle (admission to academic year 2025-26). Dates for each new
cycle are announced by NTA in a fresh public notice and Information Bulletin.

## Exam pattern

A single Computer Based Test (CBT) of 200 marks, with five sections:

| Section | Marks |
|---|---|
| Numerical Ability and Analytical Aptitude | 30 |
| Reasoning and Logical Deduction | 30 |
| General Knowledge and Current Affairs | 30 |
| English Language | 60 |
| Aptitude for Service Sector | 50 |
| **Total** | **200** |

The NCHM JEE 2025 notice schedules the test for 27 April 2025, 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The brochure states
the section-wise marks above but does not give the number of questions or the marking scheme; check the
Information Bulletin for those.

## Syllabus

The five test sections define the syllabus: numerical ability and analytical aptitude, reasoning and
logical deduction, general knowledge and current affairs, English language, and aptitude for the service
sector. The Information Bulletin lists the detailed topics under each.

## How to prepare (free, in order)

1. Work through the shared aptitude modules below, which cover the numerical ability, reasoning, English
   and general knowledge sections.
2. Practise the English Language section most, since it carries the highest weight (60 of 200 marks).
3. For the Aptitude for Service Sector section, read about the hospitality and tourism sector and current
   affairs.
