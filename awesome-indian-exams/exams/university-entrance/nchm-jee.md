# NCHM JEE (hotel management)

> **Evidence status: ⚪ unverified.** This page gives the structure and the official links. Numbers that could not be
> cross-checked are left to the official notification. Verifying them is a hive task.

NCHM JEE is the entrance for the B.Sc. in Hospitality and Hotel Administration at the Institutes of Hotel
Management.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Conducted by | National Testing Agency |
| Stages | Computer-based test → counselling |

## Official sources

- NTA exams portal: <https://exams.nta.nic.in>

## Exam pattern

One computer-based test on numerical ability and analytical aptitude, reasoning and logical deduction, general
knowledge and current affairs, English, and aptitude for the service sector. The bulletin has questions and marking.

## Syllabus

Class 10–12 level aptitude modules and service-sector aptitude.
