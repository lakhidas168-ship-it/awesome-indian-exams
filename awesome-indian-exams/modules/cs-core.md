# Computer science core

Used by the GATE computer-science family papers (CS and DA) in this list. The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list.

## What it covers

Checked against the official GATE 2027 syllabus PDFs (links below), fetched 2026-09-28:

- **Foundations:** discrete mathematics, digital logic, computer organisation and architecture
- **Software:** programming (C for CS, Python for DA), data structures, algorithms
- **Systems and theory:** theory of computation, compiler design, operating systems, databases and SQL, computer networks

Official syllabi: [GATE 2027 CS syllabus PDF](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/CS_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf) (10 sections) · [GATE 2027 DA syllabus PDF](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/DA_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf) (7 sections)

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| GATE CS | Full depth: all 10 syllabus sections, including digital logic, organisation, theory of computation, compilers, OS and networks |
| GATE DA | Shared subset only: Python programming, data structures, algorithms, and databases/SQL (DA Sections 4–5); DA's probability, linear algebra, ML and AI live in other modules, and CS-only topics (digital logic, organisation, TOC, compilers, OS, networks) are not asked |

## How to practise

Implement each data structure once in the exam's language (C for CS, Python for DA); then solve official GATE CS/DA past papers topic by topic, timed.
