# Computer science core

Counts for **2 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Discrete mathematics, digital logic, computer organisation
- Programming, data structures, algorithms
- Theory of computation, compilers, operating systems, databases, networks

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Engineering jobs: GATE, ESE, JE, PSU, state AE/JE | [GATE Computer Science (CS)](../exams/engineering/gate-cs.md), [GATE Data Science and AI (DA)](../exams/engineering/gate-da.md) |
