# Teaching and research aptitude

This module *is* UGC NET Paper 1 (common to all subjects); its teaching-aptitude half also overlaps CTET, State TETs and KVS/NVS recruitment. The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list.

## What it covers

The ten units of the UGC NET Paper 1 syllabus (official syllabus: <https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in>), grouped:

- **Teaching aptitude:** nature and objectives of teaching, learner characteristics, teaching methods, teaching aids, evaluation systems.
- **Research aptitude:** meaning and types of research, research steps and methods, thesis and article writing, ICT in research, research ethics.
- **Comprehension and communication:** reading comprehension; nature, types and barriers of communication, classroom communication.
- **Mathematical and logical reasoning:** number series, codes, ratios; structure of arguments, Venn diagrams, fallacies.
- **Data interpretation:** tables, graphs and charts.
- **ICT, people and environment, higher education:** internet basics and digital initiatives; environment and sustainable development; institutions, governance and policies of higher education.

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| UGC NET | The full module: Paper 1, 50 questions for 100 marks, no negative marking |
| CTET, State TETs | Teaching-aptitude half only (pedagogy, learner characteristics, evaluation); no research component (syllabus via <https://ctet.nic.in>) |

## How to practise

Cover all ten units in rotation, not just favourites, then solve official UGC NET Paper 1 sets unit by unit. Paper 1 rewards breadth across every unit.
