# Legal reasoning

Counts for **3 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Applying a legal principle given in a passage to facts
- Basic concepts of contract, tort, criminal and constitutional law
- Legal current affairs and landmark judgments

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Law entrance | [AILET (NLU Delhi)](../exams/law/ailet.md), [CLAT](../exams/law/clat.md), [MAH CET Law](../exams/law/mh-cet-law.md) |

## Free resources

- India Code (official texts of Acts): <https://www.indiacode.nic.in>
- Press Information Bureau: <https://pib.gov.in>

## How to practise

Principle → facts → conclusion: practise applying only what the passage gives.
