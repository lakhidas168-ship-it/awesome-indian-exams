# Banking and financial awareness

Counts for **9 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- **Banking structure:** Types of banks (Public, Private, RRBs, Cooperative, Payment/Small Finance Banks), RBI functions, Monetary policy (Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR, SLR, MSF, Bank Rate).
- **Financial systems:** Payment and settlement systems (UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, AEPS), Financial inclusion schemes (PMJDY, PMMY), Basel norms, NPAs, and Credit rating agencies.
- **Financial markets:** Money market, Capital market, Government securities, and Inflation.
- **Current awareness:** Recent banking news, Union Budget, and Economic Survey.

## How to practise

Read the RBI's "Financial Education" section and monitor the PIB for updates on financial inclusion schemes and monetary policy announcements.
