# Banking and financial awareness

Counts for **9 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- RBI, monetary policy, repo/reverse repo, CRR/SLR
- Banking structure, types of banks, financial inclusion schemes
- Payment systems (UPI, NEFT, RTGS), NPAs, Basel norms
- Current banking and economy news

| Family | Exams |
