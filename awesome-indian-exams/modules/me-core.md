# Mechanical engineering core

Counts for **1 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Engineering mechanics, strength of materials, theory of machines, machine design
- Thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer
- Materials, manufacturing, industrial engineering

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Engineering jobs: GATE, ESE, JE, PSU, state AE/JE | [GATE Mechanical Engineering (ME)](../exams/engineering/gate-me.md) |
