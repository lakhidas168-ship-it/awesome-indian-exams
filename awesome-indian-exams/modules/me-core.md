# Mechanical engineering core

The shared mechanical syllabus for GATE ME and the mechanical papers of JE-level exams. The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) lists every exam that counts it.

## What it covers

- **Applied mechanics and design:** engineering mechanics, strength of materials, theory of machines, vibrations, machine design
- **Fluid mechanics and thermal sciences:** fluid mechanics and machinery, thermodynamics, heat transfer, IC engines, refrigeration and air-conditioning, turbomachinery, power-plant basics
- **Materials, manufacturing and industrial engineering:** engineering materials, casting, forming, joining, machining, metrology, CAD/CAM and automation, production planning, inventory, operations research

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| GATE ME | Full depth in all three blocks, including vibrations, additive manufacturing and operations research; MCQ, MSQ and NAT |
| SSC JE / RRB JE (Mechanical) | Same blocks at diploma level, with more weight on thermal, fluid machinery and production processes |

## How to practise

Thermodynamics and strength of materials first: several other subjects build on them. Then solve official previous papers topic by topic.
