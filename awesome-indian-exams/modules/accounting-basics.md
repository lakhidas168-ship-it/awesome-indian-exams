# Accounting and business basics

Counts for **3 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- **Accounting:** principles and framework, journal, ledger, trial balance, bank reconciliation, inventories,
  depreciation, bills of exchange, final accounts of sole proprietors, partnerships and companies; cost
  accounting basics (CMA).
- **Business laws and communication:** Indian Contract Act, Sale of Goods Act, Partnership and LLP Acts,
  Companies Act basics; business correspondence and reporting (CA) / business communication (CMA).
- **Business mathematics and statistics:** ratio, equations, interest and time value of money, permutations,
  series; data description, central tendency and dispersion, probability, correlation and regression, index numbers.
- **Business economics and management:** demand and supply, production and cost, market structures, national
  income, money and banking, Indian economy; management basics (CMA).

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| CA Foundation (ICAI) | Deepest: every block here is a full 100-mark paper (Accounting; Business Laws; Quantitative Aptitude; Business Economics) |
| CMA Foundation (ICMAI) | Deepest: four papers covering the same blocks plus cost accounting and management, all objective |
| UPSC EPFO | Shallow: only the general-accounting-principles slice of the recruitment-test syllabus |
