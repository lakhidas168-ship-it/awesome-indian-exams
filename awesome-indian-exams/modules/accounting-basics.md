# Accounting and business basics

Counts for **3 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Accounting principles, journal, ledger, trial balance, final accounts
- Business laws and business economics basics
- Business mathematics and statistics
