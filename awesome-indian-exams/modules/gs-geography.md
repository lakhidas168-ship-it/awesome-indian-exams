# Geography of India and the world

Counts for **22 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

Based on the official syllabi of exams like [UPSC CSE](https://www.upsc.gov.in) and [TNPSC Group 1](https://www.tnpsc.gov.in):

- **Physical Geography:** Landforms, climate, oceans, and atmospheric phenomena.
- **Indian Geography:** Physical features, river systems, climate, soils, natural vegetation, agriculture, minerals, energy resources, and industries.
- **World Geography:** Major physical divisions, climatic regions, and mapping.
- **Human and Economic Geography:** Population, settlements, economic activities, and resources.
- **Disasters:** Types, management, and mitigation.

## Depth by exam family

| Family | Depth |
|---|---|
| UPSC | High (Conceptual, analytical, and mapping) |
| State PSC | Moderate to High (Factual and conceptual) |

## How to practise

Study with an atlas open; mark every place you read about. Solve previous year questions from the official exam body websites.
