# Geography of India and the world

Counts for **22 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Physical geography: landforms, climate, oceans
- Indian geography: rivers, soils, agriculture, minerals, industries
- World geography and maps
- Human and economic geography, disasters
