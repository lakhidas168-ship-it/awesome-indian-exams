# Biology (NCERT Class 11–12)

Counts for **6 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Diversity of life, structural organisation, cell biology
- Plant and human physiology
- Reproduction, genetics and evolution
- Biology in human welfare, biotechnology, ecology

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
