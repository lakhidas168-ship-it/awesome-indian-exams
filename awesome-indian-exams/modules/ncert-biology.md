# Biology (NCERT Class 11–12)

Counts for **6 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- **Diversity and organisation of life:** living world, plant and animal kingdoms, morphology and anatomy of
  flowering plants, structural organisation in animals, cell structure and function, biomolecules, cell cycle
- **Plant and human physiology:** photosynthesis, respiration, transport and nutrition in plants; digestion,
  breathing, circulation, excretion and osmoregulation, movement, control and coordination in humans
- **Reproduction, genetics and evolution:** reproduction in flowering plants and humans, reproductive health,
  principles of inheritance, molecular basis of inheritance, evolution
- **Human welfare, biotechnology, ecology:** health and disease, microbes in human welfare, biotechnology
  principles, processes and applications, organisms and populations, ecosystem, biodiversity and conservation

## How to practise

For NEET, read NCERT line by line more than once: questions follow its wording, and half the paper is Biology.
For CUET, focus on Class 12 units (reproduction, genetics, biotechnology, ecology). Practise labelled diagrams
and one-page flowcharts per chapter; state CET biology papers reward the same NCERT base.
