# Chemistry (NCERT Class 11–12)

Counts for **13 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Physical: mole concept, atomic structure, thermodynamics, equilibrium, kinetics, electrochemistry, solutions
- Inorganic: periodicity, bonding, s/p/d/f-block, coordination compounds
- Organic: GOC, hydrocarbons, functional groups, biomolecules, polymers

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| JEE Main, JEE Advanced and state CETs | Full Class 11–12 syllabus; numerical-heavy physical chemistry plus NCERT-line inorganic |
| NEET UG | Factual, NCERT-worded questions across physical, organic and inorganic (45 questions, 180 marks) |
| CUET UG Chemistry domain | Class 12 NCERT only; 50 questions in 60 minutes |
