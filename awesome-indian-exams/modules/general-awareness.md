# General awareness and current affairs

Tested almost everywhere: SSC, railways, banking, defence, state PSCs, CLAT, MBA exams, even ESE Paper I. See
the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md).

## What it covers

- **Static GK:** Indian history, geography, polity, economy, general science, art and culture.
- **Current affairs:** government schemes, appointments, awards, sports, summits, reports and indices, science
  and defence news, usually for the 6–12 months before the exam.
- **Sectoral awareness** for some families: banking and financial awareness (banking), legal current affairs
  (CLAT), energy and environment (ESE Paper I).

## Free, official sources

- NCERT Class 6–10 social science and science textbooks for static GK: <https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php>
- Press Information Bureau (official releases on schemes and decisions): <https://pib.gov.in>
- Union Budget and Economic Survey: <https://www.indiabudget.gov.in>
- Yojana and Kurukshetra magazines: <https://www.publicationsdivision.nic.in>

## How to practise

Fix NCERT as the base for static GK. For current affairs, keep **one** monthly notebook built from PIB and the
Budget/Economic Survey, instead of many compilations. Revise it monthly and before every mock test.
