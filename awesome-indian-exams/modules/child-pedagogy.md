# Child development and pedagogy

Counts for **5 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Child development: stages, theories (Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg)
- Learning and pedagogy, inclusive education
- Assessment, CCE, motivation
- Language and subject pedagogy

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
