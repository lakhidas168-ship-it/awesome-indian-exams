# Civil engineering core

Counts for **1 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Structural analysis, solid mechanics, RCC and steel design
- Geotechnical, water resources and environmental engineering
- Transportation engineering and geomatics

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Engineering jobs: GATE, ESE, JE, PSU, state AE/JE | [GATE Civil Engineering (CE)](../exams/engineering/gate-ce.md) |

## Free resources

- NPTEL (free IIT/IISc courses; search the subject): <https://nptel.ac.in/courses>
- MIT OpenCourseWare: <https://ocw.mit.edu>

## How to practise

Solid mechanics and structural analysis carry the most marks.
