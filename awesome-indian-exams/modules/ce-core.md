# Civil engineering core

Counts for **1 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

Checked against two official syllabi: the GATE 2027 CE syllabus (8 sections) and SSC JE 2026
Paper-II Part-A (Civil and Structural). They cover the same core; SSC JE sets it at diploma
depth and adds estimating, costing and valuation.

- **Structures:** engineering mechanics, solid mechanics, structural analysis, concrete and steel design
- **Geotechnical:** soil mechanics, foundations, earth pressure, slope stability, ground improvement
- **Water and environment:** fluid mechanics, hydraulics, hydrology, irrigation, water and wastewater
  treatment, air pollution, solid waste
- **Transport and survey:** highways, pavements, traffic engineering, surveying and geomatics
- **Construction management:** materials and mix design, PERT/CPM, estimation and costing

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| GATE CE | Deepest: numerical MCQ/MSQ/NAT problems across all 8 syllabus sections |
| SSC JE (Civil) | Same core at diploma depth, all MCQ; adds estimating, costing and valuation |
| ESE Civil, PSU, state AE/JE | Same core again; ESE Mains is descriptive, so confirm depth from each notification |

## How to practise

Solid mechanics and structural analysis first: they carry the most marks and feed RCC and steel
design. Then solve official GATE CE and SSC JE Civil papers topic by topic, tracking accuracy
and seconds per question.
