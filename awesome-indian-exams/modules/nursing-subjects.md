# Nursing subjects

Counts for exams in the medical family (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

The syllabus is based on the B.Sc. Nursing curriculum as prescribed by the Indian Nursing Council (INC).
- **Fundamentals of Nursing:** Anatomy, Physiology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Nutrition, Psychology, Sociology.
- **Core Nursing:** Medical-Surgical Nursing, Paediatric Nursing, Obstetric and Gynaecological Nursing, Community Health Nursing, Mental Health Nursing.
- **Advanced Nursing:** Nursing Research, Statistics, Management of Nursing Services and Education, Pharmacology.

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| Nursing Recruitment (e.g., AIIMS NORCET) | Full breadth of B.Sc. Nursing curriculum, focus on clinical application and case-scenario based questions. |

## How to practise

1. Revise the B.Sc. Nursing curriculum subject by subject.
2. Focus on clinical application and case-scenario based questions as per the NORCET pattern.
3. Use previous years' papers for timed practice, paying attention to the section-locked format.
