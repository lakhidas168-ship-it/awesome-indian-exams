# Nursing subjects

Counts for **1 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Fundamentals of nursing, anatomy and physiology
- Medical-surgical, paediatric, obstetric and community health nursing
- Mental health nursing, pharmacology, nursing research

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Medical: NEET and medical PG | [AIIMS NORCET (Nursing Officer)](../exams/medical/aiims-norcet.md) |
