# English language and comprehension

Part of SSC, banking, CDS, NDA, AFCAT, CLAT, CAT (VARC), CUET, BITSAT and most state exams. See the
[overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md).

## What it covers

- **Grammar:** tenses, subject–verb agreement, articles, prepositions, modifiers, parallelism.
- **Vocabulary:** synonyms and antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms and phrases.
- **Comprehension:** reading passages, para jumbles, sentence completion, cloze test.
- **Descriptive writing** (banking mains, some PSC and MBA exams): see the separate descriptive-writing module.

## Depth by exam family

| Family | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| SSC, defence | Grammar rules and vocabulary |
| Banking | Reading comprehension, cloze test, error detection, under time pressure |
| CLAT, CAT | Long passages and inference-heavy reading |

## Free resources

- NCERT English textbooks (grammar and reading at school level): <https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php>
- Khan Academy grammar course: <https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/grammar>
- Press Information Bureau releases (free, formal English for daily reading): <https://pib.gov.in>

## How to practise

Read one long formal article every day and summarise it in three lines. Keep a personal list of the words and
grammar rules you got wrong in practice papers, and revise that list weekly.
