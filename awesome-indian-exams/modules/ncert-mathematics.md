# Mathematics (NCERT Class 11–12)

Counts for **17 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

Based on the NCERT Class 11 and 12 Mathematics curriculum, which forms the core syllabus for exams like [JEE Main](https://jeemain.nta.ac.in/) and [UPSC NDA](https://www.upsc.gov.in/):

- **Algebra**: Sets, Relations and Functions, Complex Numbers, Quadratic Equations, Sequences and Series, Binomial Theorem, Matrices, Determinants.
- **Calculus**: Limits, Continuity and Differentiability, Derivatives, Integrals, Applications of Integrals, Differential Equations.
- **Coordinate Geometry, Vectors and 3D Geometry**: Straight Lines, Conic Sections, Introduction to Three-Dimensional Geometry, Vector Algebra.
- **Probability and Statistics**: Statistics, Probability.
- **Trigonometry**: Trigonometric Functions, Inverse Trigonometric Functions.
