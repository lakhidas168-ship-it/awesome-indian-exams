# Mathematics (NCERT Class 11–12)

Counts for **17 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Algebra: sets, functions, complex numbers, sequences, binomial, matrices, determinants
- Calculus: limits, derivatives, integrals, differential equations
- Coordinate geometry, vectors and 3D geometry
- Probability, statistics, trigonometry
