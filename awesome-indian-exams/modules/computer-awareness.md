# Computer awareness

Used by SSC, banking and RRB JE exams. The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list.

## What it covers

- **Computer basics:** organisation of a computer, CPU, input/output devices, memory and its
  organisation, storage and backup devices, ports, Windows Explorer, keyboard shortcuts.
- **Software:** Windows operating system (plus Unix/Linux basics for RRB JE); MS Office basics
  (Word, Excel, PowerPoint).
- **Internet and email:** web browsing and searching, downloading and uploading, managing an
  email account, websites and browsers, e-banking.
- **Networking and cyber-security basics:** networking devices and protocols; threats (hacking,
  virus, worms, Trojan) and preventive measures; data representation (bits and bytes).

## Depth by exam family

| Family | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| SSC (CGL, CHSL) | Dedicated 15-minute Computer Knowledge Test in Tier-II; mandatory but qualifying, −1 per wrong answer |
| Banking | Computer aptitude inside mains — a standalone Computer Knowledge section or merged with reasoning, under sectional timing; IBPS PO/MT XVI mains has no separate computer section, so check that year's notification |
| RRB JE | 10-mark "Basics of Computers and Applications" section in CBT-2, slightly wider (Unix/Linux, data representation) |

## How to practise

Learn by doing: perform each Windows, Office and email operation once on a real computer, then drill
official previous computer sections under time. Re-revise shortcuts, ports and threat names weekly.
