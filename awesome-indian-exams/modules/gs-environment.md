# Environment and ecology

Counts for **4 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Ecology and ecosystems, biodiversity, conservation
- Climate change, pollution, environmental laws and bodies
- Protected areas, international conventions
- Environmental impact assessment and sustainability (also ESE Paper I)

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Engineering jobs: GATE, ESE, JE, PSU, state AE/JE | [UPSC Engineering Services (ESE), Electrical](../exams/engineering/upsc-ese-ee.md) |
| UPSC | [UPSC Civil Services (CSE)](../exams/upsc/upsc-cse.md), [UPSC Indian Forest Service (IFoS)](../exams/upsc/upsc-ifos.md) |
| State PSC civil services | [UPPSC PCS (Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services)](../exams/state-psc/uppsc-pcs.md) |
