# Quantitative aptitude

Used by more exams in this list than almost any other module: SSC, railways, banking, defence, CAT and CLAT,
state exams and the GATE general aptitude section. The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list.

## What it covers

- **Arithmetic:** number system, simplification, percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, simple and compound
  interest, profit and loss, time and work, time–speed–distance, mixtures.
- **Algebra:** linear and quadratic equations, identities, inequalities.
- **Geometry and mensuration:** lines, triangles, circles, areas and volumes.
- **Trigonometry** (SSC and defence), **data interpretation** (banking, CAT).

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| SSC, railways | Wide syllabus including geometry and trigonometry, at speed |
| Banking | Arithmetic, number series and heavy data interpretation, under sectional time limits |
| CAT and other MBA exams | Fewer questions but deeper reasoning, especially algebra and arithmetic |
| GATE, ESE Paper I | A small numerical-ability section |

## Free resources

- NCERT Mathematics, Class 6–10 (the base of all arithmetic and geometry): <https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php>
- NIOS Secondary Mathematics (self-study material): <https://www.nios.ac.in>
- Khan Academy arithmetic and pre-algebra: <https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic>

## How to practise

Learn each chapter from NCERT, then solve **official previous papers** of your target exam topic by topic. Track
two numbers per topic: accuracy and seconds per question. Speed comes after accuracy.
