# Electronics and communication core

Used by the engineering-jobs family (GATE EC, GATE IN and the PSU/AE/JE papers that recruit on them). The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list.

## What it covers

Checked against the official GATE 2027 syllabi for EC and IN (links below):

- **Networks, signals and systems:** node/mesh analysis, network theorems, two-port networks, transient and phasor response; LTI systems, convolution, Fourier/Laplace/z-transforms, DFT/FFT, sampling and FIR/IIR basics.
- **Electronic devices and analog circuits:** semiconductors, p-n junction, BJT, MOSFET, optoelectronic devices; diode circuits, amplifiers, op-amps, filters, oscillators.
- **Digital circuits:** Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential logic, ADCs/DACs, semiconductor memories, computer organisation basics (IN adds microcontrollers, data acquisition and IoT basics).
- **Control systems:** transfer functions, block diagrams, signal flow graphs, transient/steady-state response, Routh-Hurwitz/Nyquist stability, Bode and root-locus plots, PID and compensators, state-space.
- **Communications:** random processes and noise, analog modulation (AM/FM), digital modulation (ASK/PSK/FSK/QAM), information theory, error control (IN adds optical sources, fiber-optic sensing and 4-20 mA loops).
- **Electromagnetics:** Maxwell's equations, plane waves, transmission lines, Smith chart, waveguides, antennas (IN covers the electrostatics/magnetostatics subset under Electricity and Magnetism).

| GATE IN | Overlapping subset: signals, control, analog/digital, communications and electromagnetics basics; plus measurements, sensors and optical instrumentation beyond this module |
