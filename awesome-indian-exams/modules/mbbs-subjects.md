# MBBS subjects (pre-clinical, para-clinical, clinical)

One shared syllabus for the medical PG and medical-officer family: FMGE, NEET PG, INI-CET and UPSC CMS.
The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list. Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- **Pre-clinical:** anatomy, physiology, biochemistry.
- **Para-clinical:** pathology, microbiology, pharmacology, forensic medicine, community medicine.
- **Clinical:** general medicine, general surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, paediatrics, ophthalmology,
  otorhinolaryngology (ENT), psychiatry, dermatology and STD, anaesthesiology, orthopaedics, radiodiagnosis,
  radiotherapy.
- The FMGE blueprint (the most detailed official split) weights clinical subjects at two-thirds of the paper,
  with medicine, surgery, obstetrics-gynaecology and community medicine alone nearly half the marks.

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| FMGE (screening test) | Full breadth across all 19 subjects; pass mark (150 of 300), no negative marking |
| NEET PG, INI-CET (PG entrance) | Same MBBS base with clinical-application and image-based questions, timed blocks, negative marking |
| UPSC CMS (medical officer) | Clinical papers only: medicine with paediatrics; surgery with gynaecology-obstetrics and preventive medicine |

## How to practise

1. Learn each subject in NMC curriculum order: basic sciences first, then the matching clinical subject.
2. Solve official previous papers of your target exam in sealed timed blocks (FMGE: 50 questions in 50 minutes
   with no going back; NEET PG: 36 questions in 42 minutes).
3. Track accuracy and marks per hour per subject; give extra rounds to medicine, surgery, obstetrics-gynaecology
   and community medicine — nearly half the FMGE paper.
