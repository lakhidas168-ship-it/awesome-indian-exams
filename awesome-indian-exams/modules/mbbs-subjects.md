# MBBS subjects (pre-clinical, para-clinical, clinical)

Counts for **4 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Pre-clinical: anatomy, physiology, biochemistry
- Para-clinical: pathology, pharmacology, microbiology, forensic medicine, community medicine
- Clinical: medicine, surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, paediatrics, and allied subjects

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Medical: NEET and medical PG | [FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination)](../exams/medical/fmge.md), [INI-CET](../exams/medical/ini-cet.md), [NEET PG](../exams/medical/neet-pg.md) |
| UPSC | [UPSC Combined Medical Services (CMS)](../exams/upsc/upsc-cms.md) |

## Free resources

- National Medical Commission (curriculum): <https://www.nmc.org.in>

## How to practise

Integrate: learn each topic from basic science to clinical application.
