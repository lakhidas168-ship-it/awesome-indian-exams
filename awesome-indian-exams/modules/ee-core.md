# Electrical engineering core

Used by the engineering-jobs family (GATE, ESE, JE, PSU, state AE/JE). The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list.

## What it covers

- **Circuits and fields:** network theorems, transient and steady-state response, two-port networks, three-phase circuits; electrostatics, magnetostatics, magnetic circuits.
- **Signals, control, measurements:** continuous and discrete signals, Fourier/Laplace/z-transforms; feedback, Bode/Nyquist/root-locus, PID, state space; bridges, instrument transformers, error analysis.
- **Machines, power, electronics:** transformers, DC/induction/synchronous machines; generation, transmission, load flow, faults, protection, stability; diodes, op-amps, logic, ADC/DAC; thyristor/MOSFET/IGBT, converters, rectifiers, inverters.
- **Beyond GATE (ESE/JE only):** electrical materials and computer fundamentals (ESE); estimation and costing, utilisation of electrical energy (SSC JE, RRB JE).

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| GATE EE | Full depth on all nine sections above, numerical plus theory |
| GATE IN | Overlapping subset: circuits, machines basics, signals, control, analog/digital, measurements |
| ESE Electrical | GATE core plus materials, computer fundamentals and microprocessors; descriptive mains answers |
| SSC JE, RRB JE, state AE/JE, PSU | Diploma/BTech core at speed; JE adds estimation and utilisation |
