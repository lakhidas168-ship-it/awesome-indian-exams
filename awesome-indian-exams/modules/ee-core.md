# Electrical engineering core

Counts for **7 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Circuits, EM fields, signals and systems
- Machines, power systems, control systems
- Measurements, analog and digital electronics, power electronics
