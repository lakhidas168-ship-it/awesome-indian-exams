# CSAT: comprehension, reasoning, basic numeracy

Counts for **11 exams** in this list — UPSC civil services and nine state PSC civil services (see the
[overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

The UPSC-pattern prelims aptitude paper (Paper II / CSAT), which the state PSCs below copy:

- Reading comprehension
- Interpersonal skills, including communication skills
- Logical reasoning and analytical ability
- Decision making and problem solving
- General mental ability
- Basic numeracy (Class 10 level) and data interpretation (charts, graphs, tables, data sufficiency)

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| UPSC CSE and IFoS | Prelims Paper II: qualifying (33% to stay in the race); it screens, it adds nothing to merit |
| UPPSC PCS | Prelims General Studies II (CSAT): qualifying at 33% |
| Other state PSCs (APSC, CGPSC, HPSC, KPSC, MPPSC, MPSC, OPSC, UKPSC) | A qualifying aptitude/CSAT paper on the same UPSC pattern; paper number, marks and qualifying rule vary — confirm in each year's notification |

## How to practise

CSAT is qualifying but eliminates many: solve one timed official previous paper a week and track accuracy
per topic (comprehension, reasoning, numeracy). Speed comes after accuracy.
