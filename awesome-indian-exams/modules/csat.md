# CSAT: comprehension, reasoning, basic numeracy

Counts for **11 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Reading comprehension
- Logical reasoning and analytical ability
- Decision making and problem solving
- Basic numeracy (Class 10 level) and data interpretation
