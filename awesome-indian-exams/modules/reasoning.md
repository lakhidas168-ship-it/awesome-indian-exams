# Logical and analytical reasoning

Tested by SSC, railways, banking, CLAT, CAT (as logical reasoning), defence and most state exams. See the
[overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md).

## What it covers

- **Verbal reasoning:** analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense,
  order and ranking, syllogism, statement–conclusion, inequalities.
- **Analytical puzzles:** seating arrangement, scheduling, grouping (banking and CAT weight these most).
- **Non-verbal reasoning:** figure series, mirror and water images, paper folding, embedded figures (SSC, railways,
  defence).
- **Critical reasoning:** assumptions, arguments and inferences (CLAT, CAT, UPSC CSAT).

## Depth by exam family

| Family | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| SSC, railways, defence | Many short, rule-based questions, including non-verbal ones |
| Banking | Long puzzles and seating arrangements under a time limit |
| CLAT, CAT, CSAT | Passage-based critical reasoning and logic sets |

## Free resources

Reasoning is learned by solving, not reading. The best free material is each exam's **official previous
papers and answer keys**, linked from the exam pages in this list.

## How to practise

Sort previous questions by type and solve 20 of one type in a row until the pattern is automatic. Then mix types
under time. Review every wrong answer and write the rule you missed.
