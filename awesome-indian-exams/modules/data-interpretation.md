# Data interpretation

Counts for **8 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Tables, bar, line and pie charts, caselets
- Percentages, ratios and averages applied to data
- Data sufficiency
- Set-based and missing-data puzzles (banking mains, CAT)

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| Banking, insurance and regulators | [IBPS PO](../exams/banking/ibps-po.md), [SBI PO](../exams/banking/sbi-po.md) |
| Teaching and research: TET, NET, KVS | [CSIR NET (JRF / Assistant Professor)](../exams/teaching/csir-net.md), [UGC NET](../exams/teaching/ugc-net.md) |
| Management entrance: CAT, XAT and others | [CAT](../exams/management/cat.md), [MAT](../exams/management/mat.md), [SNAP](../exams/management/snap.md), [XAT](../exams/management/xat.md) |

## Free resources

- NCERT textbooks: <https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php> (Class 8–10 maths, statistics chapters)

## How to practise

Do one full DI set daily under a timer; convert fractions and percentages mentally.
