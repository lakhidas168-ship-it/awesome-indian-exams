# General science (NCERT Class 6–10 level)

A large scoring block in railway exams (RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician), and part of SSC, defence, CTET, NDA
and state exams. See the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md).

## What it covers

- **Physics:** motion, force, work and energy, light, sound, electricity, magnetism.
- **Chemistry:** matter, atoms and molecules, acids, bases and salts, metals and non-metals, carbon compounds.
- **Biology:** cells, tissues, human body systems, nutrition, diseases, heredity, environment.

## Free resources

- NCERT Science, Class 6–10 (the syllabus most of these exams draw from): <https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php>
- NIOS Secondary Science and Technology: <https://www.nios.ac.in>
- Khan Academy science courses: <https://www.khanacademy.org/science>

## How to practise

Read the NCERT chapters in order from Class 6 to 10 and make one-page notes per chapter. Most questions test a
definition, a unit, an example or a simple numerical, and all of these come straight from those chapters.
