# Hindi language

State PSCs, SSC, banking, teaching and school-entrance exams all test Hindi: prepare it once.
The [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md) has the full list.

## What it covers

- Grammar: sandhi, samas, karak, vachan, ling, kaal, vachya
- Vocabulary: paryayvachi, vilom, anekarthi, muhavare and lokoktiyan
- Comprehension (apathit gadyansh) and error detection and correction
- Translation and essay writing (for JHT and state PSC mains)
- Language pedagogy: how children acquire language, teaching skills, remedial teaching (for CTET and other teaching exams)

## Depth by exam family

| Family | What the exam rewards |
|---|---|
| SSC JHT | Degree-level Hindi: correct use of words, phrases and idioms, translation both ways, essays |
| State PSC | School-level vyakaran and comprehension; essay and translation in mains |
| SSC GD, IBPS RRB | Fast objective Hindi: vocabulary, grammar, comprehension |
| Teaching (CTET, KVS, NVS, DSSSB) | Comprehension and grammar plus language pedagogy (half of each CTET language section) |
| JNVST (Class 6 and 9) | Basic Hindi at primary level |

## How to practise

Read one Hindi editorial a day and keep a notebook of muhavare and your grammar mistakes. For teaching
exams, practise pedagogy questions from official CTET papers; for JHT, translate one passage each way daily.
