# Hindi language

Counts for **16 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Grammar: sandhi, samas, karak, vachan, ling, kaal, vachya
- Vocabulary: paryayvachi, vilom, anekarthi, muhavare and lokoktiyan
- Comprehension (apathit gadyansh) and error correction
- Translation and essay writing (for JHT and state PSC mains)
