# Science and technology (current)

Counts for **2 exams** in this list (see the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md)). Prepare it once.

## What it covers

- Space, defence and nuclear technology
- Biotechnology, health and diseases
- IT, AI, emerging technologies
- Science in everyday life and current developments

## Where it counts

| Family | Exams |
|---|---|
| UPSC | [UPSC Civil Services (CSE)](../exams/upsc/upsc-cse.md), [UPSC Indian Forest Service (IFoS)](../exams/upsc/upsc-ifos.md) |

## Free resources

- ISRO: <https://www.isro.gov.in>
- Department of Science and Technology: <https://dst.gov.in>
- Press Information Bureau: <https://pib.gov.in>

## How to practise

Follow PIB science releases; note the 'what, why, so what' of each.
