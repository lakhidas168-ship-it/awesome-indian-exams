# Free official learning platforms

Everything here is run by the Government of India or a public institution, and costs nothing. Together they cover
school textbooks, degree-level courses, practice tests, libraries and current affairs, so a full preparation is
possible without paying for coaching.

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** Every link points to the platform's own official website. The links were
> located by web search on 2026-09-27, and the list's daily link check re-opens them. If one breaks, open an issue
> with the new official link.

| Platform | Run by | What you get free | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [NCERT textbooks](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php) | NCERT, Ministry of Education | Every NCERT textbook, Classes 1–12, Hindi and English | The base of JEE, NEET, CUET, UPSC and state PSC general studies |
| [NIOS course material](https://nios.ac.in/online-course-material.aspx) | National Institute of Open Schooling | Secondary and senior-secondary study material in several languages | A second, simpler explanation of school topics |
| [DIKSHA](https://diksha.gov.in) | Ministry of Education | Textbook-linked lessons, explainer videos and practice for school subjects | School-level revision in your state's language |
| [NTA Abhyas](https://www.nta.ac.in/abhyas) | National Testing Agency | Mock tests in the real NTA interface, in Hindi and English | JEE Main and NEET practice |
| [SWAYAM](https://swayam.gov.in) | Ministry of Education | University courses (Class 9 to postgraduate) with lectures, reading and assessments | Degree-level subjects: engineering, commerce, humanities, law |
| [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in/courses) | IITs and IISc | Complete IIT lecture courses with assignments | GATE, ESE, JE and PSU technical subjects |
| [e-PG Pathshala](https://epgp.inflibnet.ac.in) | UGC (INFLIBNET) | Postgraduate e-content in about 70 subjects | UGC NET subject papers, optional subjects |
| [National Digital Library of India](https://ndl.iitkgp.ac.in) | IIT Kharagpur for the Ministry of Education | Search across crores of books, lectures and question papers | Finding an official or open copy of a book or paper |
| [Press Information Bureau](https://pib.gov.in) | Government of India | Official releases from every ministry | Current affairs for UPSC, state PSC, SSC and banking |

## Free coaching

SATHEE (IIT Kanpur), IIT-PAL lectures, the SWAYAM Prabha TV channels and the fee-paying schemes for eligible
students are on [free coaching](free-coaching.md).

## Official previous papers and cut-offs

See [previous-year papers](previous-papers.md): UPSC, SSC, GATE, NTA and state commission archives, plus the
official JoSAA and MCC counselling data.

## A free plan that uses them

1. **Build the base from textbooks.** Pick your exam in the [overlap map](overlap-map.md) and read the shared
   modules it needs. For each module, start with NCERT and NIOS.
2. **Learn the higher-level subjects from courses.** Technical and degree-level subjects come from NPTEL, SWAYAM
   and e-PG Pathshala, following the order on your exam's page.
3. **Practise on the real interface.** Use NTA Abhyas for JEE Main and NEET, and the official previous papers for
   every exam.
4. **Follow current affairs from the source.** Read PIB every day for exams with a general-awareness section.
