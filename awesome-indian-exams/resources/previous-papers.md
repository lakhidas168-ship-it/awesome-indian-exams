# Previous-year papers: the official archives

Previous-year questions (PYQs) are the best free guide to what an exam really asks. Many bodies publish their own
papers, and those are the only copies you can trust. "Memory-based" papers on coaching sites are reconstructions:
useful at most for practice, never as the source of a fact.

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** Every link below points to the body's own website. The links were located
> by web search on 2026-09-27, because the official sites could not be opened from the authoring machine. The
> list's daily link check re-opens every link. Archives move when a new cycle starts; if one breaks, open an issue
> with the new official link.

## How to use previous papers well

1. **Take one full paper cold, timed, before studying a subject.** Your score tells you where to start.
2. **Tag every question with a module and a topic.** Use the shared modules in the
   [overlap map](overlap-map.md): after a few papers you see which topics come back every year, and those come
   first in your plan.
3. **Keep a mistake log**, not just a score. Re-solve every wrong question after a week, then after a month.
4. **Solve the latest papers last, as full mocks**, in the real time limit.
5. **Prefer the official answer key.** Where the body publishes a final answer key, it overrides every coaching
   solution.
