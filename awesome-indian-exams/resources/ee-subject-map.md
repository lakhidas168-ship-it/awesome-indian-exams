## Reading the map

- **GATE → ESE:** add Electrical Materials, Computer Fundamentals, microprocessor basics, ESE Paper I (general
  studies and engineering aptitude), and descriptive answer writing.
- **GATE → CSE EE optional:** add communication (analog and digital) and microprocessors.
- **GATE → SSC JE / RRB JE:** add Estimation and Costing and Utilisation. Keep the core at speed-and-accuracy
  depth, because the negative marking is heavy.
- **Every exam:** official previous papers first. Each exam page links its official source.

> **Evidence status:** built from the syllabus summaries on each exam page, which are themselves still
