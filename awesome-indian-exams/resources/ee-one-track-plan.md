# One-track plan for the electrical track

This document outlines the subject overlap between GATE EE, ESE Paper-II, SSC JE, RRB JE, and PSU papers. The goal is to build a core foundation that serves all these exams, with specific additions for each.

## Extra Topics by Exam

| Exam | Extra Topics Added |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GATE EE** | General Aptitude (15 marks), Engineering Mathematics (13 marks). |
| **ESE Paper-I** | Current issues, Engineering Aptitude, Standards & Quality, Energy & Environment, Project Management, Material Science, ICT, Ethics. |
| **SSC JE** | Estimation & Costing, Utilisation of Electrical Energy, Reasoning (50 marks), General Awareness (50 marks). |
| **RRB JE** | General Science (30 marks), Reasoning (25 marks), General Awareness (15 marks), Basics of Computers, Environment & Pollution Control. |

*Note: This plan is built from the official syllabi linked on each exam page.*
