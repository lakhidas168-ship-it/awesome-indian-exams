# UPSC CSE study plan: 6-month and 12-month tracks (free)

> Built from the shared modules mapped to `upsc-cse` in `registry/exams.toml` and the
> [UPSC CSE exam page](../../exams/upsc/upsc-cse.md). The official notification on
> <https://upsc.gov.in> is the authority for the syllabus and pattern: read it first, then follow
> the week order below. Everything recommended here is free and official; no paid or coaching
> material is used.

## How to use this plan

1. **Week 1, before studying:** solve one previous GS paper and one CSAT paper cold and timed, from the
   official archive above. Tag every question with its module; your weakest modules get the earliest
   revision slots.
2. **One module at a time, NCERT first:** read the NCERT base for the module, then revise it after a few
   days, a few weeks and a few months (see [study methods](../study-methods.md)).
3. **Current affairs daily:** 30–45 minutes of the Press Information Bureau (<https://pib.gov.in>),
   plus the Economic Survey and Budget documents at <https://www.indiabudget.gov.in> and Yojana /
   Kurukshetra at <https://www.publicationsdivision.nic.in> for the economy block.
4. **Write from the middle third:** one essay a week and two GS answers a week, using previous-paper
   questions; compare against the official syllabus, not against coaching model answers.
5. **Last month: mocks and previous papers only**, no new topics.

## 12-month track (52 weeks)

| Weeks | Module(s) | Work (free sources) |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| 1–2 | Diagnostic | Previous GS + CSAT papers cold, timed; read the notification syllabus on <https://upsc.gov.in> |
| 3–10 | [Polity](../../modules/gs-polity.md) | NCERT civics/politics base, then Constitution topics chapter by chapter |
| 11–18 | [History](../../modules/gs-history.md) | Ancient → medieval → modern → culture; timelines revised weekly |
| 19–25 | [Geography](../../modules/gs-geography.md) | Physical + India + map work; 10 minutes of map practice daily |
| 26–32 | [Economy](../../modules/gs-economy.md) | NCERT economics + Economic Survey/Budget documents + Yojana/Kurukshetra |
| 33–38 | [Environment](../../modules/gs-environment.md) + [Science-tech](../../modules/gs-science-tech.md) | Environment static base, then science current affairs from PIB |
| 39–42 | [CSAT](../../modules/csat.md) | Comprehension + reasoning + Class 6–10 numeracy to the 33% qualifying bar |
| 43–48 | Weakest-module second pass + [Descriptive writing](../../modules/descriptive-writing.md) | Re-solve tagged PYQs; weekly essay + answers on previous-paper questions |
| 49–52 | Mocks only | Full previous papers timed; mistake log re-solved after a week |
| 1–52 daily | [General awareness](../../modules/general-awareness.md) | PIB daily; monthly self-recap from your own notes |

## 6-month track (26 weeks)

For repeaters or those with a GS base. Same order, half the time; drop the second pass and write
from week 8.

| Weeks | Module(s) | Work (free sources) |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | Diagnostic | Previous GS + CSAT papers cold, timed; read the notification syllabus |
| 2–5 | [Polity](../../modules/gs-polity.md) | Constitution-first fast pass from NCERT base |
| 6–9 | [History](../../modules/gs-history.md) | Modern-heavy pass; ancient/medieval as revision notes |
| 10–12 | [Geography](../../modules/gs-geography.md) | India + physical concepts + map work |
| 13–15 | [Economy](../../modules/gs-economy.md) | Survey/Budget + current-linked concepts |
| 16–18 | [Environment](../../modules/gs-environment.md) + [Science-tech](../../modules/gs-science-tech.md) | Static base + PIB science current affairs |
| 19–20 | [CSAT](../../modules/csat.md) + [Descriptive writing](../../modules/descriptive-writing.md) | Qualifying-level drills; start weekly essay + answers |
| 21–22 | Weakest modules only | Tagged PYQs you got wrong, re-solved |
| 23–26 | Mocks only | Full previous papers timed; no new topics |
| 1–26 daily | [General awareness](../../modules/general-awareness.md) | PIB daily; skip older backlog, follow current + last 12 months |

## Weekly rhythm (both tracks)

- Six study days, one lighter day for revision and rest.
- Every week: one timed sectional or full mock; log every mistake as concept gap, silly mistake,
  time pressure or guess, and fix each kind differently (see [study methods](../study-methods.md)).
- Every month: check remaining weeks against the official exam date and move unfinished modules forward.
