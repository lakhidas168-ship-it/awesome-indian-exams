## Modules in study order

Biology leads (half the paper: 90 of 180 questions); physics and chemistry run alongside every week.

| # | Module | Scope in this plan |
|---|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | [Biology (NCERT Class 11–12)](../../modules/ncert-biology.md) | NCERT line by line: diversity → physiology → reproduction/genetics → biotech/ecology |
| 2 | [Chemistry (NCERT Class 11–12)](../../modules/ncert-chemistry.md) | Physical (problem practice) → organic → inorganic (NCERT-line reading) |
| 3 | [Physics (NCERT Class 11–12)](../../modules/ncert-physics.md) | Mechanics → electromagnetism → optics → modern; derive every formula once |

## How to use this plan

1. **Week 1, before studying:** solve one previous paper cold and timed, from the official NTA archive.
   Tag every question with its module and chapter; the weakest chapters get the earliest revision slots.
2. **Biology is NCERT wording:** read the NCERT lines, not summaries; questions follow the textbook
   closely. Physics and chemistry come from NCERT first, then the NCERT exemplar problems.
3. **With −1 per wrong answer, skipping is a skill:** practise leaving doubtful questions unanswered in
   every mock and check whether your score rises.
4. **NTA Abhyas for practice volume:** previous papers plus Abhyas mocks are the studying, not just a
   final check.
5. **Last sixth: full mocks and previous papers only**, no new chapters.

## 12-month track (52 weeks)

| Weeks | Module(s) | Work (free sources) |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| 1–2 | Diagnostic | One previous paper cold, timed; read the bulletin chapter list on <https://neet.nta.nic.in> |
| 3–16 | [Biology](../../modules/ncert-biology.md) + [Chemistry](../../modules/ncert-chemistry.md) + [Physics](../../modules/ncert-physics.md), Class 11 | Biology: diversity, cell, plant and human physiology. Chemistry: physical (mole, thermo, equilibrium, kinetics). Physics: mechanics, thermo, waves. NCERT exercises per chapter |
| 17–30 | [Biology](../../modules/ncert-biology.md) + [Chemistry](../../modules/ncert-chemistry.md) + [Physics](../../modules/ncert-physics.md), Class 12 | Biology: reproduction, genetics, evolution, biotech, ecology. Chemistry: organic + coordination/d/f-block. Physics: electrostatics → magnetism → optics → modern. Exemplar per chapter |
| 31–36 | All three modules, gap-filling | Remaining inorganic chemistry + revision of human welfare/ecology; NIOS material (<https://www.nios.ac.in>) for any chapter that still resists |
| 37–42 | All three modules, second pass | Exemplar + weak-chapter re-solves from tagged PYQs; biology NCERT re-read line by line |
| 43–48 | All three modules, timed | NTA Abhyas mocks + previous papers, analysed question by question |
| 49–52 | Mocks only | Full 3-hour papers; revise from your mistake log, no new chapters |

## 6-month track (26 weeks)

Same biology-led structure at double pace; for students already in Class 12 or repeating.

| Weeks | Module(s) | Work (free sources) |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | Diagnostic | One previous paper cold, timed; read the bulletin chapter list |
| 2–8 | [Biology](../../modules/ncert-biology.md) + [Chemistry](../../modules/ncert-chemistry.md) + [Physics](../../modules/ncert-physics.md), Class 11 | Physiology-heavy biology + physical chemistry + mechanics; NCERT exercises |
| 9–15 | [Biology](../../modules/ncert-biology.md) + [Chemistry](../../modules/ncert-chemistry.md) + [Physics](../../modules/ncert-physics.md), Class 12 | Genetics-led biology + organic + EM/optics; exemplar per chapter |
| 16–18 | All three modules, gap-filling | Inorganic + modern physics + ecology/biotech revision |
| 19–21 | All three modules, second pass | Weak chapters from tagged PYQs; biology NCERT re-read |
| 22–26 | Mocks only | NTA Abhyas + previous papers timed; mistake-log revision, no new chapters |

## Weekly rhythm (both tracks)

- Six study days, one lighter day for revision and rest; biology every day, physics and chemistry alongside.
- Every week: one timed sectional or full mock; log every mistake with its reason and re-solve it after
  a week, then after a month (see [study methods](../study-methods.md)).
- Every month: check remaining weeks against the official exam date and move unfinished chapters forward.
