# Free coaching: government programmes and schemes

Coaching does not have to cost lakhs. The Government of India, public institutions and several states run free
coaching programmes. Some are open to everyone; others pay a coaching fee and a stipend for eligible students.

> **Evidence status: 🟡 secondary.** Every link points to the programme's official website. The links and figures
> were located by web search on 2026-09-27 and cross-checked across several sources. Eligibility, amounts and dates
> change every year: the scheme's current guidelines on its official site always win.

## Open to every student

| Programme | Run by | What it gives | Exams |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SATHEE](https://sathee.iitk.ac.in) | Ministry of Education with IIT Kanpur | Free online coaching: video lectures, practice, mock tests, notes in many Indian languages | JEE, NEET, CUET, SSC, CLAT, ICAR, RRB |
| [IIT-PAL](https://iitpal.iitd.ac.in) | IIT Delhi for the Ministry of Education | Class 11–12 physics, chemistry, maths and biology lectures by IIT faculty | JEE, NEET |
| [SWAYAM Prabha](https://www.swayamprabha.gov.in) | Ministry of Education | Free 24×7 educational TV channels on DD FreeDish, also online | School to degree level; IIT-PAL for JEE |
| [NTA Abhyas](https://www.nta.ac.in/abhyas) | National Testing Agency | Free mock tests in the real NTA interface | JEE Main, NEET |

## For eligible students: fees and stipend paid

| Scheme | Run by | Who can apply | What it pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Free Coaching Scheme for SC and OBC students](https://coaching.dosje.gov.in) ([scheme page](https://socialjustice.gov.in/schemes/30)) | Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment | SC and OBC students with family income up to ₹8 lakh a year, and PM CARES Children Scheme beneficiaries | The coaching fee at an empanelled institute (reported up to ₹1.2 lakh), plus a monthly stipend (reported ₹4,000, up to nine months) |
| [Free coaching for De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes](https://dwbdnc.dosje.gov.in/content/free-coaching) | Development and Welfare Board for DNTs | DNT students (see the scheme's guidelines) | Coaching support, per the guidelines |
| [Jamia Residential Coaching Academy](https://jmi.ac.in/ACADEMICS/RCA) (apply at [admission.jmi.ac.in](https://admission.jmi.ac.in)) | Jamia Millia Islamia, a central university | Minority, SC, ST and women graduates eligible for the Civil Services Examination, through an entrance test | Free civil services coaching with a hostel, for about 100 selected students a year |
| [Jai Bhim Mukhyamantri Pratibha Vikas Yojana](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/jbmpvy) | Government of Delhi | SC, ST, OBC and EWS students of Delhi, as the scheme defines | Coaching fees for civil services, SSC, railway, bank, engineering, medical and other exams, plus a stipend |
| [Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/cmacs) ([Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Rajasthan](https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in/)) | Government of Rajasthan | Rajasthan-domiciled SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS and minority students with family income up to ₹8 lakh a year, selected on merit | Free coaching at an empanelled institute for UPSC, RPSC, SSC, banking, railway, REET, police, engineering, medical, CLAT, CA/CS/CMA and other exams; accommodation assistance up to ₹40,000 a year for students studying away from home |

## Find your own state's scheme

Most states run their own free coaching for civil services, police, teacher and state PSC exams. Search
[myScheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in), the government's scheme finder, for "coaching" and filter by your state.
Scholarships for your degree are on the [National Scholarship Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in).

Never pay an agent to "get you into" a free scheme: every scheme above takes applications only on its official
portal.
