# Open-source projects by and for aspirants

Other people have built useful free tools and datasets for Indian exams. This page links to them instead of
copying them, so their authors keep the credit and every project stays under its own license.

> **Community projects, not official sources.** They are listed from their own descriptions (found on
> 2026-09-27) and have not been reviewed by the hive yet. Check each project's license before reusing its
> content. Never treat a community answer as more reliable than the official answer key. Projects that host
> coaching institutes' paid material are not listed.

## Previous-year questions and solutions

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [GATE Overflow](https://gateoverflow.in) | Community questions and answers for every GATE CSE paper since 1987, with discussion. Free PDFs are released on [GitHub](https://github.com/GATEOverflow/GO-PDFs) |
| [JEE Main PYQ database](https://github.com/HostServer001/jee_mains_pyqs_data_base) | A tool to browse, filter and cluster 14,000+ JEE Main previous-year questions |
| [Online exam questions](https://github.com/Samkarya/online-exam-questions) | An open JSON question bank with a schema, Markdown and LaTeX, for practice apps |

## Datasets (mainly for building AI tutors and research)

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [JEEBench](https://github.com/dair-iitd/jeebench) | JEE Advanced 2016–2023 physics, chemistry and maths questions, from IIT Delhi researchers (EMNLP 2023) |
| [MedMCQA](https://github.com/medmcqa/medmcqa) | 194,000 multiple-choice questions from medical entrance exams |

## Counselling and college choice

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [JEE dashboard](https://github.com/nisarg73/jee-dashboard) | Past opening and closing ranks for IITs and NITs, to plan choice filling. Check the numbers against the official [JoSAA archive](https://josaa.admissions.nic.in/applicant/seatmatrix/openingclosingrankarchieve.aspx) |

## Study planners and trackers

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Competitive Exam Tracker](https://github.com/Sanjaykumars1722/Competitive-Exam-Tracker) | Exam countdowns, syllabus checklists, Pomodoro timer and mock-test analytics |
| [Pariksha Sathi](https://github.com/SnakeEye-sudo/pariksha-sathi) | A syllabus-aware study planner with streaks |

## Notes vaults and preparation plans

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [UPSC vault (vijaycham)](https://github.com/vijaycham/UPSC) | An Obsidian vault linking subject notes to 31 years of UPSC previous-year questions |
| [UPSC CSE vault (GauravSharmaCode)](https://github.com/GauravSharmaCode/upsc-cse) | An interlinked Obsidian + Markdown + Git vault for long-term revision |
| [UPSC prep hub](https://github.com/maticcretic-commits/upsc-prep) | Pattern, syllabus, book list, NCERT road map and a 12-month plan |
| [GATE 2026 resources (CS and DA)](https://github.com/Shreya-Shindee/gate-2026-prep-resources) | Free GATE CS and DA resources with checked links |

**Tip:** this list itself works as an Obsidian vault too. Download the repository and open the
`awesome-indian-exams` folder in Obsidian: every link between exams, modules and resources keeps working.

Know a free, open project that should be here? Open an issue with its link and license.
