## 1. NMDC commissions ₹5,427-crore integrated iron-ore project in Bastar (Steel)

- NMDC Ltd commissioned a 2 MTPA (million tonnes per annum) pellet plant at Nagarnar, Chhattisgarh — part of a
  ₹5,427-crore integrated project that links its Bailadila iron-ore operations with its Nagarnar steel plant.
- The project has three parts: a new iron-ore processing plant at Bacheli, a 15 MTPA slurry pipeline, and the
  2 MTPA pellet plant at Nagarnar. A 135-km slurry pipeline carries processed concentrate from Bacheli to
  Nagarnar, replacing road transport.
- The pellet plant uses straight-grate induration technology and lets NMDC convert fines and slimes from its
  Bailadila mines into concentrate and then pellets — a new value-added product in its portfolio.
- CMD Amitabh Mukherjee monitored the first production from NMDC headquarters (bad weather blocked the site
  visit). The project fits NMDC's long-term plan to raise iron-ore output capacity to 100 million tonnes; the
  pipeline passes through 61 villages of Bastar and Dantewada districts.
- Why it matters: minerals, PSUs and infrastructure are recurring UPSC Prelims / SSC GK / banking-awareness
  topics (iron ore, Bailadila, slurry pipelines, value addition).
- Release: Ministry of Steel, posted 27 Sep 2026, 11:48 PM (Release ID 2315706) —
  <https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2315706>

## 3. Exercise Tarang Shakti-2026 begins at Jodhpur (Defence)

- The Indian Air Force began the operational phase of Exercise Tarang Shakti-2026 at Air Force Station Jodhpur,
  after the inaugural ceremony on 26 September. The multilateral air exercise runs from 26 September to
  12 October 2026 on the theme "Strength in Partnership" (collective strength through interoperability).
- Seven friendly countries take part: Australia, USA, UAE, France, Germany, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh; more
  countries join as observers. Foreign aircraft include the F-35 Lightning-2, Rafale, F-16, A400M, MRTT,
  C-130, C-130J and KC-30A.
- This is the second edition (the first was held in 2024). Side events include seminars, industry visits, a
  marathon, heritage tours and yoga sessions.
- Why it matters: military exercises (name, participants, venue, edition) are asked in every UPSC, SSC, CDS,
  AFCAT and banking-awareness paper.
- Release: Ministry of Defence, posted 27 Sep 2026, 4:45 PM (Release ID 2315627) —
  <https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2315627>

## 4. INS Sahyadri and INS Kulish complete ADMM-Plus maritime activity at Subic Bay (Defence)

- Frontline stealth frigate INS Sahyadri and missile corvette INS Kulish completed the Experts' Working Group
  on Maritime Security Joint Cooperation Activity (EWG-MS JCA) at Subic Bay, Philippines, under the ASEAN
  Defence Ministers' Meeting-Plus (ADMM-Plus) framework.
- The activity began with the opening ceremony at Naval Operating Base Subic on 21 September 2026 (hosted by
  the Philippine Navy, co-chaired by Japan) and closed on 25 September 2026. The sea phase covered search and
  rescue, hydrographic survey, casualty/medical evacuation, deck landing and assistance to disabled vessels.
- The release frames the participation as part of the Act East policy and the MAHASAGAR vision (Mutual and
  Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions).
- Why it matters: ship names and classes, straits/bays, groupings (ADMM-Plus, ASEAN) and doctrines (Act East,
  MAHASAGAR) are high-yield for defence and IR questions.
- Release: Ministry of Defence (Navy), posted 27 Sep 2026, 5:52 PM (Release ID 2315663) —
  <https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2315663>

## 5. Livestock Insurance Portal to launch on 28 September (Animal Husbandry and Dairying)

- The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD) launches its integrated "Livestock Insurance Portal"
  in New Delhi on 28 September 2026. It will be launched by Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh alias Lalan
  Singh (Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, and Panchayati Raj).
- The portal is a single digital platform for policy issuance, claim settlement, monitoring and reporting under
  DAHD's livestock insurance scheme, with real-time monitoring and data-driven management.
- It will work in Hindi and English plus 11 regional languages, aimed at transparent, technology-based risk
  protection for livestock farmers.
- Why it matters: government schemes and portals (ministry, launch date, language coverage) appear in SSC,
