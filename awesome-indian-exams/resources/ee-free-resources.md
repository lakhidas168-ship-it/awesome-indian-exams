# Free resources for EE exam preparation

Only free, legal sources, linked to the publisher. No reposted PDFs or Telegram dumps: they are often pirated,
outdated or wrong.

## Full courses

- **NPTEL** (IIT/IISc video courses, free to watch; certification exams are paid and optional).
  Browse the Electrical Engineering discipline: <https://nptel.ac.in/courses>
- **SWAYAM-NPTEL** (the same courses with deadlines and assignments): <https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in>
- **MIT OpenCourseWare** (full lecture notes, problem sets with solutions):
  - Circuits and Electronics (6.002): <https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/>
  - Signals and Systems (6.003): <https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-003-signals-and-systems-fall-2011/>
  - Electromagnetics and Applications (6.013): <https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/>
  - Electric Machines (6.685): <https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-685-electric-machines-fall-2013/>
  - Power Electronics (6.334): <https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-334-power-electronics-spring-2007/>
  - Linear Algebra (18.06): <https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/>
- **Khan Academy, Electrical Engineering** (fundamentals, good for SSC/RRB JE level):
  <https://www.khanacademy.org/science/electrical-engineering>
- **All About Circuits textbook** (free online textbook, DC to semiconductors):
  <https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/>
- **Paul's Online Math Notes** (calculus, differential equations, linear algebra):
  <https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu>

## Practice by simulation

Seeing a circuit behave is often faster than re-reading a derivation.

- **Falstad circuit simulator** (runs in the browser, animated current flow): <https://www.falstad.com/circuit/>
- **ngspice** (open-source SPICE): <https://ngspice.sourceforge.io>
- **GNU Octave** (free MATLAB-compatible, for control and signals practice): <https://octave.org>
- **Scilab** (free numerical computing with Xcos block diagrams): <https://www.scilab.org>
