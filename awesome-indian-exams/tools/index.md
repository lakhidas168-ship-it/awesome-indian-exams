# Free study tools

Everything here is free, needs no login and shows no ads. Your progress is saved only in your own browser, so
nothing about you leaves your device, and every tool works in the [offline copy](https://lakhidas168-ship-it.github.io/awesome-indian-exams/download/awesome-indian-exams-offline.zip)
too.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Study planner](study-planner.md) | Pick your exam: every shared module it needs, a week-by-week plan to your exam date, a checklist, a focus timer and a daily streak |
| [Flashcards](flashcards.md) | Spaced-repetition review that brings each card back just before you would forget it |
| [Marks calculator](score-calculator.md) | Your score under any negative-marking scheme, and whether guessing pays |
| [AI study prompts](ai-study-prompts.md) | Copy-paste prompts that turn any free AI chat into a tutor, an answer evaluator or a flashcard maker, grounded in official sources |
