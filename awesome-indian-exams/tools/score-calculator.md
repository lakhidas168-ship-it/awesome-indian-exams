# Marks calculator

Enter how many questions you got right and wrong to see your score under the exam's marking scheme. The presets
are the common schemes as published in recent notifications. Always check your own exam's current notification,
because schemes change and some papers mix question types.

!!! note "Runs on the website"
    This tool runs in your browser on the [website](https://lakhidas168-ship-it.github.io/awesome-indian-exams/tools/score-calculator/) or in the
    [offline copy](https://lakhidas168-ship-it.github.io/awesome-indian-exams/download/awesome-indian-exams-offline.zip). On GitHub you only see this text.

**Using it for strategy:** after a mock, compare the marks you lost to wrong answers with the marks you left
unattempted. If negative marking costs you more than a few questions' worth, attempt fewer, surer questions next
time.
