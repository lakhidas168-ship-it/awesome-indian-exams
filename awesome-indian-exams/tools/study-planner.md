# Study planner

Choose your exam and set the exam date from the official notification. The planner lists every shared module the
exam needs, each linked to its free resources, and spreads the modules you have not finished over the weeks left.
It keeps the last part of your time for full mocks and previous papers. Tick a module off when you can solve its
previous-year questions.

!!! note "Runs on the website"
    This tool runs in your browser on the [website](https://lakhidas168-ship-it.github.io/awesome-indian-exams/tools/study-planner/) or in the
    [offline copy](https://lakhidas168-ship-it.github.io/awesome-indian-exams/download/awesome-indian-exams-offline.zip). On GitHub you only see this text.

**Why modules?** Most exams share most of their syllabus, so a module you finish counts for every exam that uses
it. See the [overlap map](../resources/overlap-map.md).
